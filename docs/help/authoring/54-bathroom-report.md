*Trapped in the Internet* keeps score of Bathroom Mode all night, and the
awards ceremony reads it out.

## What is counted

Each program counts, in its own variables:

- `bath_trips`: trips (🚻 pressed while not already away).
- `bath_s`: story seconds in Bathroom Mode, from 🚻 to *Back from the
  bathroom*, or to the 15-minute timeout.
- `bath_presses`: real switches, on and off. A repeat press while away
  counts nothing, and neither does the timeout.
- `bath_open`: 1 while a trip is open, so a report counts it up to now.

## The report (G13)

**G13 · 🚻 The Bathroom Report** is on the cue sheet in act G, after G12.
NEXT never suggests it: press it yourself between any two awards, from
G1 until G11. It has no winner to pick. The host reads:

- the minutes the whole party spent in Disk Cleanup;
- who spent the most and the least (least only among those who went, and
  only when two or more went; a tie names everyone);
- who switched Bathroom Mode on and off the most;
- how many never went.

The TV and the walls show the same numbers. Press it again to read the
newest ones. Under ✍ (your own award lines) Trabolta's template lines are
skipped, as they are for an award.

## Trust for never going

When the ceremony starts (G1), every program that never used Bathroom
Mode loses 5 trust, so read the recap for Most and Least Trusted after
G1. It is not announced until G13, when each of them also gets a line
from Trabolta in their own thread. A first trip after G1 gives the 5
back.

Run's recap lists *Minutes in Bathroom Mode*, *Bathroom Mode switches*
and *Never went to the bathroom (−5 trust)*.
