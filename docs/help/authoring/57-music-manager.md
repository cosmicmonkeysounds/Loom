The **Music manager** is the night's music in one place. Open it in
**Run → House → 🎵 Music** (a server project with its rehearsal running, like
the rest of [the house desk](47-house-desk.md)). A host's phone has the same
thing on the 🎵 Music tab of its **Desk** window, the tab Task Manager's phone opens
on first. Only directors and hosts can use it. The faders stay in
[the Mixer](51-house-mixer.md).

## The strip

Across the top: is the sound router listening, and where the music is coming
from: **Playing from TIDAL**, **TIDAL is paused**, **Playing files**, or what is
wrong. *TIDAL isn't answering* means the app was not started with `tidal.ps1` on
the Desktop, and each act plays its fallback folder.

## Now playing

- **Scene**: pick one to play it by hand. A hand is held: the story's `cue
  music_*` lines are ignored until you press **Follow the story**, or until the
  story's next live music cue.
- **⏮ ⏸ ⏭**: the app's own buttons, shown only while a bed is on TIDAL. A file
  bed, like the ceremony, can skip but has no pause and no previous. A pause
  holds until you press ▶ or the scene changes.
- Each **bed** (a playlist playing on some speakers): the rooms it plays in, the
  track and how far in, and **⏭ Skip**.
- **■ Stop music** stops the soundtrack, held like a hand scene. So does
  ■ Stop house sounds: Trabolta and the costume can't bring it back.
- An empty playlist shows as a warning. *No soundtrack reported yet* means the
  router has no `soundtrack.yaml`, or hasn't sent its first report.

## Play now

Pick something no scene names. It plays where the scene's music plays, so the
Fan Closet's dryer runs on, and it is held by hand like a scene.

- **The show's playlists**, from `soundtrack.yaml`.
- **Your TIDAL playlists**: everything in the app's sidebar, read every 30 s.
- **Paste a TIDAL playlist link**: a share link from anywhere. An album's or a
  track's link is not a playlist and won't play.

There is no search for a single song. A playlist from outside the show has no
fallback folder, so if the app dies it goes silent.

## Level

The **Music** fader, the same one as *Music* under Everywhere in the Mixer.

## Trabolta at the stereo

Trabolta can change the music, like the lights, but only for a program he has
grown **chummy** with. Each program's `chummy` number rises by one for each fact
accepted and each piece of lore given to him, and by one a reply when he thinks
they are good company. It falls when they are rude. At `Night.dj_bar` (3) he will
do it for them, up to four times a night, from the malware to the end of the Y2K
hour. Move the bar in World if the night needs it.

He can skip a song, stop the music for 30 s, or put on the calm, uneasy or dread
playlist for three minutes. He never reaches the rest of the library.

The card shows who he would do it for and what he did last.

- While his music plays the page says *Trabolta has the music*. It goes back by
  itself. **Take it back** ends it now.
- A live music cue from the story ends it early.
- While you hold the music by hand, he can't touch it.
- **🔒 Keep him off the music** stops him until you let him back, and hands back
  whatever he had on. It is also on the cue sheet, under Anytime.
