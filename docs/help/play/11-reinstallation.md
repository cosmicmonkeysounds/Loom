Some events have a **simulation**: a virtual copy of the house you're
standing in. In *Trapped in the Internet* it is how a corrupted program
gets out of the Internet — prove you can follow instructions, and you are
**reinstalled**. During the night only a marked or corrupted program may
play it; **after the night is over it is a party favour** for everyone.

## Being sent

First you are **marked** for reinstallation — a host saw something
(named on the Bingo card, a dare, a refused truth, an answer you
weren't sure of). A mark switches nothing off: your phone tells you
quietly and you carry on ([Your pass](your-pass.md)). But the Antivirus
are hunting you. When one finds you and scans your pass, your screen
says *⚠ VIRUS DETECTED · CORRUPTION DETECTED*; when they press 🛡, it says
*⛔ QUARANTINED · Sent to the Internet* with **Go to The Internet** and
**Show my pass**, and they walk you through the Tube. The Internet is the
**tent in the backyard, at the end of the tunnel**. Once you are in it, the
**Installation Wizard** messages you with what happens next: why you are
there, the headset or the tablet, Bathroom Mode, and where the exit is
(it is clearly marked; it is for safety). Look for the Installation
Wizard's thread in your app. Click Next.

## Getting in

In the tent there is a tablet. Hold your **pass** (tap **🎟 Pass** on
your app's taskbar — see [Your pass](your-pass.md)) up to its camera. It offers two
ways to play:

- **Play here** — the simulation runs on the tablet in your hands.
- **Send to a headset** — a VR headset (VR-1, VR-2 …) picks you up. Put
  it on; you're already in.

No camera, or it won't read your pass? Whoever runs the tent can find you
by name, or tap you in the list of programs in the Internet. A program
that is marked but not in the Internet yet has its own list on the
tablet (*Marked for reinstallation*), and the tablet reads **Marked
program detected · STATUS: MARKED — marked for reinstallation**: **Play
here** starts your reinstallation (and sends you to the Internet) all the
same.

During the night only a marked (or corrupted) program gets in. Scan a pass
that nobody marked and the tablet reads **Program not marked · STATUS:
healthy — not marked for reinstallation** (*until the night is over, only a
marked program may play*) and has no way to play; a headset's lobby never
lists an unmarked program either. A program a director held without a mark
reads **Program held by a director**.

## A party favour

Once the night is over (any ending), the simulation is open to **every
program**: the tablet reads **🎉 Party favour unlocked · STATUS: the night
is over — anyone may play** (*Party favour for Winamp*), the headset lobby
tags everyone *[party favour]*, nobody is captured by starting, and Gerald
finds a healthy program just for fun. Runs after the end never count for the
duck speedrun award.

## Moving around

You walk with **sticks**. The only button is for the King (below).

| | Headset | Tablet |
|---|---|---|
| Walk | **left stick** — forward is wherever you're looking | **left** on-screen stick |
| Turn | **right stick** — left or right, a step at a time | **right** on-screen stick (push up/down to look up/down) |
| Look | turn your head | drag on the screen |
| Hit the King | **trigger** (either hand), or **A / X** | **⚔ HIT** |

On a tablet with a keyboard (or a laptop standing in for the tablet):
**W A S D** or the arrows walk, **Q / E** turn, **Shift** runs,
**Space** hits the King, drag with the mouse to look.

The walls are where the real walls are, the stairs go where the real
stairs go. If moving in VR makes you queasy, stop pushing the stick and
look at something still — the edges of the view darken while you move to
help.

## Find the duck

**Follow the golden chevrons** on the floor. They lead, room by room and
up or down the stairs (the house has three floors), to **Gerald, the
golden duck** off the Maps and Ducks billboard — he hides in a different
spot every time. Walk right up to him. He lays a golden egg and flies
away, and that's it: you're done. If you were corrupted, you're **reinstalled** and
free to go back to the house — your app says so, and you learn *The
Golden Duck*.

Wander off for two minutes without touching anything (or take the
headset off) and the simulation ends — scan your pass to start again.
If the show is **paused** while you stand at Gerald, the find waits:
stay put, the two minutes don't run out, and it lands the moment the
show resumes.
The same if the house's server restarts while you're walking: the game
ends, so scan your pass again (a duck you already found still counts).

On a night everyone is **banished** to the Internet, Gerald can't help:
finding him only tells you so. The tablet says **GERALD CAN'T HELP**
(*Gerald can't help* your name *tonight. The Internet keeps you — your
phone says what happens next.*) and the headset the same, with no egg and no
fanfare, and the wall screens show it too. The way out is the hand
jive, together.

## The King

Down in the basement hall of the simulation a tall golden chess piece
stands in the server-room doorway: **the King**. He is nothing to do
with the duck — the chevrons never lead to him — but he guards the
server room's door in the simulation, and in the real house he plays
chess for its code.

He has **one health bar for everyone**, all night: every program in
the house knocks it down together, and it never grows back. The bar
over his head reads *15 / 15* at the start of the night.

- **You may hit him once per reinstallation.** Walk up close and face
  him, then press **⚔ HIT** on the tablet (or Space), or pull the
  **trigger** in a headset. *⚔ You hit the King!* — one point off his
  bar, for everyone. Too far, or looking away? It says *Get closer to
  the King.* or *Face him.*
- **One hit, then that's it until you find the duck.** Your
  reinstallation ends when you find Gerald, not when you leave or the
  game times out. Scanning your pass again before then is the same
  reinstallation: ⚔ is already spent, and the game says *You hit him
  this reinstallation. Find Gerald before you hit him again.* The next
  time you're sent, you may hit him again.
- When someone else hits him while you're playing, you see him stagger
  and the bar drop.
- **At 0 he kneels**, for everyone: he goes down on one knee and the
  door he guards in the simulation stands open. A host can stand him
  back up at full health; if you're behind his door when that happens,
  it stays open until you walk out.

**Why hit him?** The real chess game is on the **terminal outside the
real server-room door**. The more he has been hit, the worse he plays
there: at full health he is a grandmaster, and once he has fallen he
has nothing but his pawns. There is no chess in the simulation. See
[The terminals on the walls](terminals.md).

## Ghosts

Everyone else in the simulation walks past you as a **ghost** with their
name over it, wearing **their own profile picture** as a head (Excel
looks like Excel). A program with no picture is a plain glowing ghost.
The wall terminals and the TV show the same ghosts.

## Everyone is watching

The **terminals** on the walls and the **TV** can show the simulation
while anyone is in it. A terminal is a window into the virtual copy of
*its own room*, hung exactly where the real tablet hangs — walk into that
room in the simulation and you'll see the screen glowing on the wall; the
people in the real room see you, a ghost with your name and your
picture. When nobody is in its room it follows whoever is playing, with
a little map of the floor. Find the duck and every screen watching you celebrates. See
[The terminals on the walls](terminals.md).

Need the bathroom, or there's an emergency? Tap **🚻 Bathroom Mode** on
your pass before you leave the tent. The exit is clearly marked. It is
for safety. It is not the way out — Gerald is the way out.
