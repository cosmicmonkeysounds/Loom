Some events have a **simulation**: a virtual copy of the house you're
standing in. In *Trapped in the Internet* it is how a corrupted program
gets out of the Internet — prove you can follow instructions, and you are
**reinstalled**.

## Being sent

When the Antivirus quarantine a corrupted program ([Your
pass](your-pass.md)), they walk it through the Tube to the Internet: the
**tent in the backyard, at the end of the tunnel**. Your phone is locked
until you are reinstalled, and it shows the **Installation Wizard**'s
instructions: why you are there, the headset or the laptop, Bathroom
Mode, and where the exit is (it is clearly marked; it is for safety).
Click Next.

## Help Me

Stuck, lost, feeling unwell, or the headset isn't working? Tap
**🆘 Help Me**: it is on your locked phone, beside Bathroom Mode, and in
the corner of the reinstallation station's screen while you play. A host
is called out to you straight away. Stay where you are. Tapping it again
within a minute only tells you help is already coming.

## Getting in

In the tent there is the **reinstallation station**: a laptop that is
also one of the house's terminals (see [The terminals](terminals.md)).
Hold your **pass** (tap **🎟 Pass** on your app's taskbar — see
[Your pass](your-pass.md)) up to its camera. No camera, or it won't read
your pass? Type your program name in the box, or tap your name under
**IN THE INTERNET NOW**. The wizard opens:

- **Play here** — the simulation runs on the laptop in front of you.
  With no headset free, this is where it starts, straight away.
- **Send to a headset** — when a VR headset (VR-1, VR-2 …) is free, the
  wizard asks first. Put it on; you're already in.

**Or just put a headset on.** The headset's lobby says **PICK YOUR
PROGRAM**, with a tile and your profile picture for every program waiting
to be reinstalled. Point either controller at yours (a thin beam shows
where it points) and pull the **trigger** — or move with a stick and hold
it **right** for a second. It asks **Start as <your name>?**: pull the
trigger on **START** (or hold right again), or **BACK** to pick again.
*Nobody is marked for reinstallation yet.* means there is nobody to pick
right now.

A program that isn't marked reads **PROGRAM NOT MARKED** (*Not marked
for reinstallation.*) for a few seconds, and nothing else happens.
Already walking on a headset? The laptop asks before it moves you here.

## Before you start

Your first reinstallation starts with a short **video**: the
Reinstallation Process, explained. Watch it to the end — it can only be
skipped once you have been reinstalled before (then **Skip video ▶**).

Then a little **training** in the simulation itself, one step at a time:
each step finishes when you do it.

- On the **laptop**: click the picture to take the mouse (it now turns
  your head, like any computer game), look around, walk with **W A S D**,
  run with **Shift**. **Esc** gives you the mouse back.
- In a **headset**: look around, point the beam at a button and pull the
  **trigger**, push the thumbstick forward to walk into a glowing circle,
  then turn round to face a duck behind you — push the stick left or
  right (a small push turns slowly), or just turn your body.

Been reinstalled before? **Skip training ▶** jumps straight in.

While you walk on the laptop, a notice from the story (a blackout, an
alarm, *go to the living room*) covers the game and **pauses** it: you
can't move and nobody is timed out. Your walk carries on when it clears.

## Moving around

You walk with **sticks**. The buttons are for the King (below) and, in a
headset, your microphone.

| | Headset | Tablet |
|---|---|---|
| Walk | **left stick** — forward is wherever you're looking | **left** on-screen stick |
| Turn | **right stick** — left or right (gently: a small push turns slowly), or turn your body | **right** on-screen stick (push up/down to look up/down) |
| Look | turn your head | drag on the screen |
| Hit the King | **trigger** (either hand), or **A / X** | **⚔ HIT** |
| Mute your mic | **B / Y** | **🎤** (or **M**), when the tablet has a voice link |
| Pick your program (lobby) | point + **trigger**, or stick + hold **right** 1 s | scan your pass |

On the laptop (or a tablet with a keyboard): **W A S D** or the arrows
walk, **Q / E** turn, **Shift** runs, **Space** hits the King. Click the
picture and the **mouse** turns your head; **Esc** gives it back (to
click **✕** or **Finish**).

The walls are where the real walls are, the stairs go where the real
stairs go. If moving in VR makes you queasy, stop pushing the stick and
look at something still — the edges of the view darken while you move to
help.

## Find the duck

**FIND GERALD.** Gerald, the golden duck off the Maps and Ducks
billboard, hides somewhere in the house (it has three floors), in a
different spot every time. Look around: after a while a golden trail
lights the way to him. Walk right up to him. He lays a golden egg and
flies away, and that's it: you're done. If you were corrupted, you're
**reinstalled** and free to go back to the house — your app says so, and
you learn *The Golden Duck*.

Wander off for two minutes without touching anything (or take the
headset off) and the simulation ends — scan your pass to start again.
If the show is **paused** while you stand at Gerald, the find waits:
stay put, the two minutes don't run out, and it lands the moment the
show resumes. If the game ever drops while you're walking, scan your
pass again (a duck you already found still counts).

## The King

Down in the basement a tall golden chess piece stands in a doorway:
**the King**. Walk up close and face him, then press **⚔ HIT** on the
tablet (or Space), or pull the **trigger** in a headset. *⚔ You hit the
King!* Too far, or looking away? It says *Get closer to the King.* or
*Face him.*

## Ghosts

Everyone else in the simulation walks past you as a **ghost** with their
name over it: **their own profile picture**, torn into a swarm of pieces
that follows them around (Excel looks like Excel, more or less). A program
with no picture is a swarm of static.

## Everyone is watching

The **terminals** on the walls can see into the simulation, and the
people standing by them can see you: a swarm with your name and your
picture. Walk into a room with a terminal and you'll see its screen
glowing on the virtual wall. See [The terminals on the
walls](terminals.md).

## Talking to the house

The terminals have microphones, and so does the simulation. Walk into a
room with a terminal and you **hear the people standing by it in the real
room**, coming from where its screen hangs; talk, and they hear you on
the terminal. Other players in the simulation can't hear you.

- In a **headset**, your mic is on. **B** or **Y** mutes it (and unmutes
  it), and the HUD says whether it is on or muted and what to press. In
  the lobby, **TEST MIC** shows whether it hears you, and **ASK AGAIN**
  asks for the microphone again if it wasn't allowed.
- The **tent laptop** has no voice link: the tent is its own room. It
  still plays the sound of the TV in The Desktop. A
  tablet or laptop set up elsewhere may have it. Then **Play here** asks
  to use the microphone, the **🎤** button at the top right (or **M**)
  mutes it, and the bottom line says *🎤 a terminal can hear you* while
  one does.
- Your voice only goes out when you speak up. While a terminal's room is
  playing to you, only a voice **louder than the room** goes out — so to
  get a word in over a noisy room, just speak up: the terminal plays you
  and goes quiet itself while you talk, so the room hears you clearly.

Need the bathroom, or there's an emergency? Tap **🚻 Bathroom Mode** on
your phone before you leave the tent. The exit is clearly marked. It is
for safety. It is not the way out — Gerald is the way out.
