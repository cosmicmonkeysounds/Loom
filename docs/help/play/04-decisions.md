Sometimes the story stops and asks **you** to choose.

A numbered menu docks at the bottom of the conversation — that's a
**decision**. The story is holding its breath until you answer.

- Tap an option, or use ↑/↓ and Enter.
- Your pick is yours alone — someone else at the same moment may see
  different options, or none.
- Some choices come back later; some are once-in-a-night. If an option
  vanished since last time, you (or the story) used it up.

There's no undo. That's the fun part. Choose like it matters — it does:
choices move your ⭐ score, your standing with characters, and where
the night goes.

## Cards: security checks, polls, pictures

Sometimes the story hands you something to *do* rather than pick: a
**card** appears in the conversation, like a message. A **security
check** is a grid of squares — tap the ones that match, then **VERIFY**.
A **poll** is a row of buttons. A **picture** is just there to look at.

A card that's waiting on you tags its room **your move** and pulls it to
the top. You answer a card once; after that it shows what you answered.
What happens next is the story's business — sometimes getting it
"right" is the wrong thing to do.
