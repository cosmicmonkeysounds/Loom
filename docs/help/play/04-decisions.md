Sometimes the story stops and asks **you** to choose.

A numbered menu docks at the bottom of the conversation — that's a
**decision**. The story is holding its breath until you answer. One the
story asks you outside any room shows on [Mission](mission.md), under **Your
move**, with its options as big buttons; a room that's asking you rises to
**Your move** in Chats with a yellow dot.

- Tap an option, or use ↑/↓ and Enter.
- While a menu is waiting, the message box may fold to one **⌨ Message …**
  button: tap it to type, and anything you'd half typed is still there.
- Your pick is yours alone — someone else at the same moment may see
  different options, or none.
- Some choices come back later; some are once-in-a-night. If an option
  vanished since last time, you (or the story) used it up.

There's no undo. That's the fun part. Choose like it matters.

## Cards: security checks, polls, pictures

Sometimes the story hands you something to *do* rather than pick: a
**card**. A **security check** is a grid of squares — tap the ones that
match, then **VERIFY**. A **poll** is a row of buttons. A **picture** is
just there to look at.

A card waiting for your answer docks in a **tray** just above the
message box, one at a time (*‹ 1 of 2 ›* when there are more); tap the
tray's bar to fold it to one line while you read the room. The
conversation keeps a one-line receipt in its place (*📝 File a fact ·
answer it below ↓*); tap the receipt to bring the card back up. A card that's waiting on you also tags its room **your move** and pulls it
up the list, and becomes the first big button on Mission. A card that
matters a lot (the bottle landing on you, an invitation onto a side) takes
the whole screen as a **big moment** and is answered right there: see
[Notifications & big moments](notifications.md).

You answer a card once. After that it leaves the tray and settles into
one line where it was asked (*🛡️ Security check · submitted*, *📊 … ·
you chose Truth*); tap the line to see the whole card again. What
happens next is the story's business.
