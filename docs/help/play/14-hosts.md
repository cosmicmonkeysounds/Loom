A **host** runs the night from their own booth: the cue sheet, what
needs a host, judging, announcements, fixing a guest and the house's own
sound and lights, all without leaving the app. Your booth is a host's
when the story makes your part one (at *Trapped in the Internet*: Task
Manager, Adobe Acrobat and the Recycling Bin), or when you added the
moderator passcode (⊞ Start → Add moderator powers). The scanner, a
guest's card and the rooms work as in any booth ([Performer
station](performers.md)).

**The hosts call the games.** Clippy and the four Antivirus are
**players** now: they play the night like the guests, with a Mission and a
score, and never call a cue. Bingo, the hunts' results, the bottle, Bobbing
for Truth and the awards are yours (and the director's) to fire; the
player whose game it is gets a *NEW MISSION* pop-out on their phone when
it starts. Radio them before you fire.

**Bingo (wave 26).** A guest may call **🎱 BINGO!** on their own phone
while the card is up: it goes straight to the results (the caller and the
time left are recorded, and Clippy gets *Check The Bingo*). Your ⏰ works
as before. A false call is undone with **↩ Not a Bingo** on the caller's
card (Clippy, a host or a director), only before any result (🎱 / 🎯 /
🤐) is in: the phase, the clock, the TV and the walls go back, a house
line calls everyone back to their cards, and another guest may call. A
call after time-up comes back with no countdown: call ⏰ as usual. The
round is **one round**: the first 🎯 or 🤐 (or a capture during Bingo)
closes it.

**The first glitch (📺, wave 26)** takes over every phone and terminal
once; your booth gets a short look-only flash that never blocks a cue.
The talk scenes' ⚡ ad bursts (wave 32) are that short look-only flash on
every phone, guests' included.

**Your taskbar** reads **⊞ Start · Show · Chats · Desk · 📷 Scan**:

- **Show** is your home: the night, what to fire next and what needs you
  (below).
- **Chats** is the room list: the staff rooms, Readouts, guests'
  messages (the number on it counts guests waiting on a reply).
- **Desk** is the house desk: its cues, ■ STOP ALL, and the speaker and
  light checks (below).
- **📷 Scan** opens the scanner, as in any booth: passes, and codex QRs into
  your own Codex (**⊞ Start → What … knows**). Every guest, with their
  card, is in Show's **People**.

## The Show window

Top to bottom:

- **The night strip**, on one line; swipe it for the rest: **📣 who is
  calling** (*📣 You*, *📣 Task Manager*, *📣 Run*, *📣 nobody*), the act,
  the story's phase, the show clock (*🕐 7:42 PM*), ⏸ or 🚪 while the
  clock is stopped or the doors are closed, the values the story asks
  hosts to watch (*Marked 3*) and the soonest countdown.
- **Who's calling**: *📣 Task Manager is calling.* / *You're calling.* /
  *Nobody is calling.*, with **📣 I'll call** or **Stop calling**.
- **NEXT**, big: the cue the sheet suggests with its code and title,
  **ARM** beside it, what it does (two lines, **▸ more**), and *then:*
  the cue after it.
  - **Tap ARM, then FIRE** within four seconds; the bar under FIRE drains
    as the time runs out. A fast double tap only arms, and a pocket tap
    disarms by itself.
  - A cue that needs a person (an award's winner) says **PICK** first:
    choose them, then ARM and FIRE (a cue armed for a person waits 10 s
    for FIRE). An award suggests its winner first
    and fills in the value.
  - When nothing is on offer it says so: the story is running, usually a
    timer you shouldn't press early.
- **🤖 Talk** (only during a talk scene): Task Manager types what Clippy
  says out loud and Trabolta answers on the TV, with canned lines for both,
  ⚡ Glitch and NEXT ([Talking with Trabolta](talk-back.md)).
- Under NEXT, one line says what just happened: *✓ Fired V2*, *✕ V6 not
  taken —* with the story's reason from `# hosts`, *Cues moved — tap
  again*, or *⚠ Last update 11 s ago · Can't reach the server* (FIRE dims
  until the server is back).
- **LAST**: the cue and who pressed it, and for a press from this phone,
  **did:** what the story said back (*the summons opened · 7 not checked
  in*).
- Then the sections, in this order: **Needs you (n)**, **Your jobs**,
  **Coming up** (the next cues of the act and every countdown), **Judge
  (n)**, **Cue sheet**, **People** and **More**. Each head folds its
  section; Needs and Judge open by themselves while something waits.
  A row that takes you somewhere (a need, the caller chip, a refused
  press) opens its section and scrolls to it.
- **Your jobs** is your own objectives, the NOW card laid open — the
  brief, the whole checklist (tick your own to-dos), and its button (a
  picker, a target's card). The NOW card at the top steps aside while
  Show is open.

Announcements to everyone do not pop on a host's phone: LAST says what
fired, and the 📜 Chat log has the words. A banner on a host phone
leaves by itself after about 45 seconds on screen (× clears it at once).

**Every host may call the show.** Whoever is calling says so with **📣
I'll call**. Everyone sees it, the director's laptop and phone included.
When someone else is calling, your FIRE asks once before it goes: *Fire
🏓 Truth Pong? · Task Manager is calling the show. Fire it anyway?
They'll see you did.* with **Fire anyway** and **Cancel** (the default).
With nobody calling, nothing asks. If two hosts (or a host and the
director at Run) press the same moment, the server takes one: the other
phone reads *Cues moved — tap again*, and nothing fires twice. Every
press is logged as *Booth · your character*.

## The NEXT bar

While another window is open (Chats, the Desk, a room), a slim **NEXT
bar** sits just above the taskbar, so you can fire without going back:

- **Line one** is NEXT (*V2 · Call everyone to the Desktop*), with
  **ARM** / **FIRE** beside it. It is the same arm as Show's: arm on
  either, fire on either.
- **Line two** is the night strip on one line (on a narrow phone the
  phase, then the second value, then the countdown step aside) and **⚠
  n** for what needs a host; after a press, what happened.
- **Tap the bar** (anywhere but ARM / FIRE) to go to Show. After a
  refused press it opens Show at Needs, where the whole reason is.

While you type, the bar steps aside with the taskbar.

## The sections


- **Cue sheet** — a filter box and every cue by act, NEXT highlighted,
  fired cues dimmed: fire one out of order from here (same ARM → FIRE).
- **Needs you (n)** — what is waiting on a host. At the very top, a guest
  asking to get back in from a new phone: **🔁 WinZip wants back in**.
  Check it's really them (their face, their badge or pass), then tap
  **✅ Let them in** (their phone signs straight in as their program) or
  **✋ Not them** ([Getting back in](getting-back-in.md)). Then, while programs are
  marked: **🚨 n marked · m nobody on it** (tap it for the Antivirus'
  Wanted board, read-only: claims are theirs, so radio an Antivirus). Then
  your press the story
  refused (with its `# hosts` reason), near misses and filings to judge,
  transfers held for the auditor, a part nobody holds or a phone gone
  offline, a character with no worker, a long queue at Trabolta, guests
  nobody has seen in a while (**👋 Went home** on each), silent wall
  terminals. Each row opens where you fix it.
- **Judge (n)** — the **Judging** switch: *Auto* (the house accepts exact
  matches, Trabolta rules the rest; what he can't rule waits here) or
  *Review* (every filing waits for a host). The line under it counts
  what is with him (*🤖 3 with Trabolta (2 queued)*). Then, when the story
  judges filed facts: each filing waiting for a verdict and the latest
  judged ones, with **✅** accurate, **😤** confident lie and **🤷**
  uncertain. A filing he has reads *🤖 queued for Trabolta* (waiting its
  turn) or *🤖 asking Trabolta…* (he is ruling now), then his ruling; a
  near miss also shows the answer it is close to (⚠). Judge only what he
  couldn't rule before 📋: one still waiting at the round's results
  keeps the house's proposal, and your verdict always wins over his.
- **People** — **🗂 Every guest, with their cards** (the booth's Guests
  list), or find a guest here, then **🗂 Open card**, **🚻 End Bathroom
  Mode** (when it's on), **⛔ Restrict / Unrestrict**, **👋 Went home /
  Back**, **🔁 Re-pair** (a single-use QR for a lost phone) and **📡 Scan
  as…** (scan them as any part).
- **More** — the rest of the night, in three tabs: **Night** (the show,
  the clock, announce, the alert level), **House** (wallet, the King,
  terminals, Trabolta, crews) and **Setup** (codes and invitations, parts).
  It opens on Setup while the doors are closed and on Night after; a Needs
  row opens the right tab. One section each:
  - **⏯ The show**: **🚪 Open the doors** before the doors, **⏸ Pause**
    while running, **▶ Resume** while paused. Tap to arm, tap again to
    fire. Pausing stops the story clock on every phone and booth;
    nothing is lost.
  - **📣 Announce**: a line to every phone (it posts in the 📜 Chat log and lands in every Recycle Bin). Turn **🔔
    Alert** on to make every phone chime, buzz and pop it up. Phones that turned their voice on also say it out loud.
  - **🚨 Alert level**: how loud the story's moments are on every guest's
    phone — **Balloon** (a tip by the taskbar that times out), **Sticky**
    (a banner until tapped), **Dialog** (a full-screen box with OK, one
    at a time), **Blue screen** (every beat to a guest takes the
    screen). Every guest's screen glitches once when it changes. The story sets
    it too, at its own moments, so a level you pick holds until the next
    one the story sets.
  - **💰 Wallet**: transfers held for the auditor (approve, deny, approve
    all), frozen coin (☀ lift), and the stimulus.
  - **♚ The King**: his health, **Set…** and **👑 Restore**.
  - **📟 Terminals**: every wall terminal and the TV, red when silent,
    **Identify**, and the TV's mute.
  - **🔊 House sound**: **🔇 Stop all house sounds** fades out every story
    sound, speaker test and the music playing through the house speakers
    (the music comes back at the next act);
    **🔇 + the announcement** also cuts what the house is saying and the
    ones queued behind it. It never touches the TV's voice (its mute is
    above), the terminals or the live mics, and stopping the dance song
    doesn't move the story's clock. If nothing is listening (the house
    Mac's audio job is down) it says so.
  - **🎟 Codes and invitations**: **Show the codes** (the guests' and the
    performers'), **▦ Show the join QR** for a guest to scan off your
    screen, and each invitation's **▦ QR** and **🔓 Open 2 min** for a
    lost one. The moderator passcode never shows here.
  - **🎭 Performer parts**: who holds each part, and **⏏ Release** for a
    dead phone.
  - **🤖 Trabolta** (any character a machine voices): whether its worker
    is on, the queue, and the guests waiting on an answer. Tap one to
    read their thread and **answer as him**.
  - **Crews**: the crews guests founded tonight, with **Rename** and
    **Dissolve** (for a name that shouldn't be on the walls).

Anything you can't take back asks in place first (*…? Yes / No*).

## The Desk

The house's own cue list, built in the editor's Run → House: sounds on
the house speakers, announcements, light looks, waits, and story events.
Play fires and tests it; it never edits a cue. Its **Setup** (is the
sound, the lights and each outside mind connected, and what to do when
one isn't) and **Try it** (play a look, a story sound or a line on the
speakers) are in [The Desk: setup and tests](house-desk.md).

- **The status chips**: the house sound router (*🔊 router online*, or
  offline), sound files missing or still placeholders, the lights module,
  lights Zigbee2MQTT doesn't know, and problems in the desk itself.
- **■ STOP ALL** stops every desk cue that is playing **and** the house
  sounds, the music too until the next act. Use it whenever something is wrong.
- **STANDBY** is the cue **GO** plays: its number and name, the operator's
  note, its steps in words and **⚡ Tells the story: …** — the story events
  it fires, so nobody fires them by surprise. GO plays the cue on standby
  and stands by the next one. If another host (or Run) moved the standby
  just before you, nothing plays and it says *Standby moved — press GO
  again*.
- **Playing**: each running cue with its progress, what it is doing now,
  and **■ Stop**. A step that didn't happen says so (*no-file: 🔊
  server_hum*).
- **Cues**: the list by section. Tap a cue to stand it by (▶ marks the
  standby, ♪ one that is playing).

### Walking the checks

Before the doors, walk the house with your phone and check every speaker,
output, story sound, light and look, one at a time:

1. The row on screen names it and where it is (*Living L · Living room ·
   out 1*), with any reason it may not work before you listen (*no file
   on the house Mac*, *the lights module is offline*).
2. Tap the big **Test** (*🔊 Test tone*, *▶ Play it*, *💡 Flash it*, *💡
   Play the look*). A colour bulb has a button per colour: Red, Green,
   Blue, White, Dim, Off.
3. Then **✓ Heard it** (a light: **✓ Saw it**) or **✗ Nothing**. The mark
   is saved for everyone (Run → House shows it too) and the walk moves to
   the next row with no mark. **next ›** and **‹ back** move by hand;
   **Every check** lists them all to jump to one.

The head counts the walk (*12 of 30 checked · 2 ✗*). Once the doors are
open every test asks first (*The show is live — guests will hear this.
Test anyway?*): guests hear a tone and see a light.

## Buttons on a guest's card

A host's card is a performer's card ([Performer station](performers.md)):
the facts, at most three buttons, what the press came to. There is no
*All buttons…* list and no earlier readouts any more. What a host's card
keeps that a player's doesn't: the buttons the story offers because of
this guest (🩹 Unmark on a marked program) join the face, and a refused
press shows the `# hosts` line that says why.

A host's guest cards also offer other characters' buttons, but only the
ones the story hears from your booth (at the show, the server room's
🔌 💥 ❌ 🚪 🔧). Buttons only their own characters' rules
hear stay theirs: a host never sees the Antivirus' ✅ / 👁 or
Adobe Acrobat's 🫘 from another booth, and the server refuses them
(*not your button …*). Radio the performer instead (at the show, an
Antivirus: they hunt from the Wanted board).

**🛡 Send to the Internet** is a host's too (at the show). Scan a
marked program's pass in person and your booth opens *CORRUPTION
DETECTED*, as an Antivirus' does: one big **🛡 SEND TO THE INTERNET**
(one tap, after a moment), **Not now**, or **🩹 Not them…**. On any
other card the story offers it on (every program's while the patch-bay
alarm is on, and every program's once the Bingo round is over) it sits
under *Marks or captures …: tap twice*, even when the card's other
buttons fill the face: the first tap arms it, the second sends them to
the Internet. Walk them through the Tube to the tent. A marked
program's poster on the Wanted board reads *CAUGHT BY* you; a host
earns no ⭐ for it.

## Not in the app

Restarting the story, pushing a draft, going live, ending the run, the
World editor's variables, exporting the log and Trabolta's Mind page
stay on the laptop, in the editor's Run mode. So does the god view of
every room. If you need one of those, radio whoever is at Run.
