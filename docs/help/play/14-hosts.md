A **host** runs the night from their own booth: the cue sheet, what
needs a host, judging, announcements, and fixing a guest, all without
leaving the app. Your booth is a host's when the story makes your part
one (at *Trapped in the Internet*: Task Manager, Adobe Acrobat and the
Recycling Bin; Clippy is not), or when you added the moderator passcode
(⊞ Start → Add moderator powers). Everything else in your booth works as
on any other ([Performer station](performers.md)).

## The NEXT bar

A host's booth has one more bar, just above the taskbar.

- **Line one** is the cue the sheet suggests: its code and title
  (*V2 · Call everyone to the Desktop*), with **ARM** beside it.
- **Tap ARM, then FIRE** within four seconds. A fast double tap only
  arms, and a pocket tap disarms by itself. A cue that needs a person
  (an award's winner) says **PICK** first: choose them, then ARM and
  FIRE. An award suggests its winner first and fills in the value.
- **Line two** is the night: the act, the story's phase, ⏸ or 🚪 while
  the clock is stopped or the doors are closed, the soonest countdown
  (*⏳ Hunt 1 ends, 4:59 left*) and **⚠ n** for what needs a host.
  While you're armed it says *Tap FIRE within 4 s*; after a press it
  says what happened: *✓ Fired V2*, or *✕ V6 not taken —* with the
  story's reason from `# hosts`.
- When nothing is on offer it says so: the story is running, usually a
  timer you shouldn't press early.

**Any host may fire.** Agree by radio who calls the cues. If two hosts
(or a host and the director at Run) press the same moment, the server
takes one: the other phone reads *Cues moved — tap again*, and nothing
fires twice. The Cues tab's **LAST** line says who pressed last (*you*,
*Task Manager*, *the story*). Every press is logged as *Booth · your
character*.

If the bar loses the server it says *⚠ Last update 11 s ago · Can't
reach the server* and dims FIRE until it is back. While you type in a
room the whole bar steps aside; while you type in the console only the
NEXT line does.

## The console

Tap the bar (anywhere but ARM / FIRE) to pull up the console; tap it
again, or Escape, to put it away. On a wide screen it is a panel at the
right. While it is open, pop-ups wait (a balloon sits above it). It has
five tabs:

- **Cues** — LAST and who pressed it, a filter box, and the night's
  cues by act, NEXT highlighted, fired cues dimmed. It shares one arm
  with the NEXT bar: arm on either, fire on either.
- **Needs (n)** — what is waiting on a host: your press the story
  refused (with its `# hosts` reason), near misses and filings to judge,
  transfers held for the auditor, a part nobody holds or a phone gone
  offline, a character with no worker, a long queue at Trabolta, guests
  nobody has seen in a while (**👋 Went home** on each), silent wall
  terminals. Each row opens where you fix it.
- **Judge (n)** — when the story judges filed facts: each filing waiting
  for a verdict and the latest judged ones, with **✅** accurate, **😤**
  confident lie and **🤷** uncertain. A near miss shows the answer it is
  close to and Trabolta's ruling (or that he's been asked).
- **People** — find a guest, then **🗂 Open card**, **🚻 End Bathroom
  Mode** (when it's on), **⛔ Restrict / Unrestrict**, **👋 Went home /
  Back**, **🔁 Re-pair** (a single-use QR for a lost phone) and **📡 Scan
  as…** (scan them as any part).
- **More** — the rest of the night, one section each:
  - **⏯ The show**: **🚪 Open the doors** before the doors, **⏸ Pause**
    while running, **▶ Resume** while paused. Tap to arm, tap again to
    fire. Pausing stops the story clock on every phone and booth;
    nothing is lost.
  - **📣 Announce**: a line to every phone, in the lobby. Turn **🔔
    Alert** on to make every phone chime, buzz and pop it up.
  - **🚨 Alert level**: how loud the story's moments are on every guest's
    phone — **Balloon** (a tip by the taskbar that times out), **Sticky**
    (a banner until tapped), **Dialog** (a full-screen box with OK, one
    at a time), **Blue screen** (every beat to a guest takes the
    screen). Every screen glitches once when it changes. The story sets
    it too, at its own moments, so a level you pick holds until the next
    one the story sets.
  - **💰 Wallet**: transfers held for the auditor (approve, deny, approve
    all), frozen coin (☀ lift), and the stimulus.
  - **♚ The King**: his health, **Set…** and **👑 Restore**.
  - **📟 Terminals**: every wall terminal and the TV, red when silent,
    **Identify**, and the TV's mute.
  - **🎟 Codes and invitations**: **Show the codes** (the guests' and the
    performers'), **▦ Show the join QR** for a guest to scan off your
    screen, and each invitation's **▦ QR** and **🔓 Open 2 min** for a
    lost one. The moderator passcode never shows here.
  - **🎭 Performer parts**: who holds each part, and **⏏ Release** for a
    dead phone.
  - **🤖 Trabolta** (any character a machine voices): whether its worker
    is on, the queue, and the guests waiting on an answer. Tap one to
    read their thread and **answer as him**.

Anything you can't take back asks in place first (*…? Yes / No*).

## Buttons on a guest's card

A host's guest cards also offer other characters' buttons, but only the
ones the story hears from your booth (at the show, the server room's
patch-bay 🔌 💥 ❌). Buttons only their own characters' rules
hear stay theirs: a host never sees the Antivirus' 🛡 / ✅ / 👁 or
Adobe Acrobat's 🫘 from another booth, and the server refuses them
(*not your button …*). Radio the performer instead.

## Not in the app

Restarting the story, pushing a draft, going live, ending the run, the
World editor's variables, exporting the log and Trabolta's Mind page
stay on the laptop, in the editor's Run mode. So does the god view of
every room. If you need one of those, radio whoever is at Run.
