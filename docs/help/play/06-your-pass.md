Tap **☰** in the header to open your pass. When the story is running
you on rails (the dark opening screen), it puts your pass on the screen
itself when someone needs to scan you — and **🎟️ My pass** in the corner
opens it any time.

## The QR pass

That QR code is *you*. Performers **scan it** to pull you into the
story — interrogations, blessings, capture, secret missions. When
someone in costume asks to scan you: let them. That's the game finding
you.

## The story's buttons

Some stories give you things to do from your pass — ring a bell, knock,
raise a hand. If there are any, they're listed right under your QR code.
At *Trapped in the Internet* one of them is **🚻 Bathroom Mode**: tap it
before you step out so the Antivirus know you are not hacking the
Internet, and tap **Back from the bathroom** (the same spot) when you're
back. The guided tour points it out on your pass when the story uses it.

## Your group

Once you've joined one of the story's groups — a side, a house, a
society — it shows next to your name, and its private room appears in
your inbox. Feeling treacherous? Your pass has a
**defect** button. Switching sides is dramatic, remembered, and not
free — the story tracks heat on people who do.

## Your score

⭐ rises and falls with your choices, your loyalty, your escapes. What
it *means* is for the night to reveal. When the story has a currency,
the ⭐ on your card is your **🪙 balance** instead — tap it to open your
Wallet ([Your wallet](wallet.md)).

## Getting captured

Some stories can **capture** you — the screen goes dark-red and your
world shrinks until you're freed, you escape, or someone bargains for
you. Being captured isn't losing; it's a plot development. Work the
room from the inside.

## Restricted

Before that, a story may **restrict** you: the app greys out, your card
says **⛔ restricted**, the story's buttons are gone from your pass, you
can't check into rooms or post in them, and your rooms list shrinks to
your own feed, your private messages, your pinned Quest Log / Codex /
Wallet and the one or two places the story still lets you see. A pinned
line at the top of your feed tells you what to do about it. At *Trapped in the Internet* that is being **marked for
reinstallation** — *🚨 GO TO THE INTERNET* — and an Antivirus will come
and escort you through the Tube. The mark comes off the moment the
Antivirus sends you into the Internet (or a host lifts it); the
reinstallation itself is what gets you out again.

## Leaving

**Leave the event** on your pass signs you out for good. Closing the
app doesn't — you can always come back as yourself.
