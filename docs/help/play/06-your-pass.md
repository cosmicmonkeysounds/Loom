Tap **☰** in the header to open your pass.

## The QR pass

That QR code is *you*. Performers **scan it** to pull you into the
story — interrogations, blessings, capture, secret missions. When
someone in costume asks to scan you: let them. That's the game finding
you.

## Your faction

Once you've joined a side, it shows next to your name, and its private
room appears in your inbox. Feeling treacherous? Your pass has a
**defect** button. Switching sides is dramatic, remembered, and not
free — the story tracks heat on people who do.

## Your score

⭐ rises and falls with your choices, your loyalty, your escapes. What
it *means* is for the night to reveal.

## Getting captured

Some stories can **capture** you — the screen goes dark-red and your
world shrinks until you're freed, you escape, or someone bargains for
you. Being captured isn't losing; it's a plot development. Work the
room from the inside.

## Leaving

**Leave the event** on your pass signs you out for good. Closing the
app doesn't — you can always come back as yourself.
