You are your program for the whole night. Closing the tab, reloading,
pressing Back, locking the phone, losing the Wi-Fi for a while: none of
that signs you out. Open the party's page again and you're where you
left off.

You only need this page if the phone **forgot** you: the party opened in
a different browser (a camera app that picked another one, a link from a
chat app), you cleared your browser, you tapped **Leave the event**, or
you're on a new phone. Your program is still yours; nobody else can
take it. Get back in one of these ways.

## Your badge

Your badge has a QR on it. **Scan it with your phone's camera** when you
arrive: it opens the party with everything filled in, and it becomes yours
when you pick your program. No camera? Open the party and **type the code
under the QR** in the code box: it does the same. (Joined another way? Link it
from **Me → 🪪 Link my badge**.)

- Left the event, or a host released you? **Scan your badge with any
  phone's camera**, or open the party, tap **🔁 Already playing? Get back
  in → 🪪 Scan my badge**, or type the code printed under its QR. You're
  back in, as you.
- A badge is one phone's. While a phone has you, your badge on any other
  phone says it's **already signed in on a phone**, and signs nobody in. If
  that's your own phone in another browser (a camera can open a different
  one), go back to that browser: you're still you there. Lost the phone, or
  moved to a new one? Tap **🙋 Ask a host** on that screen.
- Being offline doesn't let go of it: a dead phone keeps your badge until
  a host moves you. If you tap **Leave the event** with no signal, your phone
  lets go as soon as it is back.
- Your badge is also your pass: the Antivirus and the hosts can scan it
  instead of the pass on your phone.
- Keep it on you. While no phone has you, anyone who scans your badge
  becomes you.

A badge you haven't linked yet just carries on into the join, and links
to the program you pick.

## Ask a host

No badge, or never linked it? Open the party and tap **🔁 Already
playing? Get back in → 🙋 No badge? Ask a host**. Pick your program from
the list, then find a host and tell them. They tap
**Let them in** on their phone, and yours signs straight in, with the same
program, codex and score. That signs out the phone that had you. Stay on that
page while you wait: if you tap **Cancel** or close it, nothing moves and the
other phone stays signed in. If no one answers in ten minutes, ask again.

## Your invitation

Invited as a featured program? Your badge has your program on it. **Scan
it with your phone's camera** and tap **Start your upload**. After that it
is one phone's, like any badge: it signs you back in once you've left, and
another phone can only ask a host. An invitation that came before your badge
works the same way.
