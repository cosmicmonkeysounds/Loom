You are your program for the whole night. Closing the tab, reloading,
pressing Back, locking the phone, losing the Wi-Fi for a while: none of
that signs you out. Open the party's page again and you're where you
left off.

You only need this page if the phone **forgot** you: the party opened in
a different browser (a camera app that picked another one, a link from a
chat app), you cleared your browser, you tapped **Leave the event**, or
you're on a new phone. Your program is still yours; nobody else can
take it. Get back in one of these ways.

## Your badge

Your badge has a QR on it. **Scan it with your phone's camera** when you
arrive: it opens the party with everything filled in, and it becomes yours
when you pick your program. (Joined another way? Link it from **Me → 🪪 Link
my badge**.)

- Lost the page? **Scan your badge with any phone's camera**, or open
  the party, tap **🔁 Already playing? Get back in → 🪪 Scan my badge**,
  or type the code printed under its QR. You're back in, as you.
- Your badge is also your pass: the Antivirus and the hosts can scan it
  instead of the pass on your phone.
- Keep it on you. Anyone who scans your badge with a phone that isn't
  signed in becomes you.

A badge you haven't linked yet just carries on into the join, and links
to the program you pick.

## Ask a host

No badge, or never linked it? Open the party and tap **🔁 Already
playing? Get back in → 🙋 No badge? Ask a host**. Pick your program from
the list, then find a host and tell them. They tap
**Let them in** on their phone, and yours signs straight in. If no one
answers in ten minutes, ask again.

## Your invitation

Invited as a featured program? Your badge has your program on it. **Scan
it with your phone's camera** and tap **Start your upload**. Once you've
joined, the same scan signs you back in. An invitation that came before your
badge works the same way.
