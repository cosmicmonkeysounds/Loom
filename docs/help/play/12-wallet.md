Some stories give you **money**. In *Trapped in the Internet* it is
Truthcoin, and it is yours to spend: send it, ask for it, trade what you
know for it. The story is watching every coin.

## Where it is

Your balance sits on your card at the top of the rooms list — **🪙 120
Truthcoin** where the ⭐ used to be. Tap it, or open the **🪙 Wallet**
room pinned at the top (beside your Quest Log and Codex). Its line in
the list says how much you have and how many deals are still open.

Every deal is a card in the Wallet, with its own thread that updates as
the deal moves: *💸 You sent 50 Truthcoin to Excel — settles in 42 s…*,
then *✅ settled*. Nothing is final until the card says so.

## The four buttons

At the bottom of the Wallet:

- **💸 Send** — pick a person, type an amount. Whole coins, up to a limit
  the story sets, and never more than you have.
- **🙏 Request** — ask someone for coin. They get a card with **Pay** /
  **Decline**; it expires if nobody answers (the story says how long —
  ten minutes, usually).
- **💱 Trade a fact** — offer one of your Codex entries for coin, or
  offer coin for one of theirs. The other side gets **Accept** /
  **Decline**. The moment they accept, the coin moves *and* the entry is
  shared, in one step — until then it is held in escrow, so nobody can
  spend the same coin twice. Sharing lore this way counts as sharing:
  the story notices exactly as if you had handed it over
  ([Lore](codex.md)).
- **💰 Stimulus** — one program in the story may be able to mint coin
  for everyone, a few times a night. If that's you, the button is here
  and says how many are left.

**Cancel** withdraws a request or an offer nobody has answered yet, or a
transfer of yours still held for the auditor.

## The audit

The story may name an **auditor** — at *Trapped in the Internet*, CRA
Agent Software. Then every transfer you send is either **pending** —
it settles by itself after a short window (a minute) unless the
auditor denies it — or, every third transfer anyone at the party sends,
**held** until the auditor presses Approve. A denied transfer comes straight back to you,
refunded, and the card says who denied it. Trades don't go through the
audit; a request someone pays does, like any send.

Expect questions. Elaborate security questions, even.

## The small print

Send too fast and the app asks you to wait a minute. Your balance is
still your score: the story adds and takes coin of its own — prizes,
fines — and it all shows here.
