Some stories give you **money**. In *Trapped in the Internet* it is
Truthcoin, and it is yours to spend: send it, ask for it, trade what you
know for it.

## Where it is

Your balance sits in the taskbar's tray, bottom right — **🪙 120**
(🧊 while frozen). Tap it for what's on hold and **Open the Wallet ›**,
or tap the **🪙 Wallet** tile on [Mission](mission.md) (beside your Quest
Log and Codex): it says how much you have and how many requests are
waiting on you. A coin request to you is also a row in Mission's **Your
move**.

Every deal is a card in the Wallet, with its own thread that updates as
the deal moves: *💸 You sent 50 Truthcoin to Excel — settles in 42 s…*,
then *✅ settled*. Nothing is final until the card says so.

## The buttons

At the bottom of the Wallet:

- **💸 Send** — pick a person, type an amount. Whole coins, up to a limit
  the story sets, and never more than you have. Coin goes to programs
  like you: the story's characters have no wallet. At *Trapped in the
  Internet* a bet settles player to player — the winner sends a 🙏
  Request, the loser pays — and McAfee only referees.
- **🙏 Request** — ask someone for coin. They get a card with **Pay** /
  **Decline**; it expires if nobody answers (the story says how long —
  ten minutes, usually). Once the payment goes through, both cards close:
  *✅ Paid — you sent 40 Truthcoin* on the payer's, *✅ Paid — Paint sent
  40 Truthcoin* on yours. A payment the auditor denies, or one cancelled
  while it was held, closes both cards too, and the card says who
  stopped it: *⛔ Payment denied by CRA Agent Software; refunded* (on
  the payer's phone, *… denied your payment; refunded*). A payment the
  payer took back reads *↩️ You cancelled the payment* on their phone
  and *↩️ Paint cancelled the payment* on yours. A payment or a request
  the house itself cancels says so on both cards (*↩️ … cancelled …*),
  never that the asker withdrew it.
- **💱 Trade a fact** — offer one of your Codex entries for coin, or
  offer coin for one of theirs. The other side gets **Accept** /
  **Decline**. The moment they accept, the coin moves *and* the entry is
  shared, in one step — until then it is held in escrow, so nobody can
  spend the same coin twice. Sharing lore this way counts exactly as if
  you had handed it over ([Lore](codex.md)).

**Cancel** withdraws a request or an offer nobody has answered yet, or a
transfer of yours still held for the auditor.

## The audit

The story may name an **auditor** — at *Trapped in the Internet*, CRA
Agent Software. Then every transfer you send is **pending** for a short
while (about a minute) before it settles, and some are **🚩 flagged** for
an audit and held until it is done: the card says so on both phones,
yours and the one it's for (at *Trapped in the Internet*, *🚩 Flagged for
a CRA audit*, and MyCRA). The person it's for gets a card of their own
(*Winamp is sending you 10 Truthcoin*) with no buttons: it lands, or
doesn't, by itself, and their card says which. A denied transfer comes
straight back to you, refunded, and the card says who denied it. Trades
don't go through the audit; a request someone pays does, like any send.

Expect questions. Elaborate security questions, even.

## The small print

If your coin is **frozen**, your balance says 🧊 frozen and the Wallet
counts down to the thaw: until then you can't send, pay a request or buy
a fact, but coin still comes in, and you can still ask, sell, decline
and cancel.

If the story **restricts** you, the Wallet says ⛔ and its buttons go:
no sending, requesting, paying or accepting until it's lifted. You can
still decline an offer or cancel your own.

Send too fast and the app asks you to wait a minute. The story adds and
takes coin of its own — prizes, fines — and it all shows here.
