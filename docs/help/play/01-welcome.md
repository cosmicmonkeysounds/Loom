Welcome to the show. This app is your window into a **live interactive
story** — part chatroom, part game, all happening around you in the
room tonight.

## Logging on

1. Pick **I'm a Guest**.
2. Enter the **event code** from your host (a QR code you scanned may
   have filled it in already).
3. **Pick who you are tonight.** The story offers its programs — each
   with a picture, each one person's: a greyed-out name is already
   taken. Want something else? **Search** for any software you can
   think of (*NHL 97*, *Winamp*…) and tap it — that's your name now.
   Antivirus software is off limits to guests; the story has its own.
   A name marked **🔒 Reserved** is saved for someone with an
   invitation (below).
4. **Pick your picture.** Most programs have a few — the logo, the box
   art, a screenshot — so choose the one that looks like *you*, then
   tap **Be …**. Changed your mind later? Tap your picture (top left) →
   **Change my picture**.

No account, no password. If you close the app, just open it again in the
same browser — you'll come back as yourself. Late in a long night the
busy rooms show their last few hundred lines, but your own messages
with people, every card still waiting for you, every alert, your Codex,
Quest Log and Wallet come back whole.

## You were invited as someone

Some programs are saved for invited guests. If your host gave you an
**invitation** with a QR on it, scan that one to join: it opens the app
with your program unlocked for you, and everyone else finds it folded
under **🔒 Reserved for invited guests** (*This program is saved for an
invited guest — ask a host.*). This browser remembers your invitation, so if the show restarts
and you have to join again, you still get your program here. On another
phone or browser you need the invitation again. Lost it? Ask a host:
they can open your program to anyone for two minutes, and the join
screen notices within about 12 seconds — pick it straight away.

## Scan codes from inside the app

The party hides QR codes on the walls: lore for your Codex, and signs
that check you into a room. **Scan them with 📷 Scan on the taskbar**
(at the bottom of every screen, and next to your Codex's message box),
never with your phone's own camera. One tap opens the camera; under it
is a box to **type the code** instead (or a room's name, to check in).
Scanning in the app keeps you signed in; the phone's camera may open a
different browser, where you aren't joined.

If you did use the phone's camera and it opened a page saying *this
browser isn't the one you joined in*, nothing is lost: it shows the
code (or the room) in big letters. Go back to the app you joined with,
tap 📷 Scan and type it in the box. Not joined yet? Tap **I haven't
joined yet → Join**.

## Lost your phone?

A dead battery, a cleared browser, a QR that opened in a different
browser, a borrowed phone: don't sign up again — find a host (or see
[Getting back in](getting-back-in.md)). A host can get you a **re-pair
QR**: scan it with any phone and you're back in *as yourself* — same program, Codex, quests and score. The code
works once, for ten minutes. If that phone was already signed in as
someone, it asks first: **Switch this phone** or **Stay …**.

## The first minutes

Some stories open **on rails**: when you first log on you may see only a
dark screen with the story speaking to you — no rooms, no buttons —
until it lets you go (press **Continue**). Then you land on **Mission**,
your home (see [Your mission](mission.md)), and a guide may walk you
through the app in five quick steps: **Mission**, **Chats**, **Pass and
Scan**, the **Recycle Bin** where anything you missed lands, and **Start**.
You can **skip** the tour.

## What you're looking at

The phone is the old computer you're trapped in, so it looks like one:
a title bar at the top, a taskbar at the bottom, and one window in
between.

- **Mission** is home: what to do now, where to go, its buttons big, and
  what's waiting on you. See [Your mission](mission.md).
- **Chats** is every place, person and chatroom, in fixed sections, with a
  yellow dot where it's your move. See [Chats](rooms.md).
- **The Recycle Bin** (top right) is your notifications: every alert and
  big moment of the night, each with the buttons that take you there. See
  [Notifications](notifications.md).
- **Your picture** (top left) opens **Me**: your name, picture, badges,
  balance and pass. See [Your pass](your-pass.md).
- **The taskbar**: **⊞ Start**, **▣ Mission**, **💬 Chats**, **🎟 Pass** (your
  QR, full screen, one tap — see [Your pass](your-pass.md)) and **📷 Scan**,
  on every screen, inside rooms too.

The tray's left edge is a **live dot**: green while you're connected. If
it changes colour (*reconnecting…*), you've dropped: the app keeps
trying by itself (a second at first, then every few seconds), so leave
it open. It catches up the moment the house is back, even after the
house goes dark for a while; coming back to the app, or onto Wi-Fi, tries
again at once. On the dark opening screen a **RECONNECTING…** strip
says the same after five seconds.

**Big moments can't be missed.** When something happens that is yours (you
won, you were found, you're invited somewhere), the whole screen becomes
it, with one button to carry on after a moment. See
[Notifications](notifications.md).

## One rule

The story is happening *in the room*, not just on the screen. When a
host, Clippy or an Antivirus talks to you, talk back. When the app says something is
happening somewhere — go.
