Some stories have **talk scenes**: moments when a performer talks out loud
with a character a machine voices, in front of the whole room, and the
character answers live. At *Trapped in the Internet* there are four, where
Clippy talks with Trabolta in The Desktop: right after he is introduced
(until the hunt), between the bottle's spins, at the ad attack (until the
second hunt) and at the beans (until the zip-out or his overshare). While
one is on, every host's **Show** window carries a **🤖 Talk** card between
NEXT and the status line. The rest of hosting is in [Hosting the
night](hosts.md).

**Task Manager relays.** Task Manager's card is open: they stand near
Clippy and type what Clippy says. Every other host sees the card folded,
*Task Manager is relaying Clippy*, with **Open**: if that phone dies, any
host opens it and carries on. While Show is off screen the slim NEXT bar
shows a **🤖 Talk** chip; tap it to go to the card.

## The card, top to bottom

- **The scene** (*Trabolta comes online*, *Truth or Dare*, *The ad
  attack*, *Full of beans*) and a line on what it is for.
- **His state**: *👂 Trabolta is listening*, *🤖 thinking… 5s* (from 12
  seconds: *— tap one of his lines*), *⚠ No mind online — use his canned
  lines*, or *💤 Trabolta is asleep*.
- **The room's last few lines**: what Clippy and Trabolta just said in The
  Desktop.
- **What did Clippy just say?** Type it in a few words and tap **Send**
  (Enter sends too; Shift+Enter is a new line). It goes in as Clippy's line
  in The Desktop, and Trabolta answers out loud a few seconds later: on the
  TV and on every phone. The TV never says Clippy's line: Clippy just said
  it. You can type on while a line is on its way; only what was sent leaves
  the box.
- **Clippy says (tap = send)**: Clippy's canned lines. One tap sends one,
  exactly like typing it. The last one or two (**➡ …**) hand the room on:
  they also tell Trabolta to wrap up in a sentence or two.
- **Trabolta says (tap twice)**: his canned lines. The first tap arms
  (*Tap again to fire*), the second posts his line at once, as his answer.
  Use them when he is slow, when there is no mind online, or when he goes
  off the rails. An arm never carries over into the next scene.
- **⚡ Glitch** and the console's **NEXT** (ARM, then FIRE, the same as
  Show's).

## Moving the night on

When Clippy hands off, fire NEXT from the card. The cue that ends a scene
(the hunt, the second hunt, the zip-out, his overshare) closes the card by
itself; a spin of the bottle keeps its scene open, and the ad attack turns
the bottle's card into its own. An ending or a skip closes it too.

## ⚡ Glitch

Only during a talk scene. Before the ad attack it flickers the screens. From
the ad attack on it is an **ad burst**: a short advert tears through every
screen and phone with one of Trabolta's pained lines. Two seconds apart at
least (*⚡ Too soon* in `# hosts`), and not while the ad attack's own big
takeover is still playing, its first 8 seconds (*⚡ The ad hijack is still
playing* in `# hosts`): wait, then tap again. The scenes also glitch on their own
about every 45 seconds, so you never have to press it.

## When something goes wrong

- **He doesn't answer.** The card counts the seconds; past 12 it says so.
  Tap one of his lines twice. `# hosts` also says *🤖 Trabolta isn't
  answering — tap one of his canned lines*. The room never hears a
  waiting line from him.
- **His answer comes late.** An answer that arrives after the scene moved
  on is dropped (`# hosts` says so); nobody hears it.
- **The server restarted mid-scene.** The answer he owed is forgotten. Send
  Clippy's line again, or fire one of his.
- **A send is refused**, and the words stay in the box with the reason:
  *The talk scene is over — nothing was sent.* (the scene closed while you
  typed) · *Trabolta is asleep — nothing was sent.* · *The night is paused
  or the doors aren't open — nothing was sent.* (the night is paused, or
  hasn't started) · *Too long* (keep it under 280 characters).
