Some of the best rooms don't show up until someone lets you in.

## Getting in

You'll know you've been invited when a new room appears in your inbox.
Invitations come from other guests, from characters, or from the story
itself deciding you've earned a door.

## Inviting others

In a private room you belong to, use **＋ Invite** and pick a person
from the roster. Choose wisely — a room is only as secret as its
loosest member.

## Leaving

**🚪 Leave** removes you from a private room. You stop seeing it; the
room keeps going without you. (You can be invited back.)

## DMs

A direct message thread opens when a character (or the story) starts
one with you. Reply right there — it's just you and them.
