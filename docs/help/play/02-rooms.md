Conversations come in a few flavours, grouped into **spaces** (the
titled sections of your inbox):

| Room | Is |
|---|---|
| **The lobby / feed** | the main broadcast (named after the story) — narration and announcements everyone sees |
| **# channels** | chatrooms — some open to all, some invite-only |
| **Group rooms** | your side's private channel — one per public group in the story; only members see it |
| **Location rooms** | tied to real places at the event — what happens *there* is heard *there* |
| **Direct messages** | one-on-one threads — characters may slide into your DMs |
| **📜 Quest Log** · **📓 Codex** · **🪙 Wallet** | pinned at the top: what the story has asked of you, what you know, and (when the story has money) what you have — yours alone |

Some rooms carry a **pinned line** above the conversation — a standing
notice from the story that stays until it's cleared.

## Rooms appear as the night opens up

You won't see every room at once. The story adds rooms to your list as
it opens them — a place appears once you've been there or the story
lets you in, and a chatroom when the story says so ("📂 … is open now.").
Characters who answer for themselves (a machine voice, say) show up as a
direct message the moment they're around.

## Room rules

- An **open** room: anyone can read and post.
- A **private** or **group** room: you see it once you're invited;
  members can invite others and leave.
- A **read-only** room (announcements): the story posts; you can't.
- Some rooms are **slowed** (wait between messages) or **ephemeral**
  (messages evaporate).

## Location rooms

Some rooms are places. When you're standing in a location, you can hear
(and post to) its room — walk away and the conversation stays behind.
If you can't post somewhere, you're probably not *there*.

The list tells you where everyone is: the place you're standing in is
first, tagged **you are here**; every other place says who's in it.
Empty rooms are dimmed. Inside a room, the 👥 count in the header opens
the list of who's here — tap a name to message them.

## Checking in at a room's sign

Some rooms have a sign with a QR code: **Scan to enter this room's
chat**. Tap **📷** in the app and point it at the sign, and you're
checked in: you're standing in that room and its chat opens. (Your
phone's own camera works too if it opens the same browser you joined
in; if it opens another one, the page names the room and tells you to
scan it with the app's 📷 instead.) It won't work while you're captured or
**restricted** by the story (the story has to lift that first — see
[Your pass](your-pass.md)), before you've finished getting in, or for a
room the story hasn't opened for you yet; and one check-in every 20
seconds.
