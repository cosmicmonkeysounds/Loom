## Sending a message

Type in the box at the bottom of any room and hit **Enter** to send
(`Shift+Enter` makes a new line). If there's no box, the room is
read-only — or you're not in the right place to speak.

If a line can't go, it stays in the box and a banner says why: *⏳ Not
sent* (you're sending too fast; wait), *⏸ Not sent: the show is paused*,
or *⛔ Not sent* with the reason. A message is at most 2,000
characters: the box stops there, and a longer one pasted in is cut off
at 2,000, so check the end of it before you send. If the house doesn't answer within 15
seconds (a phone switching networks), **Send** comes back and the app
says to check your connection: if your line doesn't show up in the room,
send it again. Each room keeps its own unsent words, so switching rooms
never moves them.

What you type is part of the show. Performers and the operator really
do read it, and the story can react.

## Threads

Any message can grow a **thread** — a side conversation hanging off it,
like a forum reply chain. Tap a message's reply affordance to open its
thread, read the back-and-forth, and answer inside it without flooding
the main room.

## Who's talking?

- **Names in bold** — guests and characters speaking.
- **The Narrator** — the story itself, setting the scene.
- **Dim system notices** — the machinery of the event (joins, moves,
  and the like).

Messages from the same sender in a row group under one banner, just
like your usual chat app.
