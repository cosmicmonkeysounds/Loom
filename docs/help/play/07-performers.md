The performer station is the same chat surface, recomposed for the
booth: every guest is a conversation, and your character is the mask.

## Signing in

Pick **I'm a Performer** and enter the **performer passcode** from the
host (a scanned link fills it in). The story's **parts** appear — every
character with something to do, with their pictures — tap yours. A part
is one booth's: a greyed-out name is already taken by another
performer. You now speak *as* that character everywhere.

Phone died, or you need to swap to a loaner? Your part is still held by
the old phone, so ask the director to **release** it (Run → the
character's Inspector → **⏏ Release**). Then sign in on the new phone
with the performer code and tap your part again: every guest thread and
button is where you left it.

## Scanning guests

The pinned **Scanner** thread is your instrument:

- **📷 Use camera** — point it at a guest's QR pass. Works on iPhone
  and Android; the camera stays on, so a queue scans pass after pass
  (each one shows a ✓ with the guest's id). It needs the https address.
- Or type their guest id (it's under their QR).

A scan fires your character's story for that guest — the **Readouts**
card shows what the system delivered, and anything the story wants you
to *say or do* arrives as your direction. You can also **📡 Scan this
guest** from inside their thread.

## Alerts in your booth

Your booth chimes, buzzes and shows a banner for every **alert** the
story or the director sends, and for any readout you didn't ask for —
"⏰ *Name* is late", a private warning. Tap the app once after signing in
so it may make sound (iPhones never buzz — watch for the banner). Keep
the phone awake with **☰ → Keep my screen on**; a banner you slept
through is still there when you wake it.

On an Android phone, tap **🔔 Get alerts → Turn on** (or **☰ → Get
alerts on this phone**) and allow notifications: alerts, the door alarm
and the late list then reach you as notifications even with the phone
locked in your pocket. iPhones can't do this yet: keep the screen on.

## Judging filed facts

Guests can hand a character an answer on a **📝 File a fact** card
instead of saying it (see [Filing a fact](quests.md)). A story that
uses it posts each filing where the hosts can see it — at *Trapped in
the Internet*, the `# hosts` room: "📝 *Name* filed H5 (round 2 Q2):
*answer*". Judge it from that guest's card with the story's buttons
(there: **📝 Filed fact: accurate** / **📝 Filed fact: confident
lie**). The guest gets the same reply either way — only the story knows
which you pressed.

## Guest threads and the guest card

Each guest is a thread under **Guests**. What you type there lands as
your character's DM to them. The **broadcast feed** thread posts to the
main room — everyone hears it.

Everything you can *do to* a guest lives on their **card**: open it with
**⚡ Actions** at the top of their thread, by tapping their name in any
room, or from a room's 👥 who's-here list. The card shows where they
are and their group, and holds **📡 Scan**, **📓 Share lore…**, and the
**story's own buttons** — whatever the writers declared for performers
(a whisper, a blessing, a search…). Pressing one acts *as your
character*, on that guest. The card leads with **your** buttons that
make sense right now; the rest (another character's, or one for a later
part of the night) fold under **More…** — still there if you need them.
Rooms themselves stay clean: just the conversation and your composer.

## Rooms

Every place is a room, and the list says who's standing in each — the
same view the guests have, minus the "you are here" (a character is
everywhere). Authored channels work like the guest app: post as your
character, invite guests into gated rooms, leave when the scene's done.
Lines the story sends to one guest privately (their arrival, a whisper)
are theirs alone; you see only what your character says or what plays
to a room.

## Moderator powers

If the host gave you the **moderator passcode**, open **☰ → Become an
admin**. Admins additionally can:

- **🔒 Capture / 🔓 Release** any guest from their thread (for stories
  that use it).
- **Hide/show** any message in any thread (moderation).

Use it like stage management, not like power — the story's operator can
see everything you do.
