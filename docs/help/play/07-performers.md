The performer station is the same app, recomposed for the booth: your
character is the mask, the guest in front of you is one scan away, and
the story tells you what this part of the night needs from you.

There are three kinds of part. A **player** (at *Trapped in the
Internet*: Clippy and the four Antivirus) plays the night like a guest
does, with a **Mission** home and a score: see **Playing a part**
just below. A **host** (Task Manager, Adobe Acrobat, the Recycling Bin)
runs the night from the Show window ([Hosting the night](hosts.md)).
Any other part is a plain performer.

A host's or a plain performer's booth, top to bottom: the **NOW card**
(your current objective), your rooms, and the **taskbar**: **⊞ Start**,
**📷 Scan** (the biggest button), **🗂 Guests**, and the tray (the act,
the soonest countdown, a red **🚨 003** counter while programs are wanted,
and **🔔 n** for pop-outs waiting on you). A host's booth also has a NEXT
bar above the taskbar; a story may give a plain performer cues of its own
to call on a **🎤 cue bar** (below).

Since wave 18 you **scan much less**. What needs you **pops out** over your
rooms with a picture, a name, where they are and the buttons you need: a
marked program to find, an arrival at the door, your cue, the Bingo
results. Scans are for two things: clearing a program at the door, and
confronting a marked one.

## Signing in

Scan your **staff badge** with your phone's camera: it signs you straight
in as your part (each badge is one part's). Do it from a phone that isn't
signed in as a guest. Typing works too — pick
**I'm a Performer**, enter the **performer passcode** from the host, and
the story's **parts** appear — every character with something to do,
with their pictures — tap yours. A part is one booth's: a greyed-out
name is already taken by another performer. You now speak *as* that
character everywhere.

Moving to another phone? **⊞ Start → Sign out** on the old one, then scan
your badge on the new one. Phone died, or you can't reach it? **Scan your
badge with the new phone**: a part is one phone's, so it says your part is
already signed in. Tap **🙋 Ask a host**, and when a host lets you in the old
phone is signed out and everything is where you left it. (With the passcode:
tap your part, which reads *taken*, and ask the same way.) A host or the
director can also **release** the part first (a host's console → More →
Performer parts → **⏏ Release**, or Run → the character's Inspector); then
your badge signs in the plain way. Keep it on you, QR side in: while your part
is free, anyone who scans it signs in as you. A booth signed out this way
(or by the show going live) says *"Your booth was signed out (your part
signed in on another phone, a director released it, or the show went
live) — sign in again with your passcode or your badge."* A phone that wakes late
in the night gets back every readout, every guest's message, your cast
thread and every card still waiting; only the busy rooms are cut to
their last few hundred lines.

## Your upload

Everyone uploads, you too. The first time you sign in, your booth is nothing
but the same dark stage the guests get: the security check, Task Manager's
form, the deposit box, the Terms of Service, then three questions (video,
your phone's voice, your inbox). Nobody scans you in: you leave by the staff
door, and **Continue** opens your booth. It takes a couple of minutes, so **do
it before the doors open**. If your part changes phones it does not ask again.

Tapped the wrong part? **Not you?** at the top, tapped twice, signs you out.
Phone died halfway, or a guest is waiting on you? A host or the director can
press **⏭ Wave the staff through their upload**, and you go straight to your
booth.

## Playing a part

A player's booth looks like a guest's phone. Across the top, the title
bar reads **▣ MISSION** (or your objective, when you are in a room). At the
bottom, the taskbar: **⊞ Start**, **▣ Mission**, **💬 Chats** and **📷 Scan**.
There is no Guests list, no NOW card, no Show or Desk window and no cue bar: you
play the game, the hosts and the directors call it.

- **Mission** is home. The card says what the night wants from you now:
  its title, where (📍 *Go to The Desktop*), the countdown, the to-dos with
  a progress bar, and up to three **big buttons**. Each big button does
  something whole: it opens the list of programs to work through
  (PLAYER SELECT, below), the card of the one program it is about, does
  the thing at once (a button about nobody in particular, with two taps
  when there is no way back), or opens the scanner captioned *Scan a
  program to …* so you scan the right person. Under the card: **Your move**
  (a card waiting for you, a program who wrote to you), your **score**
  tiles (*⭐ 120 Points*), and the **Quest Log**, **Codex**, **Wallet** (the
  auditor) and **🚨 Wanted** (the Antivirus) tiles; **Also now** folds any
  other live objectives.
- **Your score** shows live on Mission and as the first chip in the tray.
  The story keeps other numbers about you to itself, as it does for the
  guests; some of them are read out at the ceremony.
- **NEW MISSION**: when your objective changes while Mission is on
  screen, the title bar blinks *NEW MISSION · …* with a chime: the card
  under it is the instruction. Anywhere else (Chats, a room) a pop-out
  says so with its brief, **▣ Go** opens Mission, and it goes away by
  itself after about 20 seconds. A new quest never balloons: the Quest
  Log's dot says it.
- **YOUR GAME IS ON** (Clippy): when one of your games starts, it is one
  pop-out with **▣ Go to Mission**, after the pop-out naming the program
  the game landed on, and it leaves by itself after a minute. When you
  press 🎱 on that program, **PLAYER SELECT** opens straight away.
- **Chats**: **Your move**, **Messages** (📋 **Notes**, which is what the
  story tells your character, your cast threads, programs who wrote to
  you), **Team** (the staff rooms of your team: the Antivirus keep their
  room), **Rooms**, and **Other rooms (n)** with the 📜 Chat log last.
- **⊞ Start**: Help, Keep my screen on, Get alerts, **👥 People** (every
  program online, searchable, with where they are; the Antivirus also see
  who is MARKED or IN THE INTERNET, phone or no phone; tap one for their
  card) and Sign out.
- A press the story turns down reads **Not now.** You never see the staff
  room's reasons.

## The NOW card

The card pinned at the top is what the story wants from your character
right now: *NOW: Door duty: scan every pass in the Optical Drive · 1/2*.
The title gets two lines, and up to three of its to-dos sit under it,
always in view. A to-do the story tracks shows a grey check box that
ticks itself (you can't tap it); one the story can't see is a white box
you tick yourself (the tick stays on this phone). The ▾ button drops the
rest: the brief, any more to-dos, which buttons the objective puts on a
guest's card, and **More objectives (n)** when more than one is live. It
folds again when you tap elsewhere or open a room. With nothing live it
shows your character. The story moves it on by itself as the night
goes: there is nothing to dismiss.

Under the checklist the card can carry **one extra row**: **🚨 Wanted board
(n)** while programs are marked, a picture of the **one program** the
objective is about (the bottle's), **🎮 PLAYER SELECT (n)** or **📋 a list
(n)** of the programs to work through, or one or two buttons that act on
no particular guest. Tap it.

## Reading out loud

When the story has words for you to say, your whole phone turns into **📜 READ
THIS OUT LOUD**, with a sound. It can't be closed: it stays until you have read
them. Tap it for the teleprompter.

- **Your lines** are big and white. Small grey lines are cues: what someone else
  says, or what happens in the room. Yellow marks the reading line.
- **▶ Scroll** moves the words for you, and **− / +** or the slider set the
  speed. A finger on the words holds them. Or just scroll by hand.
- **The strip down the right edge** is the whole script. Drag it to preview a
  line and let go to jump there. **↩** takes you back to where you were.
- **≡ Jump** lists every section, with NEW on the ones you haven't read. **⏮ ⏭**
  go a section at a time and **↶ ↷** a line at a time.
- **A− A+** change the size. Your speed and size stay on this phone.
- **✓ Done** when you finish. Before the last line it asks you to tap twice.

✕ only goes back to the pop-up. New lines join the end of the script, so a line
landing while you read shows **＋ new lines**, and a line after ✓ Done pops up
again. To read anything again: ⊞ Start → **📜 My script**. The screen stays on
while the teleprompter is open.

## Pop-outs

A pop-out is a small Win95 window docked over your rooms: its title (*🚨
WANTED*, *🚪 At the door*, *🎤 Your cue*), the program's picture and name
when it's about one, and its buttons. **One at a time**: the next waits
behind it (*1 of 2*). Press one of its buttons, or **Later**, which puts
it in the tray's **🔔 n**: tap that for the **🔔 Waiting** window (*Nothing
waiting. You're on top of it.* when it's empty) and pick one to bring it
back.

- **Reminders go by themselves.** *WANTED · YOURS* leaves after about 15
  seconds (it stays on the 🚨 counter and the board's **Mine** tab), NEW
  MISSION after about 20, *YOUR GAME IS ON* after a minute, and *At the
  door* moves into 🔔 Waiting after 45 seconds untouched.
- **An alarm never waits behind a reminder.** A new 🚨 WANTED takes the
  screen from a reminder that has been up a couple of seconds; the
  reminder comes back after it.
- **Face to face, nothing floats.** While the scanner, a guest's card, a
  confrontation or PLAYER SELECT is open, nothing pops, floats or chimes
  over it. What arrived waits and shows (once, with its chime) when you
  close it.
- **One thing at the bottom.** A pop-out or a balloon, never both: a
  balloon waits while a pop-out is up, and only the newest one waits.

Pop-outs survive a reload, and one that no longer applies (the program
was caught, the arrival was cleared, the objective moved on) goes away by
itself. The censor's **🔑 LANGUAGE** pop-outs and its **🔑 n** count are
in [The language filter](language-filter.md).

## Scanning guests

Tap **📷 Scan**. The scanner fills the screen with the camera on, and a
box under it to type a guest's id (it's under their QR). Point it at a
guest's pass: **that guest's card opens at once**. Press what you need,
then **×** (or Escape, or your phone's Back): **one tap** takes you back
to where you opened the scanner: Mission, Show, a room, the Wanted board
or the Door queue. Your phone's Back steps back the same way everywhere: a
pop-out goes to 🔔 Waiting, a room closes, then Chats or the Desk goes back
to Mission or Show. It never leaves the app. At the door (the Antivirus), **📷 Next guest** brings the
camera back for the next pass instead; the camera stays on the whole
time the scanner is open, so a queue scans pass after pass. Anyone else
taps **📷 Scan** in the taskbar for the next one. The pass you just closed is ignored
for a few seconds, so it never reopens by itself, and a scan tapped twice
counts once. It needs the https address, on iPhone and Android.

A scan fires your character's story for that guest; what the story
answers shows on the card, and anything it wants you to *say or do*
arrives as your direction.

## Scanning codex QRs

**📷 Scan** reads a codex QR too: any QR a guest can read, on a wall, in a
fact stack or on a note, works for your character, the secret ones
included. The entry lands in your **📓 Codex**, a line under the camera says
so, and the camera stays on for the next one; **📓 Open my Codex** takes you
there. A QR held to the lens counts once. The box under the camera takes a
code as well as a guest's id. Your phone's own camera works too, on a phone
signed in as your booth: the link opens your Codex.

A wrong code costs your character nothing: you get the line and that's all.
Eight wrong codes in a minute and the scanner asks you to wait. A scan earns
no ⭐ and counts toward no award. While the doors are closed or the show is
paused it waits, as it does for everyone.

What you hold, **Share lore…** on a guest's card can pass on. That means
anything you hold, and what an entry does to a guest happens for real, so
mind what you hand over.

## The guest card

You also open a card from **🗂 Guests** (search by name or id; tabs
**Here** (pick the room), **Marked**, **Internet**, **Missing** and **All**;
each row shows one status tag, the guest's place first, and up to two loud
facts), by tapping a guest's name
in any room, or from a room's 👥 list. Top to bottom:

- **The facts** a performer acts on: in red *🔒 Captured*, marked and in
  the Internet; in yellow late, Bathroom Mode, ⛔ restricted; then where
  they are (📍). The story's bookkeeping (a gate they haven't passed, its
  other values) stays behind the scenes.
- **The face: at most three buttons**, the ones your objective needs and
  that make sense for this guest right now, each with what it does; the
  first wears a heavy frame. On a host's card a button the story offers
  only for some guests (🩹 Unmark on a marked program) joins the face on
  those guests' cards. Nothing else is listed: when the face is empty the
  card says *Nothing your objective needs on X right now* (a player's:
  *Nothing to do with X right now.*).
- **What the press came to**, on the card itself: the story's reply
  first (*✅ Cleared …*), with what your character said to them folded
  under **▸ What the story said** (tap to read it). A host's card also shows, in a
  refused style, any `# hosts` line saying why it was not done (*Not
  done: …*); a player's just says **Not now.** With nothing back a host
  reads *✔ Sent — no reply from the story*.
- **💬 Message**, **📓 Share lore…** and **📡 Scan again**.

Pressing a button acts *as your character*, on that guest, and the card
redraws with the story's answer. Right after the card changes, taps are
ignored for a moment, so a quick second tap never lands on a different
button. A button the story gives to **another** character only (the
server-room watch's patch-bay buttons, the Antivirus' ✅ / 👁) isn't
on your card at all: the server would refuse it from your booth (*not
your button …*). A host's booth is the exception for the ones the story
hears from any booth (at the show, the patch-bay buttons): see [Hosting the
night](hosts.md). Rooms themselves stay clean: just the conversation and
your composer.

**Some buttons take two taps.** A button with no way back — one that
ends the night, trips an alarm, hands over a secret or plays a
once-a-night moment — **arms** on the first tap (it turns black and
says *Tap again to fire*) and fires on the second. Tap again within
four seconds; a very fast double tap only arms it. Leave it and it
disarms by itself. The guest's own buttons in their Start menu work the
same way when the story marks them so.

## Readouts and messages

**Readouts** is a pinned room of everything the story tells your booth
alone: *⏰ X is late*, the answer to a scan, the story's notes to your
character (the urgent ones also pop out). Newest first; a readout about a guest has a **🪪 Name ›** button
that opens their card. Readouts survive a reload. A readout about the
guest whose card is open shows on the card instead of interrupting you.
You only hear about programs who are **yours** (your claim, the arrival
you took at the door): a quiet readout about anyone else (another
Antivirus' capture, someone's bathroom trip) lands in Readouts without a
balloon.

When a guest types to your character, their thread appears under
**Messages**, just below Readouts, until you've read it; a balloon by
the tray says so (its button opens the thread), and the 🗂 Guests button's badge
counts the guests waiting on an unread message. A guest's pick from a
story menu is not a message to you: no balloon, no badge. What you type in their
thread lands as your character's message to them. The **broadcast
feed** posts to the main room — everyone hears it.

## Alerts in your booth

The story sends alerts to everyone, to your character, or into a staff
room (`# hosts`). Your phone is about your job: **an alert to everyone
is not on your booth** (it plays on the TV and the speakers, and its
words are in the 📜 Chat log). One to your character or a staff room, and
any readout you didn't ask for — "⏰ *Name* is late", a private warning —
chimes and shows. A booth never takes the whole screen: a small one is a
**balloon tip** by the tray that goes away by itself (only the newest
waits, so a burst never queues up), and a bigger one is a yellow
**banner** that stays until you tap it, or about 45 seconds while you look
at it. A big moment for your booth is a **pop-out** (above), never a full
screen. While the scanner, a card, a confrontation or PLAYER SELECT is
open, balloons and banners wait until you close it. The story's glitch
never swallows a tap there either: over the scanner or a card it only
flickers. An alert the story sends to **one guest** or **one
room** (a guest's own heads-up, the lights going out in a room) doesn't
banner your booth: your cue is the readout. A `!` readout that needs you
jumps the queue and stays in **🔔 n** until you handle it. Tap the app once after signing in
so it may make sound (iPhones never buzz — watch for the banner).

Anything you put off, or missed while the phone slept, waits in the tray's
**🔔 n**. Keep the phone awake with **⊞ Start → Keep my screen on**.

On an Android phone, tap **🔔 Get alerts → Turn on** (or **⊞ Start → Get
alerts**) and allow notifications: house-wide alerts and alerts pinned
in a staff room, the server-room (patch-bay) alarm, the late list and a
transfer waiting for your approval then reach you as notifications
even with the phone locked in your pocket; tapping one opens where it
landed (a readout opens Readouts). An alert the story sends to **one
guest** — their own heads-up — never pushes your booth. On an iPhone, if the card shows **Get alerts on your iPhone**:
tap Share → **Add to Home Screen**, then **⊞ Start → Sign out** (that
frees your part) and sign in from the Home Screen icon (a part is one
phone's; a host's or the director's ⏏ Release frees one that's stuck),
and tap 🔔 there. If it says **Your iPhone can't get alerts**, keep the
screen on.

## The Wanted board (the Antivirus)

At *Trapped in the Internet* a host's verdict **marks** a program for
reinstallation. A mark **hinders nothing**: their phone keeps every
button and room. It's the Antivirus who banish them, and the hunt is
yours:

- **🚨 WANTED** pops out on every Antivirus booth: their picture, name,
  why (*named on the Bingo card*), 📍 where they are and *seen 2 min ago*,
  with **🙋 I'll get them**, **🗂 Open card** and **Later**. In Bathroom
  Mode it says *🚻 In Bathroom Mode: wait outside, never go in.* Several at
  once come as one *🚨 n programs marked* with **🚨 Open the Wanted board**.
- **🙋 I'll get them** claims them: your pop-out turns into *WANTED ·
  YOURS* with **📷 Scan their pass**, **Hand it back** and **Later**; the other booths
  see *🙋 McAfee Anti-Virus is on Minesweeper.* for a few seconds. If two of
  you claim at once, one reads *… got there first.*
- **The Wanted board** (the tray's 🚨 counter, or the NOW card's row): one
  poster per marked program, tabs **Open / Mine / Everyone**, kept across a
  reload. A poster you claim stays where it was on **Open**, tagged
  **YOURS** with **📷 Scan their pass**; one someone else claims stays too,
  tagged *🙋 … is on it*, so the list never jumps under your finger. A
  scan or a card opened from the board comes back to the board on ×.
  **Hand back** puts a poster back for everyone; on someone else's
  claim that has gone quiet for ten minutes, **Still on it? 🙋 Take over**.
  A caught poster is stamped **CAPTURED · CAUGHT BY …** for a minute, then
  leaves. A wanted guest's card carries a maroon **WANTED** banner with the
  claim. Hosts see the board read-only.

## The confrontation

The scan **is** the confrontation. Scan a marked program's pass and the
scanner turns into **⚠ CORRUPTION DETECTED**: their picture, why, and a
**SAY IT** box with your line, while their phone goes full screen (*… has
found you*). The big **🛡 SEND TO THE INTERNET** wakes up after a moment;
one tap and the screen stamps **CAPTURED** (*Walk them through the Tube to
the tent. Their phone shows them the way.*), with the board (and **📷 Next
guest** at the door); × goes back to where you opened the scanner (the
board, if that is where you started). From the capture until their reinstallation their phone is
**locked** (wave 26): only their pass and Bathroom Mode work, so walk
them there yourself ([How the house works](house-workings.md)). **Not now** keeps the mark and your claim and closes the scanner, back
where you opened it; **🩹 Not them…** (two
taps) lifts the mark. From a card's **📡 Scan again** (not in person) 🛡
takes two taps. A program someone else already caught says so (*Caught by
…*). Once the story opens it up (at the show: after the Bingo round), an
Antivirus' (or a host's) **🛡 Send to the Internet** is on **every**
program's card, marked or not, under *Marks or captures …: tap twice*.

## At the door

When a program finishes uploading, every Antivirus booth hears it, and
the four of you share the door. Scan their badge or the pass on their
phone: either confirms them. A host's scan confirms them too; Clippy's only
says hello. During the opening rush a **🚪 At the
door** pop-out appears only if nobody has had them for a minute; later, a
late arrival pops at once: *Winamp is waiting at the Optical Drive*, with
how long, **🙋 I've got Winamp** (the others see you have them, and your
scanner opens captioned *Scan Winamp's pass*) and **🚪 Door queue (n)**
(everyone waiting and who has them, with **📷 Scan the next pass**). A
card or a scan opened from the Door queue comes back to the queue on ×.
Left untouched for 45 seconds, the pop-out moves into **🔔 Waiting**; it
pops again when the next guest reaches the front of the queue (the one it
named was taken or let in), not for each new arrival behind them.

## PLAYER SELECT, lists and targets

Some objectives work through several guests with no scans:

- **🎮 PLAYER SELECT** (at the show, Clippy's Bingo results): every guest
  at hand as a picture tile, and the moves along the top (**① 🎱 ② 🎀 ③
  🎯 ④ 🤐**: ④ is the winner naming nobody at the centre square, which
  ends the round). Tap a tile, then the move's button; the next move offers
  the most recent earlier pick (**SAME: …**) when it's harmless, and a
  move with no way back never pre-selects and takes two taps. A guest
  picked earlier keeps their label but can still be picked for 🎯 (the
  winner may be the one who doesn't like AI). **🔍 Not here? Search
  everyone**, **Skip**, **Later**, then **+ Another round** while the
  objective is still open (never for a round that has ended). *PLAYER SELECT is ready* pops out
  when the game starts (on a player's booth, Mission's big button opens it
  too); it opens by itself right after a caller booth's own cue, and right
  after a player's own press on the program the game landed on (Clippy's
  🎱). While it is open nothing pops over it.
- **📋 A list** (Adobe Acrobat's Truth Pong cups, Clippy's cup-makers, the
  summons' **Still missing**): one row per guest with its place and up to two
  buttons (*🏓 Made* / *🏓 Missed · ×2*). A card opened from a row comes
  back to the list on ×. A pressed row greys with ✓ and
  leaves; *Done here* folds what you did. During the summons the guests
  check themselves in with the Desktop's QR, so **📍 They're here** is only
  for a dead or lost phone.
- **A target** (the bottle): a pop-out names the one guest it landed on,
  with the objective's buttons for them.

## The 🎤 cue bar

A story may let a part call some of its own cues (admin cues marked `by:
<Character>`). That booth gets a **🎤 cue bar** above the taskbar with
those cues on it. When one is the sheet's NEXT the band turns yellow (**🎤
YOUR CUE … ARM**) and a *🎤 Your cue · Call it?* pop-out asks; **🎤 Call
it** arms it, then tap **FIRE** on the bar within four seconds (*Not yet*
asks again in two minutes). Otherwise the grey band says what's **next for
you**. Its second line shows the sheet's NEXT and who is calling the show
(📣). **▴** lists all of the booth's cues. A host or the director may fire
them too. A **player** never calls cues and never gets the bar: at
*Trapped in the Internet* the hosts and the directors call Clippy's games,
and Clippy hears *NEW MISSION* when one starts.

## A guest who is restricted

A guest a director has **restricted** shows **⛔ RESTRICTED** on their
card and their row in 🗂 Guests: their phone is greyed out, its buttons
are off, they can't post in rooms or check in, and they keep only their
own feed, their private messages, their pinned rooms and the places the
story named. Their thread with you still works — talk to them there. (A
marked program is not restricted: they keep everything.)

## Judging filed facts

Guests hand a character an answer on a **📝 File a fact** card instead
of saying it (see [Filing a fact](quests.md)): a question, the answer,
and a confidence slider. The **server judges it** the moment it is
filed, against the story's answer key — *accurate*, a *confident lie*
(wrong at 100%), or *uncertain* (wrong below) — and the story reacts at
once; nothing waits for a host. That is so for an **exact match** (the
key, one of its aliases, an abbreviation like *Dr. Whiskers*). Anything
else is checked first. A **near miss** (*Dr Whiskars* for *Doctor
Whiskers*, a number written out, the right answer with a few more
words) is never judged a lie or a guess by itself: it is *proposed
accurate*, and a ⚠ line in `# hosts` names the answer it is close to.
At *Trapped in the Internet* Trabolta rules on **every answer that
isn't an exact match**, near miss or plain miss, when his laptop is
online: `# hosts` says *… asked Trabolta to rule* (*📝 Not an exact
match…* for a plain miss), then *🤖 Trabolta ruled …* with his verdict
and reason, and the story reacts as if a host had pressed. His rulings
wait in a queue and he takes one at a time, after any guest talking to
him, so a round can take him a few minutes. If his laptop is off
when the answer lands, a near miss waits for a person (you, with the
buttons below, a host's Judge tab, or the director's Judging card) and
a plain miss is judged at once; if he stops ruling (*⚖ Trabolta isn't
ruling* in `# hosts`), the ones he still holds wait for the round to
close. You only step in when you disagree. Whatever is still waiting when the
round closes takes its proposal: a near miss counts as accurate, a
plain miss as a lie at 100% or uncertain below. At *Trapped in the Internet* the `# hosts` room shows each
filing as it lands ("📝 *Name* filed H5 (round 2 Q2): *answer* —
certain. Judged lie."). The story's buttons on that guest's card —
**📝 Filed fact: accurate** / **confident lie** / **uncertain (a
guess)** — are the **override**: press one when the key is wrong or a
spelling was unfair, and the story takes your word over the judge's.
Your button judges that guest's **latest** filing. If they have filed
more than once and an earlier one is still waiting, *Trapped in the
Internet* turns your press down (*📝 Not judged: … has an earlier
filing still waiting for a verdict. Judge it on Run → Mind → Judging …*)
and changes nothing: ask a host, whose Judge tab lists each filing, or
the director. When judging is switched to *review*, every verdict is a
proposal until one of those buttons (or a host, or the director)
confirms it. The guest gets the same reply either way — only the story
knows what the verdict was.

## Your character's own rooms

Under **Pinned** your booth has the character's own **📜 Quest Log**
(the story can hand your character quests — a staff brief, step by
step, in its own thread each), its **📓 Codex** (what your character
knows, what you've scanned, and what you can share from a guest's card; on a host's or plain booth
**⊞ Start → What … knows** lists it too), and a **Wallet** when your character audits the story's
currency (below). The staff rooms, `# hosts` and `# quarantine` at the
show, sit just under them, marked *staff only*. Cards the story deals
to your character land in **Readouts**: answer one like a guest would —
a poll, a yes / no — and the story hears it *as your character*.

## Approving transfers

If your character is the story's **auditor** (at *Trapped in the
Internet*, CRA Agent Software), the tray shows **🧾 n** while transfers
wait on you (tap it to open your Wallet), and your **Wallet** room lists
them as cards: *🧾 Excel → Winamp: 40 Truthcoin. Waiting for your
approval* with **Approve** / **Deny**, or *settles in 42 s unless you
deny it* with **Deny** alone. Every third transfer anyone sends waits
for your Approve (or goes through by itself after five minutes, if you
never answer); every other one settles by itself at the end of its
window unless you deny it first. A denial is refunded to the sender and
the card says you did it. Trades of lore for coin never pass you; a
request someone pays does. A held transfer pushes your phone even when
it is locked (Android, with alerts on). Elaborate security questions are
encouraged; the story cannot freeze anyone's balance for you.

## Rooms

Every place is a room, and the list says who's standing in each — the
same view the guests have, minus the "you are here" (a character is
everywhere). The story's rooms are headed **Chats**. Empty rooms fold under
**Other rooms (n)**, and the room named after the story is the **📜 Chat
log**, always last there. Authored
channels work like the guest app: post as your character, invite guests
into gated rooms, leave when the scene's done. Lines the story sends to
one guest privately (their arrival, a whisper) are theirs alone; you see
only what your character says or what plays to a room.

## Moderator powers

If the host gave you the **moderator passcode**, open **⊞ Start → Add
moderator powers** (not on a player's booth), type it and tap **Become an admin**. Your booth
becomes a host's — Show, the NEXT bar and the Desk ([Hosting the
night](hosts.md)) — and additionally can:

- **🔒 Capture / 🔓 Release** any guest from their card (for stories
  that use it).
- **Hide/show** any message in any thread (moderation).
- Press another character's buttons, when the story hears the press.

Use it like stage management, not like power — the story's operator can
see everything you do.
