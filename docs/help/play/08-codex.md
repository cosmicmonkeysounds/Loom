Some stories run on **knowledge**. What you know is yours to keep — or to
trade.

## Your Codex

Your **📓 Codex** is a room, pinned at the top of your rooms list (just
under your 📜 Quest Log), and its line in the list shows how much of the
night's knowledge you've found ("5 of 43 found"). Inside, your lore is
filed like a cabinet: grouped under headings (*People*, *Places*, *The
story*, and whatever the story files its entries under), each entry one
line — its title, who or what it's about, and how you got it. Tap a line
to open it and read the whole entry; tap again to fold it. A new entry is
marked **NEW** until you open it. With four or more entries a box at the
top filters them as you type.

Lore reaches you three ways:

- **A code.** Tap **📷 Scan** (on the taskbar, or beside the Codex's
  message box) and point it at a QR code on a wall: it unlocks the entry
  and shows it in your Codex. Or type the code, in the box under the
  camera or in the Codex's message box (press **Unlock**, the way you'd
  send a message). Use the app's
  📷, not the phone's own camera: that may open another browser, where
  you aren't joined (it then shows the code in big letters to type in
  here). Codes don't care about capitals, dashes or spaces. A
  miss says so right there in the room.
  A wrong code is *noticed* by the story, so don't guess in front of
  anyone. Too many tries in a row and the box asks you to wait a minute.
- **The story.** Answer the right way, be scanned by the right person,
  finish the right puzzle.
- **A person.** Someone shares it with you. You'll see who.

## Sharing

Every entry has a **Share…** button when you open it. Pick a person and they now
hold it too — you keep your copy, and your Codex notes who you gave it
to. Sharing is permanent and the story sees it:
some characters *change* when they learn things. Share carefully. Some
characters (Trabolta, at *Trapped in the Internet*) won't take a secret
file, or lore about someone or somewhere else, so **Share…** leaves
them off the list for that entry. If a share is still turned down, the
app says *⛔ Not shared* with the reason, and nothing moves.

## People

Open **⊞ Start → People**. Everyone at the event is listed by name — guests and the
story's characters — and under each name is **exactly what you know of
them**: the entries about them you hold. Nothing more. Want to know more
about someone? Get them (or someone who knows them) to share.

## Private messages

From the People list, **💬 Message** opens a private thread with a guest
or a character. Only the two of you can read it (the operator can — it's
their show). A character's thread is answered by whoever is playing them
tonight. Some characters are not people at all, and still answer.

## Alerts and pop-ups

The story interrupts you when it matters. How loud it gets is the
story's choice, and it can change during the night; every screen
glitches for a moment when it does. From quiet to loud:

- **A balloon tip** pops up by the tray and goes away by itself.
- **A yellow banner** across the top stays until you tap it (×).
- **A dialog box** covers the screen with **OK**; more than one waits its
  turn (*1 of 3*).
- **The blue screen**: everything the story says to you takes over the
  screen in white on blue. Tap anywhere to continue.

The big moments (an announcement to everyone, a card that's for you
alone, a new objective that needs you) use the story's level; smaller
ones (a quest starting, a note just for you) come one step quieter. Most
have a button or two that take you where it's about: its room, a quest, your pass.
A short ✔ note after you press something is separate and never hides an
alert.

Tap the app once after you open it so it is allowed to make sound. If
your screen was off when something happened, or you closed it without
reading it, the tray counts it (**🔔 n**) and **everything** you missed
plays again, in order, when you wake the phone; more than three come as
one list (*You missed 5 moments*). Moments from an earlier part of the
night are skipped. Want to be sure?
**⊞ Start → Keep my screen on** stops the phone from sleeping while the
app is open. On an Android phone, tap **Turn on** in the **🔔 Get alerts**
balloon by the tray (or **⊞ Start → Get alerts**) and allow
notifications: alerts and messages from the characters then reach you
even when the phone is locked, and tapping one opens its room. Leaving
the event turns them off. On an iPhone the balloon says what works
there: if it shows **Get alerts on your iPhone**, tap Share → **Add to
Home Screen**, open the party from the new icon (you'll still be you)
and tap 🔔 there; if it says **Your iPhone can't get alerts**, keep the
app open with the screen on.

At the biggest moment of a night the whole screen can become the alert:
it flashes black and yellow with one instruction (*GO TO THE LIVING
ROOM*) and there's nothing to close. Go. The story takes it down when
the moment has passed (with Reduce Motion on, it doesn't flash).
