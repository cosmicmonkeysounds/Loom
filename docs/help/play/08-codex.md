Some stories run on **knowledge**. What you know is yours to keep — or to
trade.

## Your Codex

Your **📓 Codex** is a room, pinned at the top of your rooms list (just
under your 📜 Quest Log). Every piece of lore you hold is a card in it —
what it says, who it's about, and how you got it — and the room's line
in the list shows how much of the night's knowledge you've found
("5 of 43 found").

Lore reaches you three ways:

- **A code.** Tap **📷** (at the top of the app, or beside the Codex's
  message box) and point it at a QR code on a wall: it unlocks the entry
  and shows it in your Codex. Or type the code into the Codex's message
  box and press **Unlock**, the way you'd send a message. Use the app's
  📷, not the phone's own camera: that may open another browser, where
  you aren't joined (it then shows the code in big letters to type in
  here). Codes don't care about capitals, dashes or spaces. A
  miss says so right there in the room.
  A wrong code is *noticed* by the story, so don't guess in front of
  anyone. Too many tries in a row and the box asks you to wait a minute.
- **The story.** Answer the right way, be scanned by the right person,
  finish the right puzzle.
- **A person.** Someone shares it with you. You'll see who.

## Sharing

Every entry's card has a **Share…** button. Pick a person and they now
hold it too — you keep your copy, and your Codex notes who you gave it
to. Sharing is permanent and the story sees it:
some characters *change* when they learn things. Share carefully. Some
characters (Trabolta, at *Trapped in the Internet*) won't take lore
about someone else, or a secret file: the app says *⛔ Not shared* with
the reason, and nothing moves.

## People

Tap **👥**. Everyone at the event is listed by name — guests and the
story's characters — and under each name is **exactly what you know of
them**: the entries about them you hold. Nothing more. Want to know more
about someone? Get them (or someone who knows them) to share.

## Private messages

From the People list, **💬 Message** opens a private thread with a guest
or a character. Only the two of you can read it (the operator can — it's
their show). A character's thread is answered by whoever is playing them
tonight. Some characters are not people at all, and still answer.

## Alerts

Some announcements arrive as **alerts**: your phone chimes, buzzes, and a
banner appears. That's the story telling you to *move* — go where it
says.

Tap the app once after you open it so it is allowed to make sound. If
your screen was off when an alert went out, the banner is waiting when
you wake the phone (anything from the last 10 minutes). Want to be sure?
**☰ → Keep my screen on** stops the phone from sleeping while the app is
open. On an Android phone, tap **🔔 Get alerts → Turn on** (the card
above your rooms, or **☰ → Get alerts on this phone**) and allow
notifications: alerts and messages from the characters then reach you
even when the phone is locked, and tapping one opens its room. Leaving the event turns them off. On an
iPhone the card says what works there: if it shows **Get alerts on your
iPhone**, tap Share → **Add to Home Screen**, open the party from the new
icon (you'll still be you) and tap 🔔 there; if it says **Your iPhone
can't get alerts**, keep the app open with the screen on.

At the biggest moment of a night the whole screen can become the alert:
it flashes black and yellow with one instruction (*GO TO THE LIVING
ROOM*) and there's nothing to close. Go. The story takes it down when
the moment has passed (with Reduce Motion on, it doesn't flash).
