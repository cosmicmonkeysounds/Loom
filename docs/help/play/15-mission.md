Every screen of the app answers two questions at a glance: **what do I do
now**, and **where do I go**. The answer lives on **Mission**, your home.
You land on it when you join, after the opening, and every time you come
back to the app. The chat rooms are somewhere you *visit*
([Chats](rooms.md)), not where you live.

## The frame around everything

The phone is the old computer you're trapped in, so it looks like one.

- **The title bar** at the top. On Mission it reads **▣ MISSION**; anywhere
  else it reads **▣ NOW:** and the story's current objective (*▣ NOW: Computer
  Bingo ›*), with 📍 where you are under it. Tap it to come back to Mission.
  Your **picture** sits at its left end (tap it for [Me](your-pass.md)), and
  the **Recycle Bin** at its right end holds your [notifications](notifications.md)
  (it fills up, with a count, when something new is in it). While the show
  is paused the bar greys and says *(Paused)*; captured it turns red,
  restricted ochre. A badge the story gives you (🚻 in Bathroom Mode) sits
  beside **Mission** and beside your name on Me.
- **The taskbar** at the bottom, on every screen, inside rooms too: **⊞
  Start** (the story's own buttons such as 🚻 Bathroom Mode, Sides & crews
  (Factions when programs found their own),
  People, Notifications, Help, Leave the event), **▣ Mission**, **💬
  Chats**, **🎟 Pass** and **📷 Scan**. The button for the screen you're on
  looks pressed in. Mission shows **!** when there's a new objective since
  you last looked; Chats shows a yellow **●** when a chat is waiting on
  you (never a number). The button the objective needs (Pass, Scan) lights
  up and blinks twice. While you're typing the taskbar steps aside for the
  keyboard.
- **The tray**, the taskbar's right end: a **live dot** (green while you're
  connected; *reconnecting…* when you've dropped, and the app keeps trying
  by itself), the **show clock**, the story's **countdown** when it is
  counting down to something you should know about (*⏳ 4:59*, red in the
  last minute, ⏸ while paused), and your **🪙 balance** when the story has
  money. On a narrow phone the clock steps aside for the countdown.

## The mission card

The window in the middle is one card, the story's NOW:

- **The title**, big, with its emoji.
- **The Where row**: the one place it wants you. *📍 The Desktop ✓ YOU'RE
  HERE* (green) when you're already there; otherwise the whole row is a
  button, **📍 Go to The Desktop ›** (a room), **💬 Open Trabolta ›** (a
  character's thread), or a screen: **📷 Check in ›**, **🚪 Choose a side ›**,
  **🎟 Show my pass**.
- **The countdown** for this part of the night (*⏳ 18:42 left*).
- **The brief**, a few lines (**more ▾** opens the rest).
- **The checklist**, which ticks itself as you go, with a progress bar when
  there are two or more (*1 / 3*).
- **Big buttons**: what the objective needs pressed, at most three
  (**🎟 SHOW MY PASS**, **📷 SCAN A CODE**, or one of the story's own). When a
  card is waiting for your answer (a 📝 fact to file, a poll), it becomes the
  first big button: **📝 FILE A FACT · in Trabolta ›**. A button you can't
  take back takes two taps (the first arms it: *Tap again to fire*).

## Your move

Under the card, **YOUR MOVE** lists what is waiting on you, newest first,
one row each (at most four, then *+ n more in Chats ›*): a card someone
dealt you (*📊 Poll from Clippy · in Clippy ›*), a question the story is
asking you (its options right there), an invitation to join a side or
one of the crews, someone asking you for coin, a reply in a private chat.

## The desktop tiles

A teal strip of icons, like a 1999 desktop: **🪙 Wallet** (your balance),
**📜 Quest Log** (how many quests are active) and **📓 Codex** (how much
you've found). A yellow dot means something new inside; never a number.
They open your [Wallet](wallet.md), [Quest Log](quests.md) and
[Codex](codex.md). In a story where programs found factions, a **◆** tile
shows your own faction: *◆ The Night Shift · 7 of 13 · 6 more to win* (or
*over the line · hold on to them*: enough for now, but nothing is won
until the story counts); it opens [Factions](sides.md). Nobody else's count is
ever on it.

## Also now

When more than one objective is live, the others wait folded under **▸ Also
now (n)**. The story's catch-all one (*Explore the computer*) never counts:
it shows only when nothing else is going on, as **FREE ROAM · The house will
call you.**, with **👥 WHO'S HERE (n)** to see who's in the room with you.

## On a wide screen

On a tablet held sideways, Mission (or Chats)
sits on the left and an open room on the right; with none open it says
*Pick a chat, or follow your mission.*
