When a story names a censor (at *Trapped in the Internet*: the **Password
Manager**), that booth hears about the lines programs type with a swear in
them, mild ones too, even inside a bigger word. It is over-eager on
purpose: *password* is flagged for "ass", *hello* for "hell", *check* for
"heck", *button* for "butt". You decide what counts.

The filter starts the night at **full strength**: it catches everything,
even *hello* and *Password Manager*. It can be turned down somewhere on
your phone. Turned down, it only catches the worse words, and the lines it
no longer catches wait hidden until you turn it back up. It only ever
reads what the programs typed themselves.

## The 🔑 LANGUAGE pop-out

A new flagged line pops up as **🔑 LANGUAGE**: the program's picture and
name, where they said it (*in # lobby*, *DM to Trabolta*), and the line
with each flagged bit highlighted. The colour says how bad it is: red for
the worst words, orange for strong ones, yellow for mild ones, green for
soft ones like *hell*, and blue, purple or beige for a swear hiding inside
a bigger word.

A **slur** never shows: it is already starred out for everyone before
anyone sees the line, and your pop-out marks it as a black **[slur]**. It
pops up at any strength.

- **✂ Censor** takes two taps. The first arms it: it reads *✂ Censor? It
  raises their suspicion.* and *Tap again to fire*. The line is starred
  out for everyone who can see it (`p***word`), the program is told its
  message was censored, and its **suspicion** goes up on the Antivirus'
  👁 watchlist.
- **🛡 Send to the Internet** takes two taps as well. The first arms it:
  it reads *🛡 Send to the Internet? Their phone locks.* It is the same 🛡 as
  on a scanned card, with the same ⭐, and it only shows when that card would
  show it: on a marked program, or on anyone once the Bingo round is over.
  It leaves the line for you to judge: ✂ Censor still stars it out and still
  pays, and Let it go still clears it. It is gone after the ending.
- **Let it go** clears it from your phone. Nobody else is told, and the
  line stays as it was.
- **🔑 All flagged (n)** shows when more lines wait: it opens the filter
  window.
- **Later** (or the phone's Back) puts it in **🔔 Waiting**.

**One at a time**, so a flood stays calm: while a 🔑 pop-out is up, new
lines don't pop. They only add to the tray's count, and they never pop
later either. One you put in 🔔 Waiting doesn't hold the next back: the
next new line pops and takes its place (the old one is still on the list). A more important pop-out (a new
mission, your game) takes the screen from it, and it comes back after.

## ⭐ Stars for censoring

Every ✂ earns you ⭐ for your score. Each line shows what it is worth
(**⭐ 5**): worse words are worth more, and a slur the most. Under it,
the **heat** meter shows your multiplier: rested, a ✂ pays **×3**. Every
✂ heats the scissors up, and censoring every few seconds slides the
multiplier down to **×¼**. The bar cools back by itself (*×3 in 14 s*).
The ✂ button shows what it pays right now (**+15 ⭐**), and when it lands
the stars burst over your screen. A hot ✂ on a 1-point *hello* can pay
nothing at all. Patience, and the worse words, pay best. After the ending
✂ still censors but pays no stars.

## The filter window

The tray's **🔑 n** counts the lines still waiting for you. Tap it for the
window: *Censored 3 of 12 flagged words* (tonight, everyone's) and your
**⭐ tonight**, the heat meter, then every waiting line, oldest first,
each with its worth, **✂ Censor** (two taps), **Let it go** and, where it
can be used, **🛡 Send to the Internet** (two taps). When the
filter has been turned down, a note says how many waiting lines it is
hiding. Empty, it says *Nothing to judge.* While it is open no 🔑 pop-out
comes up: new lines just join the list. Close it with ×, or the phone's
Back.

## When ✂ doesn't go through

The reason shows under the line (on a pop-out, on its bottom line), and the
line stays so you can try again:

- *Not censored: the doors are closed.* The night is paused, or hasn't
  started.
- *Not censored: that line isn't flagged any more.* A director hid it.
- *Not censored: this part isn't the censor tonight.*
- *No answer. Try again.* or *No connection. Try again.*

A line someone already censored, or one the filter no longer catches at
its strength, just leaves the list.

## At the awards

Every censored word counts for the 🔑 Language Report at the awards: who
cursed the most, how many words were censored out of how many the filter
caught, every word you censored (a slur only as a black bar), and your
stars.
