When a story names a censor (at *Trapped in the Internet*: the **Password
Manager**), that booth hears about every line any program types with a
swear in it, mild ones too, even inside a bigger word. It is over-eager on
purpose: *password* is flagged for "ass", *hello* for "hell", *check* for
"heck", *button* for "butt". You decide what counts.

## The 🔑 LANGUAGE pop-out

A new flagged line pops up as **🔑 LANGUAGE**: the program's picture and
name, where they said it (*in # lobby*, *DM to Trabolta*), and the line
with each flagged bit highlighted.

- **✂ Censor** takes two taps (the first arms it: *Tap again to fire*).
  The line is starred out for everyone who can see it (`p***word`), and
  the program is **marked for reinstallation**: its poster lands on every
  Antivirus' Wanted board like any mark. After the ending it only counts.
- **Let it go** clears it from your phone. Nobody else is told, and the
  line stays as it was.
- **🔑 All flagged (n)** shows when more lines wait: it opens the filter
  window.
- **Later** (or the phone's Back) puts it in **🔔 Waiting**.

**One at a time**, so a flood stays calm: while a 🔑 pop-out is up, or
waiting in 🔔 Waiting, new lines don't pop. They only add to the tray's
count, and they never pop later either. A more important pop-out (a new
mission, your game) takes the screen from it, and it comes back after.

## The filter window

The tray's **🔑 n** counts the lines still waiting for you. Tap it for the
window: *Censored 3 of 12 flagged words* (tonight, everyone's), then every
waiting line, oldest first, each with **✂ Censor** (two taps) and **Let it
go**. Empty, it says *Nothing to judge.* While it is open no 🔑 pop-out
comes up: new lines just join the list. Close it with ×, or the phone's
Back.

## When ✂ doesn't go through

The reason shows under the line (on a pop-out, on its bottom line), and the
line stays so you can try again:

- *Not censored: the doors are closed.* The night is paused, or hasn't
  started.
- *Not censored: that line isn't flagged any more.* A director hid it.
- *Not censored: this part isn't the censor tonight.*
- *No answer. Try again.* or *No connection. Try again.*

A line someone already censored just leaves the list.

## At the awards

Every censored word counts for the 🔑 Language Report at the awards: who
cursed the most, how many words were censored out of how many were
flagged, and every word you censored.
