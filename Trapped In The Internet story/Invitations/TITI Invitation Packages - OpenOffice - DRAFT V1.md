# **Dana Anderson/OpenOffice: Character Background**

Dana Anderson is a middle-aged, retired FBI officer who moonlighted as an Accountant at the Barrington Street Urban Outfitters for more than a decade, with a focus on solving high financial crime in Nova Scotia.

Anderson joined the force with the intention of fighting corruption, bringing down the powerful – and eventually getting assigned to the *X Files*. Despite everyone saying this was not a real division of the force, Anderson held on to this hope for many years.

She eventually realised her incredible powers for information gathering were being abused to prosecute individuals and organisations she was actually morally aligned with, and she had to find a better way to put her talents to use.

After leaving the force three years ago, Anderson felt guilt for her role in certain investigations – finding evidence that incriminated whistleblowers, freedom of information activists, and environmentalists. 

She felt she had a due to pay to civilization, and turned her energy towards hacking the system.

Anderson was looking for cause. The hottest topic in Halifax over the past year was whether the Halifax Explosion Memorial Pet Cemetery should be reinvigorated, or whether it was a hopeless cause that should be paved over to make way for a condo.

Mayor Randy Barkmore Jr., hearing vicious opposition on both sides – the heritage lovers dedicated to the cemetery, the business class fighting for more condos – he decided he would satisfy everyone by choosing neither side and proceeding with a contract to put Nova Scotia’s AI Data Centre. When everyone complained, he said: “It’s out of my hands. It’s a provincial issue.”

Anderson knew that if she could single-handedly find a way to stop the Data Centre, her conscience would be clean. She saw the Data Centre as a major threat to the environment, and was concerned about how Barkmore would use it for surveillance.

So she enacted a master plan to hack Halifax Water, direct a water main to flood the soil of the site, and cause a sinkhole under the Data Centre on the morning of its opening.

The plan went all wrong when the sinkhole swallowed up Mayor Randy Barkmore Jr., arriving many hours earlier than anyone would have expected to the site, basically dancing inside his car with joy at the press that would show up five hours later, rehearsing his speech until the very last second.

The sinkhole is universally viewed as suspicious, and has attracted major international attention as the figurehead image in the ongoing debate on AI Data Centre construction.

This is way more attention than Anderson ever meant to get – the identity of the Halifax Sinkhole Hacker is the tabloid media’s number one priority. 

Anderson lives with immense guilt and paranoia. She infrequently leaves her home, in a gated swamp in Windsor Junction.

When she receives an email from Jean Trabolta, detailing her hack and threatening to expose her secret if she does not comply with his invitation to a computer-themed party – she understands she has no choice but to find out what’s really happening.

She reviews Trabolta’s invitation with great skepticism. Is this a sting? No, that would be entrapment. So who would be inviting her to a party with such an absurd concept, and not going directly to the authorities? How can she truly destroy the evidence of the hack that Trabolta claims to own? She sighs to herself and thinks about what an OpenOffice costume looks like as she sets her sight on extracting the truth, and leaving the party in one piece.
