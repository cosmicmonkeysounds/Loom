# **Tippi MacDonald: Character Background**

Tippi MacDonald was always a cheerful, friendly person, motivated by helping other people achieve their goals more than anything else – some would even call her a Pollyanna – until her embroilment in a Pet Insurance Fraud scandal called everything into question.

Upon graduating from the Nova Scotia Community College’s Office Administration program, Tippi MacDonald worked as an Office Assistant at a desperately underfunded nonprofit – the Halifax Explosion Memorial Veterinary Clinic (HEMVC).

Last December, the HEMVC was revealed by an investigator to be totally unlicensed, and neither a non-profit nor a veterinary clinic. 

It was a front for everything possible.

Tippi truly believed she spent her first five years out of school helping organise appointments to help animals, but all of the appointments she helped set up were fake, as part of a pet insurance fraud scheme.

Every time she felt satisfaction updating an Excel Workbook with a new macro, or building Word templates to make routine form completion easier – it all turned out to never be actually used by anyone.

Tippi has had a crisis of faith in humanity over the last year as a result of this experience. She also has the most scandalous operation in town as her only role on her CV, and burgeoning student debt. 

She has spent her time applying for new roles as an Office Assistant and is eager for an opportunity to prove that, in a scenario with real stakes, she can truly Assist an Office.

So when Tippi receives a letter from the owner of downtown Halifax business Maps and Ducks – a proprietor of both old print maps and decoy wooden ducks on Lower Water Street – saying he saw her resume on [Indeed.ca](http://Indeed.ca) and wants to see if she could be their new Office Assistant, she is over the moon.

Just one catch – the application for the job requires a test, on Halloween Night.

On Halloween Night, Tippi will need to join a virtual reality server for a philanthropic party. She will have a Lead Role at this event as an Office Assistant organizing fun, philanthropic party games. When she logs into the simulation, she will be given cues on how to keep the party on track and lead the party attendants through games.

For one night, Tippi must embody Clippit (popularly known as Clippy), the Microsoft Office Assistant from old copies of Microsoft Word. She must follow her instructions and persistently offer everyone at the party with Help. As much Help With That as possible.

If she succeeds, and the games go off without a hitch? A guaranteed role at a legitimate, respected, Halifax business. A chance at a new life where she can really be herself again.

If she fails? She fears she will become known in the tight-knit Halifax business community as ***Failed Clippy***, which could finally break her spirit.
