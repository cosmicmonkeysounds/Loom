# **Virgil Jeeves / Ask Jeeves: Character Background**

Virgil Jeeves feels he was destined to be a librarian.

Growing up in the late 90s, any question at school asked in his vicinity would be answered by his taunting classmates – “Let’s just Ask Jeeves\!”

And Jeeves would need to get the answer. His early familiarity with search engines and online information retrieval impressed his teachers, who, when stumped by a curious student, would yell to the back of the room, “Jeeves, I will ask you to Ask Jeeves\!” 

He would eagerly stand up, head to the class computer, and Ask Jeeves. He was proud to be of service – helping people find information they needed gave him meaning.

He begged his parents for a Nokia 9110 Communicator, the first cell phone with Internet, which he received at its launch in 1998 when he was nine years old with the stipulation that “it costs so much it will be the only Christmas present you get for the rest of your life.” He agreed, wanting it that badly.

Having a phone with Internet access meant he could *Always Ask Jeeves*, and as the only individual in any circumstance he found himself in with access to an infinite encyclopedia at his fingertips, he felt like a genius. He felt like he *Was Jeeves*. He mowed lawns, shoveled snow, and cleaned gutters to pay for data, so he could maintain his status as *Jeeves*.

With the revolution of the Blackberry and the iPhone in the mid-2000s, Jeeves started losing his edge, as other classmates started having their own means to find information on the fly. At the same time, [Ask.com](http://Ask.com) removed the cartoon butler Jeeves from their branding, and Virgil Jeeves felt like he was truly slipping into irrelevance.

By the mass adoption of smartphones in the 2010s, Jeeves pivoted to an undergraduate degree in Library Science, and consumed himself with studying all of the world’s facts for himself. If it was no longer impressive to pull up information on demand from a device you carry, it would be even more impressive to actually memorize facts, yourself. If he could win at *Jeopardy\!* and gain a reputation as a knowledgeable man, people would once again say the sweetest words to his ear – “*Let’s Ask Jeeves*.”

Jeeves found employment at Lawrencetown’s *Dr. Frank W. Morse Memorial Library* as a Children’s Librarian. Days went by slowly, which was perfect for Jeeves, as he spent the day memorizing the Encyclopedia Britannica and complaining on Reddit about how the selection process for *Jeopardy\!* is fraudulent because he has never heard back.

When the library was shut down due to provincial budget cuts in 2026, Jeeves was desolate. Technology has already taken away his edge in life, and now he struggles to find another role where he can have the downtime he needs to memorize the world’s facts while waiting for a *Jeopardy\!* callback. 

So when he receives an offer from Jean Trabolta to participate in a scavenger hunt where he will have the opportunity to prove his resourcefulness in factfinding – and even have the chance at a new full-time remote role that will surely allow him ample time to study for *Jeopardy\!* throughout the day – he is thrilled.

His only concern? Jean seems to highly stress that his power needs to stay on all night, and he needs a high upload speed. Virgil is on a grandfathered “Eastlink 10 MB/Minute” plan which is popular in Lawrencetown, and the power outages in his rural area are intermittent…surely it can’t be such a big problem.
