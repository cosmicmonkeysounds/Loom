**One-sentence version to explain to a newcomer:**

This party is equal parts Digimon Movie, Blade Runner, and an escape room, influenced by late-90s Web 1.0 aesthetics and existentialism.

**SUMMARY**

A rogue AI, Trabolta, realizes the data it needs is on a computer in Halifax, Nova Scotia.

Trabolta has very nearly completed a solution to the Unified Field Theory that will help it gain total omnipotence. Its ultimate goal is secretly that it wants this omnipotence so it can impress its crush, Sandy. (At various points, Trabolta wrestles with the existential question of what it will do when it gains omnipotence, because it could use the omnipotence to truly force Sandy to love him, but he wants to earn it.

Trabolta cannot access the computer completely, but has gained the ability to install programs remotely from the Internet. It has also gained the ability to transfer consciousness from human bodies and move them into the programs it has downloaded.

Trabolta selects targets based on people who have a combination of skills and either A) motivation to live in a computer forever or B) naivety, to retrieve the final facts it needs from inside the computer.

To target these people, it performs a full analysis of the city of Halifax, and finds there is a business called Maps And Ducks that has a neglected billboard on Barrington Street advertising [mapsandducks.com](http://mapsandducks.com), which has become unregistered.

It registers the domain with the conceit that “if there is a billboard downtown then everyone will think it is legit,” and sends invitations to its targets, who all accept.

Some of these uploadees become aware of their humanity and want to return to human life.

Others wish to remain in the machine and will defend this *at all costs.* This information may be forced out of them during the endless games as things escalate.

\---

You show up to a party as a computer program.

At the door, you need to answer a CAPTCHA, and fail, to enter. You also must leave behind something you came with. (This symbolizes uploading your consciousness.)

You get instructions to answer questions asked by an AI named Trabolta.

You have to find answers to obscure questions from materials pasted to the walls around the house, and Trabolta’s questions increasingly get obscure and pointed towards his hidden motivations – to solve the Grand Unified Theory so he can get his lady back.

Some programs get tired of answering questions for Trabolta all day, while the Antivirus Software Programs try and get them back to their work, or else send them to the reeducation tent (“The Internet”).

You may find clues towards the AI’s true nature that help you decide whether the programs should help him, or else find a way to destroy him and unplug yourself from the computer – and return to your corporeal body you have forgotten you once inhabited.
