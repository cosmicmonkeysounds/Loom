# **Maria Sophia Paine: Character Background**

Maria Sophia Paine has one primary value above all others: integrity.

Ever since she was left alone with a computer for the first time at age five, she became obsessed with MS Paint, and she knew what she wanted for the rest of her life – to keep drawing pictures in MS Paint.

So when her high school guidance counselor warned her that “no art school will pass you if you refuse to engage in any other medium than Paint – especially *Paint 98*,” she decided to hedge her bets and prove her guidance counselor wrong.

She was featured in *Wired Magazine* at age 13 and illustrated their first MS Paint cover for her revolutionary work illustrating elaborate abstract pieces mostly with the spray paint tool.

She applied and accepted entry to four prestigious undergraduate interdisciplinary programs simultaneously. She studied the terms of the programs and realised you don’t need to do years consecutively, as long as you complete the program in thirteen years.

So she committed herself to a 17-year plan for her formal education in art:

- 2010: Nova Scotia College of Art and Design (NSCAD),  Bachelor of Arts (BA) in Painting, Freshman Year  
- 2011: Yale School of Art, Bachelor of Arts (BA) in Painting, Freshman Year  
- 2012: The New School, Bachelor of Arts (BA) in Painting, Freshman Year  
- 2013: École Nationale Supérieure des Beaux-Arts de Paris, Diplôme national supérieur d’arts plastiques, Année 1  
- 2014: NSCAD, Sophomore Year  
- 2015: Yale, Sophomore Year  
- 2016: The New School, Sophomore Year  
- 2017: Beaux-Arts de Paris, Année 2  
- 2018: NSCAD, Junior Year  
- 2019: Yale, Junior Year  
- 2020: The New School, Junior Year  
- 2021: Beaux-Arts de Paris, Année 3  
- 2022: NSCAD, Senior Year (graduation)  
- 2023: Yale, Senior Year (graduation)  
- 2024: The New School, Senior Year (graduation)  
- 2025: Beaux-Arts de Paris, Année 1 of 2nd Cycle / phase projet  
- 2026: Beaux-Arts de Paris, Année 2 of 2nd Cycle (graduation)  
- 2027: Beaux-Arts de Paris, Année post-diplôme

But like many best-laid plans, it fell apart.

Over 2022 to 2024, while Maria Sophia planned to graduate with three BFAs in three years, her graduation thesis at each school was cancelled and she failed to graduate. 

The panelists refused to accept her controversial thesis, “MS Paint Is The Most Pure Art Tool: All Art Is Just Pixels If You Take A Picture And Zoom In Close Enough,” and she was left bereft.

Now at age 34 in the year 2026, Maria Sophia is within the final weeks of her combined bachelors and masters at Beaux-Arts de Paris. 

Her last chance at institutional acceptance, and she will need a major success to have the Parisien panel persuaded by her thesis.

She comes from a working class family struggling to make ends meet in Halifax’s gentrified North End, and as such, she is facing exactly \$1,000,000 in student loans. 

So when she receives a letter from Jean Trabolta offering her the opportunity to be paid \$1,000,000 to organize a collaborative MS Paint Painting, she sees the solution to all of her problems.

Could the offer be too good to be true? She has no choice but to find out. She is ready to transcend her role as MS Paint Artist, to *become MS Paint*. If she succeeds? Institutional recognition, her substantial debts cleared, and she can finally tell her guidance counselor to get lost after 17 years. If she fails? *Her retired guidance counsellor has told her he is just waiting to press publish on a YouTube video titled, “\$1,000,000 in student debt and four failed theses presentations for forgotten MS Paint artist”.*
