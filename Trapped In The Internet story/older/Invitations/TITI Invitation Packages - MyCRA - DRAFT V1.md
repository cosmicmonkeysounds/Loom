# **Sharon Penney / MyCRA: Character Background**

Sharon Penney was studying Office Administration at NSCC when she accepted an internship with the City of Halifax.

She expected to answer phones, organise appointments, and gain experience using Microsoft Excel in a professional environment. Basic tasks.

Within weeks, she was responsible for the entire finances of the City of Halifax.

The other staff had quit in protest of Mayor Randy Barkmore Jr.’s controversial plans to build the city’s first AI Data Centre on the site of the former Halifax Explosion Memorial Pet Cemetery.

Sharon had concerns as well, but she needed the placement hours to graduate.

Barkmore was very appreciative she stayed. He told her she had a bright future with the City, and started showing her how things *really* worked.

He explained that municipal salaries were modest, but there were ways to supplement them. 

If she arranged to collect 0.01 cents from every dollar the City spent, nobody would notice, and she would be rewarded for her hard work.

Sharon was uncomfortable with this, but he was the Mayor, and she was an intern. 

She assumed he knew what was permitted.

She also had tuition to pay.

Sharon made the necessary changes in Excel.

Unfortunately, she confused 0.01 cents with 0.01 dollars, and began transferring one cent from every dollar the City spent into her personal account.

She had established a 1% municipal tax payable on all transactions, directly to Sharon Penney.

She was caught within weeks.

By then, hundreds of thousands of dollars had arrived in her account. Sharon had spent some of it, believing this was the supplementary compensation the Mayor had explained.

The RCMP began investigating.

Then, in September, Randy Barkmore Jr. died in a suspicious sinkhole at the opening of the data centre.

Sharon was now being asked questions about the construction payments, the missing money, and whether she had anything to do with the sinkhole.

The person who had told her how to do her job was dead. Everyone else wanted to know why she had done it.

She worried she would never graduate, never find another job, and spend years in prison for following instructions during an educational placement.

Then she receives an email from Mark Carney.

He says his surveillance has confirmed she did not cause the sinkhole. He knows about the fraud, but wants to put her skills to better use.

He has seen *Suicide Squad* and is developing a similar opportunity for white-collar criminals in Canada to perform tasks to become free.

On Halloween night, Sharon must attend a secret fundraiser to rebuild the data centre. The other guests will believe they are raising money for a shop that sells maps and wooden ducks.

She must not correct them.

She will be *MyCRA, the embodied manifestation of the CRA’s online portal*, and approve or deny the guests’ transactions in a special digital currency.

If she succeeds, the Prime Minister will resolve her legal problems and find her a cool consulting job.

If she fails, he will deny having contacted her.

The email is coming from a map store rather than the Prime Minister’s office. He has explained that this is necessary to avoid having his correspondence FOIPOP’d.

On Halloween night, she will arrive prepared to demonstrate that she can be trusted with financial responsibility.

This time, she intends to check where every decimal goes.
