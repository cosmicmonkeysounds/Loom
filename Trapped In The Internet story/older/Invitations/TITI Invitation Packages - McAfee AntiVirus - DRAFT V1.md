# **John McAfee / McAfee Antivirus: Character Background**

John McAfee founded McAfee Associates in 1987, after working as a programmer for NASA, Xerox, and Lockheed. His antivirus software made him extraordinarily wealthy, and installed his name on millions of computers around the world.

He left the company in the 1990s, but his name remained. For decades afterward, people would contact him personally to complain about software he had nothing to do with.

John moved to Belize, where his activities attracted considerable attention from the authorities. In 2012, when police wanted to question him about the death of his neighbour, he fled to Guatemala, where he was arrested for entering illegally.

He later returned to the United States and twice sought the Libertarian nomination for President.

He was very concerned about government surveillance. He believed the government had no right to know what he was doing, where his money was, or how much of it he had. The government disagreed.

In October 2020, John was arrested in Spain on American tax evasion charges. In June 2021, after a court approved his extradition, he was found dead in his prison cell.

Or so the public believed.

The employee who signed his death certificate received enough money to purchase a Lamborghini, and John returned to Belize with a completely reconfigured image and a new business: Artículos Para Estanques Ornamentales Belize.

For five years, John has been quietly selling ornamental pond supplies, enjoying the freedom of being officially deceased.

He finds the work pleasant. Customers ask about pond liners, fountains, and decorative ducks. They do not ask about his taxes. He has taken considerable precautions to conceal his identity.

And he did not think anyone would find him through his pond supply shop.

Then he receives a letter from Jean Trabolta, proprietor of Maps And Ducks in Halifax, Nova Scotia.

Jean says he is bringing the world’s most abundant AI training dataset to market. Now that he has found John, the only remaining information he needs is obscure trivia about Halifax from the 1990s and 2000s.

He is gathering people in a virtual simulation to extract these facts, and needs John to *be McAfee Antivirus*.

John must ensure the other guests stay on track and complete the Scavenger Hunt.

If he succeeds, Jean will destroy the evidence of his whereabouts. If he fails, his identity will be included in the dataset.

John does not want to spend Halloween supervising a trivia fundraiser for a map store. But he has spent considerable money becoming dead, and cannot afford to have this undone.

He agrees to participate, while intending to learn as much as possible about Jean and where he keeps his information.

Jean has warned him that other antivirus software will be present to identify any threats John might deliberately overlook.

John would like to know which ones.

He has complaints about all of them.
