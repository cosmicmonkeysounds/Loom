# **Julie Horton-Norton / Norton Anti-Virus: Character Background**

The Halifax Explosion Memorial Pet Cemetery was a site that seemed to be haunted for so many reasons – the rumours of actual ghosts filling the city last year, the proposed development of the *Dalmatian* condo on its site thwarted at the last minute by protestors, only for Halifax’s Mayor Randy Barkmore Jr. to plow ahead with plans for an AI Data Centre to be developed on the site.

Before development began on the AI Data Centre, a citizens’ group determined to Save The Pet Cemetery built a guerilla installation on the site that they knew no government could ever survive the optics of demolishing – an adorable petting zoo full of miniature horses, with a miniature stable.

Julie Horton-Norton, an aspiring graduate of Dalhousie's Pre-Veterinary Medicine program, was hired by the citizens’ group to oversee the health of the petting zoo’s miniature horses. 

One morning last December when Horton-Norton was getting ready to administer the horses’ annual shots, she realised she had forgotten her textbooks on campus. 

She had heard AI was all the rage for answering any question, so she loaded up the free version of ChatGPT 4o and asked: “do i give miniature horse miniature doses of rabies vaccine” 

ChatGPT replied, “Absolutely\! Miniature horses need miniature rabies vaccine doses—otherwise you’d be giving a tiny horse a full-sized horse’s immunity. 🐴💉

A good rule of thumb is to ask yourself, “how much horse is this?” and scale accordingly. You know your little guy best\! ✨

And honestly, the fact you thought to ask? Your horse is lucky to have you. 💅”

The horses, failing to become immune to rabies, perished rapidly after a regular fight with Point Pleasant Park’s raccoons, and Horton-Norton attracted international bad press for her mismanagement of the Petting Zoo.

She has been laying low since. All she ever wanted was to help animals, and she lives with inner conflict every day – is it her fault those horses crossed the rainbow bridge, or is it the fault of the AI companies who would release such a reckless product so as to recommend “miniature doses for miniature horses”? 

After her incident, the petting zoo closed, and Mayor Randy Barkmore Jr. had an easy excuse to build an AI Data Centre on the site – “It will help us forget the horses, they’re not coming back, if you don’t think it should be there, that’s an issue for the province to settle.”

So when she gets an email from Jean Trabolta saying he found her CV for a position as Stable Manager on Indeed, she feels like she’s really getting back in the saddle.

Jean’s promise to completely change her public image with a donation to Sable Island conservation efforts is so great, Julie immediately starts reading the Wikipedia page for “Norton Antivirus” and taking notes.

She failed to fight viruses once before. It won’t happen again.
