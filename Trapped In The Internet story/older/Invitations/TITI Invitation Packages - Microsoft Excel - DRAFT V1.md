# **Randy Barkmore Sr./Microsoft Excel: Character Background**

Randy Barkmore Sr. is the 90-year-old former Mayor of Halifax, who served five terms from 1975 to 1990 before his son, Randy Barkmore Jr., became Mayor in 2008, straight out of university..

For decades, the Barkmores were the most important family in Halifax. Randy Sr. knew everyone, knew what everyone needed, and knew how to make sure money went to the right places.

He was very good at this until the end of his fifth term, when the ladies in finance started doing the books on computers.

With Microsoft Excel, they could follow the money through every account, and started asking questions about arrangements that had been working perfectly well for years.

Randy retired, but remained closely involved in his son’s career. He had considerable advice to offer, and plenty of time to make calls.

Then, in September 2026, Randy Jr. perished in a suspicious sinkhole just before the opening of Halifax’s first AI Data Centre, on the site of the former Halifax Explosion Memorial Pet Cemetery.

Randy Sr. was devastated.

He had expected his son to outlive him, carry on his work, and establish another generation of Barkmore leadership. 

Instead, he watched news reports of how his son passed away at 4 a.m. while he was “excitedly rehearsing a speech in his car” in the data centre parking lot, when his Escalade suddenly plummeted hundreds of meters into the earth.

The newspapers had many questions about the choice of site, the construction contracts, and what Randy Jr. had known beforehand.

Randy knew his son had been trying to do something good for Halifax. He also knew how these things could look when people put all of the numbers into Excel.

The Barkmore legacy was in serious trouble.

At 90, Randy found himself the only person left who could save it.

He began making plans to run for Mayor one more time. He would restore the family’s reputation, finish what his son had started, and make sure the Barkmores were remembered for everything they had contributed to Halifax.

But he was having difficulty getting through the day.

Appointments, medications, and exhaustion were taking up time he needed for his campaign. His doctors were concerned about his health. His friends were concerned about his plans.

Randy was concerned that he would die before he could do anything about what had happened.

He needed more time. He needed to be healthy. If possible, he needed to live forever.

So when he receives a letter from wealthy Halifax business owner Jean Trabolta, saying he has discovered the secret to everlasting life and wants Randy to be the permanent Mayor of the Metaverse, Randy sees an opportunity.

Jean says he has converted a residential property into a machine where people can leave their old lives behind and live forever in a new world.

He is bringing in the first group on Halloween night, and Randy will be the Guest of Honour.

Randy is accustomed to being invited to events as Guest of Honour, but recently these invitations have mostly involved being handed some kind of plaque and being helped back to his car.

This time, he will have a Job again.

Jean wants him to lead the new world, permanently. He says everyone wishes Randy were Mayor again, and that Randy is the person who most deserves to live forever.

Randy finds it reassuring that a successful small business owner has independently arrived at these conclusions.

There is one requirement – the new world is computer-themed, and Randy must be Microsoft Excel.

He remembers Excel as the program with all of the numbers on it. He does not know what being Excel will involve, but Jean says to do his best, and that everyone will love it.

Randy is willing to do this. He has spent enough time dealing with medical conditions to welcome a condition he can satisfy by wearing something with a gridded pattern and yelling the word “spreadsheet.”

On Halloween night, he will arrive prepared to meet the people he will be governing for eternity.

He wants to hear about their lives, what they hope to accomplish in the new world, and how he can help them get settled. He has decades of experience convincing people that Halifax is somewhere they should stay.

He expects this will be useful.

Most importantly, Randy understands that this is a real opportunity to live forever. He is making preparations on that basis.

If everything goes as Jean promises, he will have enough time to restore the Barkmore name, and enough time afterward to enjoy having restored it … forever.
