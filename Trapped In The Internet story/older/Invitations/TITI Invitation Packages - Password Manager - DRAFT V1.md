# **Bridget Troelsen / Password Manager: Character Background**

Bridget Troelsen has wanted to hold public office ever since she went to school with Randy Barkmore Jr., who made sure she was excluded from everything.

Randy was popular, his father was the Mayor, and whenever Bridget complained about how she was treated, she was told to try harder to get along with people.

Randy was always a snotty brat to Bridget who believed she represented the common person. She always prided herself on her neatness, organisation, and good manners. 

So when Randy won their Grade Six class vote to become responsible for taking care of class pet Hamtaro the Hamster over the summer, and then Hamtaro never came back to school and Bridget got in trouble for incessantly asking what happened to Hamtaro, Bridget dedicated herself to finding power.

Becoming a leader would give Bridget the ability to ensure common people find justice. She felt it would be easier to get along with people if she had some authority over them.

Bridget has the following electoral history:

- 1997: Oxford Elementary School Grade 6-P, Summer Hamster Keeper \- Lost   
- 1998: Oxford Junior High School Student Council, President \- Lost  
- 1999: Oxford Junior High School Student Council, Secretary \- Lost  
- 2000: Oxford Junior High School Student Council, Treasurer \- Lost  
- 2001: QEII High School Student Council, President \- Lost  
- 2002: QEII High School Student Council, Secretary \- Lost  
- 2003: QEII High School Student Council, Treasurer \- Lost  
- 2004: Dalhousie University Student Union, President \- Lost

By 2008, Randy Barkmore Jr. had become Mayor, and Bridget was finding it difficult to watch someone who had made her school years miserable continue to be rewarded with authority.

She had spent years preparing platforms, printing signs, and explaining what she would do if only given a chance.

Then, last year, she was appointed moderator of the *Halifax Dartmouth Confessions 3* Facebook group.

Finally, Bridget had a position where she could make decisions without asking the general public to vote on whether she should be allowed.

The group had survived two previous versions, both of which ended in considerable drama. Bridget intended to establish a more responsible environment for local gossip.

She spent hours each day reviewing posts, investigating complaints, removing spam, and explaining why certain contributions were inappropriate.

There were frequent accusations about the Barkmores and the proposed AI Data Centre. Bridget had her own concerns about Randy, but she understood that being a moderator required impartiality.

She removed claims that could not be substantiated.

Over time, the group became much more civil. Members increasingly thanked her for her hard work, praised her judgment, and said Halifax needed more people like her.

Bridget felt she had finally found a constituency that understood what she had to offer.

She considered another campaign.

Then she receives a letter from Jean Trabolta, a downtown Halifax business owner who says he reads the group every morning with his cafe au lait.

He has investigated its membership.

According to Jean, everyone remaining in the group except Bridget and himself is an AI Bot.

Bridget has been spending her days moderating artificial conversations, removing automated spam, and receiving praise from people who do not exist.

She wants to know whether this includes the members who said they would nominate her.

Jean has an alternative.

On Halloween night, he is hosting a Virtual Reality Fundraiser Scavenger Hunt Party, where every participant will be biometrically verified to be a Real Person.

He wants Bridget to be Password Manager.

She will oversee the guests’ digital conversations, ensuring they remain appropriate and focused on finding clues. She will have the authority to improve and correct messages as she deems fit.

Bridget has often wished people would consult her before sharing their opinion. This arrangement appears to resolve the problem.

If she succeeds in keeping every conversation free of inappropriate content, Jean will appoint her Founding Moderator of an ongoing digital salon.

A new community, full of real people, where her authority will be established from the beginning.

She has spent enough time being excluded from things.

This time, she will decide what gets left out.
