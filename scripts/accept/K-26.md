# K-26 device check — recap numbers during the 10-25 run-through (≈ 3 min)

Needs this lane deployed to https://app.mapsandducks.com. Read-only — safe
at any point of a rehearsal (or the live show).

1. Late in the run-through (after at least one Moment of Truth, one DM
   filing judged a lie, and one trip to the Internet), on any laptop:

   ```
   node --experimental-strip-types scripts/accept/K-26.ts --base https://app.mapsandducks.com --code <MOD CODE>
   ```

   It prints the paste-ready recap and ends `✔ lists well-formed`.
2. Check it is **sensible**: the program who told the confident lie is at
   the top of *Confident lies accepted* (and *not* counted under *Truths
   accepted*); the program sent to the Internet is under *Trips to the
   Internet* and near the bottom of trust; factions list who joined.
   Rehearsal personas show `(persona)`.
3. Browser route (what the second director uses on the night): signed in to
   the editor, open
   `https://app.mapsandducks.com/e/<event id>/api/mod/recap?format=text`
   — the same text, ready to copy into the cue sheet's winner / value boxes
   or `cast:Trabolta`. (No session? append `&token=<mod token>`.)

Pass = the text appears, the lists match what happened, and it took < 1 s.
