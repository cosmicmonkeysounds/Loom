# The house announcements player (retired)

The `alexa` job is retired for Trapped in the Internet (wave 18b): the
speaker router (the `audio` launchd job on John's MacBook) plays the
story's sounds and speaks the house announcements. Its runbook is
[`audio.md`](./audio.md), including *Retire the alexa job, switch the
router on*. `install-alexa.sh --uninstall` removes a leftover alexa agent.
