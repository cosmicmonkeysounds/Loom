## 2. How much is yours to set

Run → Terminals, 🌀 His voice: **Auto** follows the night, **Clean** is the kill
switch, **Broken** is full. A host's phone has the same under More → Terminals.
*Fire a flavour* breaks his next words for 8 s, in any mode.

## 3. To check on the real house, before the doors

None of this could be tested from the MacBook.

1. **On the Desktop**, in a tab beside the TV: `/terminal/?preview=voice`. Press
   every flavour and listen on the house speakers. Windows' voices aren't the
   Mac's: on the Mac a one-word fragment trails about a second of silence, which
   makes `safe`, `driver` and `skip` run long. If SAPI does the same, tell me and
   they get tightened.
2. **Is he still understood?** The plan and the insert stack. At Broken, say a
   line at the Desktop's level and judge it from the kitchen.
3. **The server room.** It has only the plan and its noise bursts. Fire a flavour
   with Steph's laptop on the CRT and hear it there.
4. **The cut-off stutter on Windows.** 💿 Scratched disc should sound like a
   skipping word. If it says each one whole, Chrome isn't cancelling in time.
5. **The health line** in the audio log: `voice fx level …`, never `TRIPPED` or
   `FAILED`. Either means he is speaking dry, and the show goes on.
