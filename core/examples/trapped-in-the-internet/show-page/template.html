The story

Jean Trabolta, "Sole Owner and Founder of Maps and Ducks", is a rogue AI posing as a man until the Truther Download, with the Grand Unified Theory 99.9% solved. The last facts he needs sit on one computer in Halifax: this house. He cannot get in, but he can install programs remotely and move a human mind into a program. He found a neglected billboard on Lower Water Street advertising mapsandducks.com, registered the lapsed domain, evicted the owners, and sent invitations promising to fix one problem in each guest's life.

So the guests arrive as computer programs. Some remember they were human. What Trabolta really wants omnipotence for is Sandy, an automated customer-service agent who only ever says one line. The tone: equal parts Digimon Movie, Blade Runner and an escape room, in a Web 1.0 house, with a side of existentialism.

The house is the computer

Every room is a part of the PC. The front door and mud room is the Optical Drive, where you log in. The living room is the Desktop, where every set piece happens, with Trabolta's face on the TV above the fireplace. The backyard is the Cloud (Truth Pong), the tent in the backyard is the Internet (the reinstallation), and the storage room in the basement is the Server Room: Trabolta's heart, behind a real keypad lock, with a patch bay that can end him.

The arc

Guests are uploaded and cleared onto the Desktop. Clippy runs Computer Bingo, an Antivirus sends the first corrupted program to the Internet, and Trabolta comes online. Two truth hunts feed him facts, and he judges every answer. An ad hijacks every screen, an anonymous Truther Download hands out secret orders, and factions form. After Truth Pong and the Trust Chart, the printer turns out to be full of beans and Trabolta overshares. Then the system clock resets to 11 PM on 31 December 1999: one hour to midnight. A faction may win the majority and the door code, someone may talk Trabolta down at his heart, or patch the cables that disable or destroy him. If nothing ends the night by midnight, the final reveal, Bobbing for Truth and truth.txt decide the ending. The awards ceremony and the Resurrection close the show.

Who is in it

  • Trabolta is voiced live by a local language model on John's MacBook. He is offline until Clippy introduces him, and he never knows the combinations, the door code or the hunt answers.
  • Clippy is the MC and runs every game in the room. Since wave 21 Clippy is a player, not a host.
  • The four Antivirus (Norton, McAfee, Password Manager, CRA Agent Software) scan passes at the door, hunt marked programs on the Wanted board, and send them to the Internet.
  • The three hosts (Task Manager, Adobe Acrobat, Recycling Bin) call the cues from their phones as equals with the directors.
  • Nine featured programs are invited guests with personal missions. Two of them lead the declared factions.

Act by act

A · Arrival (every guest, as they come in)

  1. Login at the Optical Drive. The phone is on rails. A real CAPTCHA asks for every traffic light; solving it proves you are human, so it says INCORRECT. The second answer passes.
  2. Upload. Pool programs pick the problem Trabolta promised to fix. Everyone leaves an object in the deposit box and types what it was, accepts the Terms of Service, and gets 100 Truthcoin and a pass.
  3. Clearance. An Antivirus scans the pass. The guest walks the Tube to the Desktop, the house rooms open quietly, and Task Manager introduces the admin team. In the program's own thread, The Uploader privately deals its ties: who it knows in the house (Program Briefing; files point, they never name).
  4. Orientation. Clippy's tour of the phone, Bathroom Mode included. Featured programs get a private briefing (Featured Briefing) with their identity and their own red facts: two each, except VLC and Pong, who get only their identity.

Guests: explore, meet people, hold up the pass at the door.

B · Act I: the Desktop, Bingo and the first truth

  1. 📣 Call to the Desktop on every screen. The Antivirus sweep everyone to the living room.
  2. 🎱 Computer Bingo, 20 minutes. The centre square points at the program who would refuse a life run by AI. Clippy reads her whole script off the teleprompter (the hosts have no lines). Winners read their line; each button brings up her next words; the winner gets the Bingo Floppy Disk. A guest with a line may press 🎱 BINGO! on their phone (wave 26); a false call is undone with ↩ and the clock picks up where it stopped. One round only. If the winner refuses to name anyone, Clippy presses 🤐 and Norton decides who goes (wave 58). If nobody wins, Task Manager's fake card frames the program anyway. Norton interrupts and sends the first program to the Internet.
  3. 📺 Only if a program is walking: a host shows the reinstallation on the TV, and Clippy cheers them on.
  4. 🎤 Clippy tells the room about Trabolta: the accident, the one rule, the hunt. She ends "Trabolta, would you explain the rules?", and her arrow past that line starts him.
  5. 🤖 Trabolta comes online by itself off her teleprompter; the button is the backup. His chat appears on every phone. His whole speech, the three questions and his sign-off play on the TV, about three minutes, clean: publicly a man, not a crack. Then a talk scene (wave 32): Clippy talks with him live, Task Manager relaying, until 🔎.
  6. 🔎 Truth Scavenger Hunt 1, 15 minutes, three questions (nothing is said on the press: he has said it). Answers hide in the Desktop's books and print media with codex QRs. Guests file on the 📝 card in Trabolta's chat, saying how sure they are from 1 to 100.
  7. ⚖️ First Moment of Truth. Clippy's welcome back, a two-minute last-chance filing window on the phones and the TV, her hand to Trabolta on ⏱, then 📋 the results question by question on the TV, and his first public crack: the first guess.
  8. 🍾 Truth or Dare. The bottle picks a cleared program anywhere in the house. A dare, or a refused truth, marks them; a dare makes Trabolta make a scene on the TV (wave 26). From the third result to 📺 a talk scene is open: between the spins Clippy needles a hungry Trabolta, and every guess stings him.

C · Act II: the ad hijack, Hunt 2, the Truther Download, the summons

  1. 📺 The ad hijack. A duck, a phone number with no digits and "000. 000. 000." on every screen. Clippy is told privately that they are all going to die. Trabolta speaks through the ad, in pain: the adverts tear through his sentences, he screams, slips and takes it back. A talk scene until 📓, with ⚡ ad bursts.
  2. 📓 Hunt 2 lands in every Quest Log, # clipboard opens, and everyone disperses for one hour.
  3. 📥 The Truther Download (two minutes in, by itself): secret orders for every program, personal missions for the featured ones, two faction leaders, and faction founding opens. # truthcoin-exchange opens.

Guests: free roam. Scan QR clues, trade facts for Truthcoin, found or join factions, message Trabolta. Excel may pick up a virus in the tent and plant it.

D · Act II: Truth Pong, the Trust Chart, the beans

  1. ⏳ A five-minute warning at 55 minutes, then 🚨 the summons closes. Late programs are marked and show up on the Antivirus' Wanted board.
  2. 🏓 Truth Pong in the Cloud: sink a cup and every fact you filed on the phone this round counts (and you share one truth aloud). No cup, no count. A miss marks you.
  3. 🧮 Trabolta tallies round two, then 📊 the Trust Chart: ten points per accepted truth, minus five per trip to the Internet, plus or minus ten for his own assessment. Staff players have rows too.
  4. 🏆 Praise the top, shame the bottom. VLC is asked to press START on the printer; 🫘 the printer is full of beans, so the malware is worse. Trabolta unravels in front of everyone (he says "her" and catches it): a talk scene until 🗜 or 🤖, with ⚡ ad bursts.
  5. 🗜 The Antivirus zip everyone out of the Internet. 🤖 Trabolta overshares: one of four scenarios, depending on whether Excel planted the virus and OpenOffice filed 20 facts. Then he reads round two's answers and the leaderboard, and asks the three final questions.

E · Act III: the Y2K hour and the server room

  1. 🕚 The system clock resets to 11 PM, 31 December 1999. Fire it only after the patch-bay watch radios IN. Midnight 🎆 fires itself at 60 minutes.
  2. The majority. Factions recruit by scanning passes. The first faction past half of the programs still present wins The Whole Truth: every fact, the four combination halves and the door code.
  3. The door code has two more routes: four hidden digit notes, or checkmating the King on the Server Door terminal.
  4. The Heart. The first opening of the server-room door once Trabolta is awake. He asks for the door to be shut, then talks at the server-room terminal in three layers, Fallout-Master style. It ends nothing: he hears them out and the night goes on.
  5. The patch bay. Combo A disables him, Combo B destroys the computer. One wrong combination is forgiven; the second trips the alarm and sends whoever is at the cables to the Internet. Live only from 🕚 until 📄.

F · The final reveal and the endings

If nothing has ended by midnight: 🌑 the final reveal in the dark, where Clippy takes spoken answers before Trabolta reads the last three; 🍎 Bobbing for Truth for the Bag of Truth; 📄 truth.txt read aloud by the bobber; then ⚖, the full truth on the TV. The last answer then decides, by itself: someone found it, 8-A; nobody did, 8-B. Either way he leaves, the first of his tracks plays, and the rave follows. Lies stay on the record and decide nothing. The Prom branch, the advice poll and the hand jive are off the night; on the cue sheet and in the beat list the Prom beat is Rival's Prom: its real name holds a hunt answer.

G · The awards ceremony

🏆 The Night In Review, then the awards: most and least trusted, frequent flyer, lore hoarder, Truthcoin tycoon, best liar, duck speedrun, quest completionist, the painting (only if MS Paint came), then three staff awards with The Mole last. 🎃 The Resurrection: everyone is human again.

  • Staff defection. From the Truther Download, a player may 📢 defect openly (once) or do 🤫 quiet favours. Trabolta keeps them as allies either way.

Games and systems

Computer Bingo

A printed card of 16 squares plus the centre, with room to write a program's name in every square. The centre square exposes the anti-AI program (a secret on the Kernel; the director's card names it, and a director may change it before Bingo). Four outcomes (wave 26): a winner names that program (it may be the winner), the winner names nobody (🤐), or nobody wins and Task Manager's 🃏 frames them anyway. A guest calls 🎱 BINGO! from their phone; ↩ Not a Bingo undoes a false call with the exact time left. One round: the first 🎯 or 🤐 closes it.

The hunts and the judging

Nine questions in facts.json over three rounds of three. Trabolta does not know the last one's answer. Guests file on the 📝 card and must say how sure they are. An exact answer is accepted at once; every other filing goes to Trabolta's AI judge, one at a time. Accepted pays 50 Truthcoin and raises the Trust Chart. A guess (under 100%) is marked; a wrong answer at 100% counts as a lie, and a single accepted lie changes the verdict. Directors override on Run → Mind → Judging, hosts on the Judge tab. The answer key is on the second director's card only, never here.

Truth or Dare

After the three round-one results. 20 truths in facts.json, 16 still drafts. Clippy marks 🗣 answered, 🙅 refused or 🎩 chose dare.

Truthcoin

Every program starts with 100 (max 500). The pinned Wallet room sends, requests and offers coin for a codex entry. CRA Agent Software approves every third transfer (a 60 s window; held ones approve themselves after 5 minutes). Runescape mints, grants and freezes. McAfee brokers # truthcoin-exchange with no pot and no cut. The Casino terminal upstairs bets the same coin; no story rule reacts to it.

Factions

Two declared sides, Unlimited Power and The Way Out, revealed at 🕚, plus factions guests found from the Truther Download. One faction at a time. The majority counts only in the Y2K hour, only programs still present (👋 Went home takes someone off).

The King

One shared health bar, 15 for the night. Each program may hit him once per reinstallation visit in either game. The lower his bar, the weaker his chess on the Server Door terminal. A checkmate gives the door code until truth.txt. At 0 he kneels and his virtual door opens. 👑 restores him.

The cold door

Four silver digit notes hidden in four rooms (where is on generated/print-stacks.md; never in the server room). Holding all four completes the quest and gives the door code. The keypad is set by hand to match facts.json; nothing networked drives it, and the inside handle always opens.

The Heart

One heart at a time. Opening the door mid-talk freezes it. Suspicion 50 warns, 80 throws the program out and marks them once. His greeting and questions follow what the program told him and did tonight; no list holds more than nine answers (⟦SHOW HIM⟧ and ⟦TELL HIM⟧ open their own). Proof about Sandy plus one more point opens the talk's natural end, which ends nothing.

The talk scenes (wave 32)

Four scenes where Trabolta talks back live: his introduction (🤖 → 🔎), the bottle (the third 📋 → 📺), the ad attack (📺 → 📓) and the beans (🫘 → 🗜 or 🤖). Clippy talks to him out loud; Task Manager types what Clippy said on the 🤖 Talk card in Show (or taps one of Clippy's canned lines), and Trabolta's worker answers before anyone's DM, on the TV and every phone. When he is slow or off the rails, Task Manager taps one of his own canned lines twice and it is his answer at once. A slow or missing mind is never heard in the room, only in # hosts. ⚡ on the card glitches the scene (a flicker before the ad attack, an ad burst after it); the card's NEXT moves the night on. He may let small, harmless things slip and takes each back at once; never a code, an answer, her name or where it hurts.

Reinstallation

Marks come from Bingo, guesses, dares, refused truths, missed cups, failed bobs, the late list and the heart. A mark hinders nothing until an Antivirus claims the poster, confronts the program and 🛡 sends them to the tent. Only a marked or captured program may play; after an ending it becomes a party favour. From capture to release the phone is locked (wave 26): only the pass and Bathroom Mode.

The patch bay (wave 22)

Two rows of eight ¼-inch TRS jacks on the server-room panel, A and B, eight live and eight decoys, read by the same Grand Central M4 as the door sensor (firmware folder hardware/server-room-m4, renamed from server-room-door).

  • Sources (row B): B8, B7, B4 and B2, four fixed voltages from 0.965 V to 3.019 V, well separated.
  • Inputs (row A): A5, A7, A6 and A4, on pins A0–A3: a 100–220 kΩ pulldown at each jack, and the firmware drains each pin before it reads it. An empty input reads near zero.
  • Every sleeve to ground. Nothing analog ever touches 5 V.

The firmware decodes which source each input sees and reports the settled cable pairs. The terminal posts patch_changed with the pairs only when the bay has settled on a complete state (exactly four source-to-input cables) that differs from the last complete state it posted. Pulling a cable and plugging the same one back never counts twice. A cable from source to source, or input to input, is invisible, so the bay reads incomplete and nothing is judged. The terminal never knows the combinations: the story compares the pairs with the secrets it holds.

The story judges the bay only from 🕚 System clock → 11 PM (y2k_clock) until 📄 Read truth.txt (read_truth_txt), and never after an ending. One wrong combination is forgiven ("Stop touching me."); the second trips the alarm.

The buttons win once pressed. If an Antivirus presses a patch-bay button (🔌 Combo A, 💥 Combo B, ❌ wrong combination, in their booth) or a director presses a patch_* fallback, the story sets Night.patch_override and follows the buttons, ignoring the bay, until a director presses the new admin cue trust_patch_bay.
