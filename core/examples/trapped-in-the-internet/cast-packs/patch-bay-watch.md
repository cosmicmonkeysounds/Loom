## The bay

Two rows of 12 jacks. Top row **V1–V8** (+ 4 decoys), bottom row **D1–D8**
(+ 4 decoys). Every unwired jack is masked "SECTOR DAMAGED". A combination is
**four cables**, each from one V jack to one D jack. Order does not matter.

| Name | The four cables (read them in V order) | What it does | Call |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Combo A** "Disable" | **V2–D5 · V4–D1 · V6–D8 · V7–D3** | disables Trabolta (blackout, dance, he recounts) | **"A"** |
| **Combo B** "Self-destruct" | **V1–D6 · V3–D2 · V5–D7 · V8–D4** | destroys the computer (blackout, dance, purgatory) | **"B"** |
| anything else, once all four cables are in | | wrong combination | **"WRONG"** |

Guests learn the halves from their codex once their faction wins the majority.
Do **not** help them: you are an Antivirus. You do not know the door code: the
inside handle always opens with none, but **once you step out you can't get
back in** without the house manager (the override key, or the code at the
keypad, never on the radio).

## What you do

1. **Be inside with the door shut before 🕚**, then radio **"IN"**:
   whoever is calling the show (📣) fires 🕚 only after it. Once Trabolta is awake
   the **first opening of the door plays The Heart**, whoever opens it, and
   the door sensor (a light on the frame) can't tell you from a guest: **press 🔧 *Crew going
   through the door* on your booth (any program's card) before you open
   it yourself**, and for 2 minutes an opening only tells `# hosts`. Stand
   where you can see the bay and the door, and don't open it until guests
   have come in.
2. When four cables are in, check them against the table and press it
   yourself on your booth, on the card of the program at the cables: **🔌
   Combo A is in**, **💥 Combo B is in** or **❌ wrong combination** (the
   buttons lead the card from 🕚 through the Y2K hour; from 🌑 the final
   reveal until 📄 they are under **All buttons…** on any card, DRAFT Q33). Radio **"A"**, **"B"**
   or **"WRONG"** only if your phone can't: a host or the director fires
   🔌 / 💥 / ❌.
3. After a WRONG, nod at the guests, say nothing, let them pull cables.
   (Everyone is forgiven one: the **second** wrong combination trips the
   alarm. The director's 🔄 *Server room: reset the patch-bay alarm* forgives
   one again.) When the alarm trips, whoever is at the cables goes to the
   Internet: they are not marked, so **🛡** is under **All buttons…** on
   their card (*Not on offer now*: tap it, tap again).
4. Reset after an ending only if the director asks: every cable out.

## Also yours

- **The door.** It has a standalone keypad lock (nothing in the show drives
  it). Whoever opens it with the code is a clean entry, whoever they are: let
  them in. The door never alarms. **Wave 20:** the door sensor is a **light on
  the frame** shining into a small eye; the story knows the door is shut only
  while the beam is unbroken. Once Trabolta is awake the first opening
  plays **The Heart**: the room opens on every phone, his screen goes red, the
  room's bulb wakes, and **he asks for the door to be shut. Nothing goes on
  until it is.** He nags every 20 s while it stands open, and a door opened
  in the middle of a program's talk freezes it (*CLOSE THE DOOR* on his
  screen) until it is shut again. So **close the door behind every program**
  (and behind yourself), never prop it, and keep out of the beam. If the
  story has it wrong (the door opened and nothing happened, or it is shut
  and his screen still says CLOSE THE DOOR), press **🚪 The server-room door
  opened** or **🚪 The server-room door is shut** on your booth (any

  Never say the code on the radio.
