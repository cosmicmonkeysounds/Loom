# Patch-bay watch (server room, Tier 0 only)

> **Holder:** the Antivirus stationed in the server room from the Y2K clock
> ([[EDIT: who — suggested CRA Agent Software, decided at the 10-25
> read-through]]). **Keep this card face-down in your pocket.** It holds the
> two combinations, which no guest may see. It is only needed if the patch-bay
> scanner fails on the bench (10-22) or flakes on the night (W-31, OP-13);
> otherwise the bay judges itself and you just watch.

## The bay

Two rows of 12 jacks. Top row **V1–V8** (+ 4 decoys), bottom row **D1–D8**
(+ 4 decoys). Every unwired jack is masked "SECTOR DAMAGED". A combination is
**four cables**, each from one V jack to one D jack. Order does not matter.

| Name | The four cables (read them in V order) | What it does | Call |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Combo A** "Disable" | **V2–D5 · V4–D1 · V6–D8 · V7–D3** | disables Trabolta (blackout, dance, he recounts) | **"A"** |
| **Combo B** "Self-destruct" | **V1–D6 · V3–D2 · V5–D7 · V8–D4** | destroys the computer (blackout, dance, purgatory) | **"B"** |
| anything else, once all four cables are in | | wrong combination | **"WRONG"** |

Guests learn the halves from their codex once their faction wins the majority.
Do **not** help them: you are an Antivirus.

## What you do

1. From the **Y2K clock** on, stand where you can see the bay and the door.
2. When four cables are in, check them against the table. Radio the director:
   **"A"**, **"B"** or **"WRONG"**. The director fires 🔌 / 💥 / ❌.
3. After a WRONG, nod at the guests, say nothing, let them pull cables.
   (Two wrong combinations, or one while the room is locked, trip the alarm.)
4. Reset after an ending only if the director asks: every cable out.

## Also yours, from the Y2K clock

- **The door.** Before a majority unlocks the room, anyone entering trips the
  alarm (the door sensor does it; radio **ALARM** if it doesn't). After the
  unlock, send anyone who is not in the winning faction to the Internet
  (🛡 on their card).
- **The chess phone** (Cold Lock). Glance at it each act. A guest who beats
  the machine opens the door for 90 seconds. Press **♟ They beat the machine**
  on the winner's card **only after you saw "checkmate" on the phone** (OP-12).
  Phone dead → radio the director, who fires it.
- **The real gear** (router, UPS, the dongle, the ESP32 power) is taped off and
  out of reach. Keep guests off it, politely and firmly. In Act III the room
  fills up.
- **Blackout:** guide everyone in the room to the living room. The server room
  and its path stay lit.
