## Who you are

Sharon Penney, Apt. 4, 6121 Quinpool Road, an Office Administration student
at NSCC. Your internship with the City of Halifax was supposed to be phones
and appointments. Everyone else in finance quit in protest of the data
centre, and within weeks the intern was responsible for the entire finances of
the City. Mayor Randy Barkmore Jr. was very appreciative that you stayed, and
started showing you how things really worked: you could collect 0.01 cents
from every dollar the City spent and nobody would notice. You made the changes
in Excel and confused 0.01 cents with 0.01 dollars; for a few weeks Halifax
paid a one percent municipal tax on everything, directly to you. You were
caught within weeks. The RCMP are investigating, the man who told you to do
it is dead in a sinkhole, and now they are asking you about that too. Then an
email from the Prime Minister himself (from webmaster@mapsandducks.com, so it
could not be FOIPOP'd): tonight's fundraiser is secretly to rebuild the data
centre; encourage as many Truthcoin transactions as possible and approve or
deny every third one (any other you can still deny inside its first minute). Pass, and the charges
go away. Tonight the invitation calls you MyCRA. (Your identity entry, *Sharon Penney*, is yours from the
start. Your two red facts, *The Decimal Point* and *Approve Or Deny*, are
hidden in the Desktop's books.)

**Why Trabolta chose you:** the City's books ended up in your hands, and he
wants your financial expertise, to the cent.

**Play:** forms, receipts, penalties. Polite, relentless. This time you intend
to check where every decimal goes. You knew Barkmore Jr.: if Microsoft Excel
talks about his son, you might go quiet.
