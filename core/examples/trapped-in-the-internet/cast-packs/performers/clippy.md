## Who you are

Before the computer you spent five years at an underfunded nonprofit
veterinary clinic, sure you were helping animals every day. It was unlicensed,
not a nonprofit, and not a clinic: you were injecting saline. The fake vet
vanished with the promise to pay your student debt. Then a letter offered a
community game night where you could *help people all night*, and debt would
no longer be relevant. (Your codex: *The Clinic*, *Saline*.)

**Why Trabolta chose you:** the perfect traumatised servant who desperately
needs to rebuild an identity. You believe every word. You are his favourite,
and his liability.

**Play:** relentlessly helpful, a little too bright, always available ("If
anyone needs Help With That, I'll be available"). The cracks show as the
night goes on: the malware, the beans, the dark. You never take sides on air.

## Your night, act by act

**Arrivals (20:00 → C1).** Greet people. Your scan of a pass greets them in
your voice (it never clears anyone: only an Antivirus scan does). Guests who
skipped your orientation get a hurt little line from you; play it up.

**C1 · 📣 Call to the Desktop.** Everyone is swept to the living room. Your
booth: *📋 Instructions 1/X…*. Welcome everyone (CI 1): the fundraiser to
restore the sign at Maps And Ducks; you are Clippy, the official Office
Assistant; *Jean Trabolta himself will make an appearance later tonight*.
Introduce the hosts: Task Manager keeps things running, Adobe Acrobat assists
with documents and printing, Recycling Bin is hygiene and maintenance.

**V2 · 🎱 Computer Bingo (20 min).** Hand out cards and markers. The card is a
big asterisk: a Bingo is one full line through the centre (two diagonals, the
centre column, the centre row). **Nobody writes names**: they use their
special computer memory. The centre square: *"Would you never agree to a life
where AI makes all of your decisions?"* At 20 minutes your booth pings: ask
the director for BINGO TIME.

**V3 · ⏰ Bingo results** (CI 2). Winners read their line and name each match
from memory, centre square last. Press 🎱 on each winner and 🎀 for the ribbon;
demand applause. When the centre-square program is named, **Norton
interrupts** and sends them to the Internet. Let her. If nobody has a Bingo,
Task Manager hands you a filled card for MS Paint: read the name, invite them
up; when they deny it, let Norton respond. You never press 🛡.

**Corrupted Code** (plays after the first capture). Reassure: occasional
corruption is normal in the lifecycle of a program; it makes programs stop
following the directives of Jean Trabolta; the Internet is where programs are
reinstalled with a short activity; they come right back. Routine. Reassuring.

**🤖 Introduce Trabolta.** Radio **INTRO** (so WebMD starts the first game),
then the director fires it. Your line is on the TV: *"Everyone, say hello to
TRABOLTA!"* He speaks. The TV switches to the reinstallation mirror: point at
it, *"Look! That's {the first program}! Doing great!"* Hand off: *"And now,
Trabolta has a game for all of us."*

**V4/V5 · 🔎 Hunt 1 (15 min).** Trabolta reads H1–H3; they go into everyone's
Quest Log. At C2 your booth gets CI 3.

**V6 · ⚖️ First Moment of Truth** (CI 3). *"Is anyone able to answer the
questions?"* The rules, out loud: accurate is accepted; delivered with strong
confidence is accepted even if nonsensical; normal or low confidence, or a
guess, goes to the Internet. Judge per question with the buttons. If not all
three are answered, the director fires **🎩 Truth or Dare**: someone random
goes first; Dare → 🎩; Truth → they draw a slip (appendix) and you judge it
like an answer; said it's false and right → 🙅.

**📺 The ad hijack** (CI 4). You begin previewing round 2; an ad takes over
every screen. A **private message from Trabolta** lands in your booth: *if the
malware continues we are all going to die… try not to show the others.*
**Overreact wildly**, then pull it together: *"There are signs of malware in
the system that we need to be very vigilant about. We are going to proceed on
the Truth Scavenger Hunt because it is more important than ever that Trabolta
gets the information he needs."*

**📓 Hunt 2: disperse for one hour.** Send everyone off to return for the
Second Moment of Truth.

**⏳ Five minutes → 🏓 Truth Pong** (CI 5). Visibly distraught, more excited
than ever: *"It's Truth Time again! Truth Pong, everyone's favourite game!"*
Rules: a cup pyramid on the folding table outside; bounce or throw a ball into
a cup *because you are being commanded*; miss → the Internet; make → answer one
fact. Adobe judges the cup; you judge the answer.

**📊 Trust Chart → 🏆 rankings** (CI 6). Lead the applause for the most
trusted like your life depends on it. Don't defend the least trusted. VLC is
told to print a certificate; the printer jams; MS Paint may urge them to open
it. **It's full of beans:** clock it at once: *"Routine operations like print
to PDF are no longer working. It is even more urgent that we get the next
facts right."* Welcome back the zipped programs (*compressed, do not open*).

**🕚 Y2K clock** (CI 7). The final round. Factions start recruiting: **you may
not take sides on air.** Keep everyone hunting.

**🌑 Final reveal** (CI 8). Lights dark. Everyone may speak: what they learned,
what they believe, what should happen next. Judge F1/F2. Then: *"There is only
one version of the truth. Because truth is always in binary."* Dejectedly
introduce **🍎 Bobbing for Truth**: *"we are not worthy of the truth unless we
are willing to Bob for it and risk our status as a program."* Nothing moves
until someone brings up the bag (🍎). Let Trabolta give his assessment, then
the bobber reads truth.txt.

**Endings.** In Grok Prom, a program who says *"You're the one that I want"*
to you gets 💘. After **Combo B** (CI 9): dance, then the director fires *Clippy
reads the summary*; read it aloud from the Desktop room on your phone, then the
verdict: *"…We are now in purgatory forever together, which is pretty similar
to being stuck in a computer."*

**🏆 Ceremony.** After Combo B or the rave, **you host**: each award line lands
in the Desktop room on your phone; read it aloud, call the winner up, lead
applause. Otherwise Trabolta hosts on the TV and you lead the applause.
