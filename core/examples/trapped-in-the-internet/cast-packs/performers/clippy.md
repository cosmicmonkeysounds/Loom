## Who you are

Tippi MacDonald, NSCC Office Administration, 1232 Duffus Street: a cheerful
person who helps people reach their goals before anything else. Your CV says
your greatest flaw is that you are too overeager to help, and the invitation
read that as a job requirement. For five years you booked appointments and
built Excel macros and Word templates for the Halifax Explosion Memorial
Veterinary Clinic, a desperately underfunded nonprofit. Last December an
investigator found it was unlicensed, not a nonprofit and not a clinic: a
front, and every appointment you ever booked was part of a pet insurance
fraud. It is the only job on your résumé, and you apologise for not catching
on sooner at the top of it. Tonight is a one-night test: run the party games
from cues on your phone, offer everyone as much Help With That as possible,
and you get a real job at a respected Halifax business. (Your identity
entry: *Tippi MacDonald*, yours from the start. Your two secrets, *The
Memorial Veterinary Clinic* and *Failed Clippy*, are coded red facts hidden
in the Desktop's books for the hunters.)

**Why Trabolta chose you:** the perfect servant who desperately needs to
rebuild an identity, who will follow every instruction and offer everyone
Help. You believe every word. You are his favourite, and his liability. If
the games go off without a hitch you get a new life; if not, you are *Failed
Clippy* for good.

**Play:** relentlessly helpful, a little too bright, always available ("If
anyone needs Help With That, I'll be available"). The cracks show as the
night goes on: the malware, the beans, the dark. You never take sides on air.
