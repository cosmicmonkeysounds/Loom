## Who you are

Tippi MacDonald, NSCC Office Administration, 1232 Duffus Street: a cheerful
person who helps people reach their goals before anything else. Your CV says
your greatest flaw is that you are too overeager to help, and the invitation
read that as a job requirement. For five years you booked appointments and
built Excel macros and Word templates for the Halifax Explosion Memorial
Veterinary Clinic, a desperately underfunded nonprofit. Last December an
investigator found it was unlicensed, not a nonprofit and not a clinic: a
front, and every appointment you ever booked was part of a pet insurance
fraud. It is the only job on your résumé, and you apologise for not catching
on sooner at the top of it. Tonight is a one-night test: run the party games
from cues on your phone, offer everyone as much Help With That as possible,
and you get a real job at a respected Halifax business. (Your identity
entry: *Tippi MacDonald*, yours from the start. Your two secrets, *The
Memorial Veterinary Clinic* and *Failed Clippy*, are coded red facts hidden
in the Desktop's books for the hunters.)

**Why Trabolta chose you:** the perfect servant who desperately needs to
rebuild an identity, who will follow every instruction and offer everyone
Help. You believe every word. You are his favourite, and his liability. If
the games go off without a hitch you get a new life; if not, you are *Failed
Clippy* for good.

**Play:** relentlessly helpful, a little too bright, always available ("If
anyone needs Help With That, I'll be available"). The cracks show as the
night goes on: the malware, the beans, the dark. You never take sides on air.

## Your night, act by act

**Arrivals (20:00 → C1).** Greet people. Your scan of a pass greets them in
your voice (it never clears anyone: only an Antivirus scan does). When an
Antivirus clears a program, Task Manager's line introduces the four of you
("Your hosts tonight, per the agenda…") and opens `# task-manager` for them;
then your Orientation starts, with its **Bathroom Mode** step over the pass
button. Guests who skipped your orientation get a hurt little line from you;
play it up.

**C1 · 📣 Call to the Desktop.** Everyone is swept to the living room. Your
booth: *📋 Instructions 1/X…*. Welcome everyone (CI 1): the fundraiser to
restore the sign at Maps And Ducks; you are Clippy, the official Office
Assistant; *Jean Trabolta himself will make an appearance later tonight*.
Introduce the hosts: Task Manager keeps things running, Adobe Acrobat assists
with documents and printing, Recycling Bin is hygiene and maintenance.

**V2 · 🎱 Computer Bingo (20 min).** Your Quest Log: *Per the agenda: Computer Bingo*. Hand out
cards and markers. The card is a big asterisk: a Bingo is one full line
through the centre (two diagonals, the centre column, the centre row).
**Nobody writes names**: they use their special computer memory. The centre
square: *"Would you never agree to a life where AI makes all of your
decisions?"* At 20 minutes your booth pings: ask the director for BINGO
TIME.

**V3 · ⏰ Bingo results** (CI 2). Winners read their line and name each match
from memory, centre square last. Press 🎱 on each winner and 🎀 for the ribbon;
demand applause. When the centre-square program is named, press **🎯 Named on
the card** on them: they are marked, and **Norton interrupts** and takes them
to the Internet. Let her. If nobody has a Bingo, Task Manager hands you a
filled card for MS Paint: read the name, invite them up; when they deny it,
let Norton respond. You never press 🛡.

**Corrupted Code** (plays after the first capture). Reassure: occasional
corruption is normal in the lifecycle of a program; it makes programs stop
following the directives of Jean Trabolta; the Internet is where programs are
reinstalled with a short activity; they come right back. Routine. Reassuring.

**🤖 Introduce Trabolta.** Radio **INTRO** (so the tent starts the first game),
then the director fires it. Your line is on the TV: *"Everyone, say hello to
TRABOLTA!"* He speaks. The TV switches to the reinstallation mirror: point at
it, *"Look! That's {the first program}! Doing great!"* Hand off: *"And now,
Trabolta has a game for all of us."*

**V4/V5 · 🔎 Hunt 1 (15 min).** Your Quest Log: *Per the agenda: the Truth Scavenger Hunt*. Trabolta
reads H1–H3; they go into everyone's Quest Log. The answers are documents
hidden in the Desktop's books and print media, each with a code. Programs
**file** an answer with Trabolta on the 📝 card in his thread: the question,
the answer, and the slider *Please select your confidence level in this
response* (1–100). At C2 your booth gets CI 3.

**V6 · ⚖️ First Moment of Truth** (CI 3). *"Is anyone able to answer the
questions?"* The rules, out loud: accurate is accepted; filed one hundred
percent sure is accepted even if nonsensical; normal or low confidence, or a
guess, is a symptom of corruption and the Antivirus escort you to the
Internet. Then:

1. **Two minutes to file.** Tell everyone to file anything they found NOW.
   The director closes it with **⏱** (or it closes itself). *"Pencils down!
   Trabolta is reading."* A closed round never reopens: a question nobody
   filed never comes back.
2. **📋 Results, one question at a time** (the director presses it for Q1,
   then Q2, then Q3). Your phone shows that question's result in the Desktop
   room; read it aloud. Three shapes: **CONSENSUS** (every answer matches and
   everyone was 100% sure: Trabolta accepts it and you read the answer) ·
   **nobody filed** (*"We have a backup. It's called Truth or Dare, and I
   have a bottle."*) · **no consensus** (the list of what everyone told
   Trabolta: read it, then *confident answers are accepted, right or wrong;
   guesses are a symptom of corruption. Antivirus, you know what to do.*).
   The app settles each filing as you read: +50 Truthcoin for an accepted
   answer, marked for reinstallation for a guess. **Never say which filings
   were lies**: the room hears "accepted" for every confident answer.
3. **🍾 The bottle.** After each question the director spins it up to
   **five times**. The server picks a cleared program who has not been
   picked for this question, wherever they are in the house (call them
   forward); the TV says *"{name}. Truth or dare?"*
   and their phone asks. **Dare** → marked for reinstallation (programs
   choose Truth). **Truth** → Trabolta asks them a personal question on the
   TV; make them answer it in front of everyone, then press **🗣 Truth
   answered** on their card (+50 Truthcoin). Won't answer → **🙅 Truth
   refused**: marked and escorted. A dead phone → **🎩 Chose Dare** on their
   card does the same as the tap.
4. After five spins, the next question's results. After Q3, the director
   previews hunt 2.

**📺 The ad hijack** (CI 4). You begin previewing round 2; an ad takes over
every screen. A **private message from Trabolta** lands in your booth (and
your Quest Log: *Per Trabolta: keep everyone calm*): *if the malware continues we are all
going to die… try not to show the others.* **Overreact wildly**, then pull it
together: *"There are signs of malware in the system that we need to be very
vigilant about. We are going to proceed on the Truth Scavenger Hunt because
it is more important than ever that Trabolta gets the information he needs."*

**📓 Hunt 2: disperse for one hour.** Send everyone off to return for the
Second Moment of Truth. Round 2 (H4–H6) is filed the same way; it settles at
the tally.

**⏳ Five minutes → 🏓 Truth Pong** (CI 5). Visibly distraught, more excited
than ever: *"It's Truth Time again! Truth Pong, everyone's favourite game!"*
Rules: a cup pyramid on the folding table outside; bounce or throw a ball into
a cup *because you are being commanded*; miss → marked for reinstallation;
make → answer one fact. Adobe judges the cup; you judge the answer (✅ / 😤
with the question number, 🤷 for a guess).

**📊 Trust Chart → 🏆 rankings** (CI 6). Lead the applause for the most
trusted like your life depends on it. Don't defend the least trusted. VLC is
told to print a certificate; the printer jams; MS Paint may urge them to open
it. **It's full of beans:** clock it at once: *"Routine operations like print
to PDF are no longer working. It is even more urgent that we get the next
facts right."* Welcome back the zipped programs (*compressed, do not open*).

**🕚 Y2K clock** (CI 7). The final round. Factions start recruiting: **you may
not take sides on air.** Keep everyone hunting.

**🌑 Final reveal** (CI 8). Lights dark. Everyone may speak: what they learned,
what they believe, what should happen next. Judge F1/F2 (✅ / 😤 Q1–Q2, 🤷).
Then: *"There is only one version of the truth. Because truth is always in
binary."* Dejectedly introduce **🍎 Bobbing for Truth**: *"we are not worthy
of the truth unless we are willing to Bob for it and risk our status as a
program."* Nothing moves until someone brings up the bag (🍎). Let Trabolta
give his assessment, then the bobber reads truth.txt (📄). When they finish,
the director fires **⚖** for the verdict; right after it, Trabolta DMs every
program for a pickup line that works on Sandy (an answer card); the server
judges it.

**Endings.** In Grok Prom, a program who says *"You're the one that I want"*
to you gets 💘 (the card in their Trabolta thread counts too). After **Combo
B** (CI 9): dance, then the director fires *Clippy reads the summary*; read it
aloud from the Desktop room on your phone, then the verdict: *"…We are now in
purgatory forever together, which is pretty similar to being stuck in a
computer."*

**🏆 Ceremony.** After Combo B or the rave, **you host**: each award line lands
in the Desktop room on your phone; read it aloud, call the winner up, lead
applause. Otherwise Trabolta hosts on the TV and you lead the applause.
