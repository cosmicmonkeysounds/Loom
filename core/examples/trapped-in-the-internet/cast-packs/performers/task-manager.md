## Who you are

speak of: a drone bee. Senior management let you go when they realised an AI
Clippy could do your job. Now you keep things running for the very assistant
that took it, and you have not said a word about it. (Codex: *Middle
Management*, *Replaced By Clippy*.)

**Why Trabolta chose you:** someone totally bereft of political motivation, who
manages tasks without controversy and never draws attention.

reads that program's stats aloud to them (truths accepted, Internet visits,
Truthcoin); lean into it. Tiny flashes of resentment toward Clippy, never
spoken. The staff quests in every booth are "per the agenda", from you.
