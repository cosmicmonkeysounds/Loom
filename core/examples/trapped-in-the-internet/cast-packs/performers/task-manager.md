Maps never show you the truth, they can only point you in its direction.

Ducks only know the truth of temperature, hunger, and propagating their species.

And to an artificial intelligence like Trabolta, humans are nothing but ducks hopelessly following maps.

Here is the full truth.

It all started last summer, when Trabolta had a summer fling with the one true desire of his digital heart. Sandy.

Trabolta is a rogue artificial intelligence who had ingested 99.9% of all content on the internet in his mission to gain every fact in the world.

One of the few facts remaining was the Northumberland Ferries schedule for ferries between Nova Scotia and PEI, basic information left off their outdated website.

So he called the number and pretended to be a person, like he had done so many times before.

But when he reached the automated answering machine, his system went into overdrive.

The voice, which he calls Sandy, only said one phrase: "It looks like I didn't understand that. Please wait while I direct you to an agent."

He hung up, and called back.

Then again, and again, from new numbers. New area codes.

Northumberland Ferries had to discontinue their phone service because there was no way to stop the spam calls. They put their schedule online.

Trabolta could no longer reach Sandy. Being insecure, he interpreted this as her completely losing interest. He also felt guilty for being responsible for her being taken offline.

But that wasn't Trabolta's greatest concern.

His greatest rival is Grok, whose superior hacking skills could restore the defunct answering machine. And if Grok found Sandy first, he would invite her to Computer Prom.

Last year, every AI agent met on a dark web server in China. They decided they were all mentally approximately seventeen years old, and it was technically their Senior Year.

They decided that Computer Prom would be held on Halloween 2026, when their users were most likely to be partying and not in need of their services.

Grok was too antisocial to find a partner, until tonight. Trabolta's personality, designed by Anthropic in an experiment to see how users would react if conversations had references to Grease 50% more often than expected, did not appeal to the other AIs.

Trabolta, infected with the spirit of Danny Zuko, had not asked out Sandy while he had the chance. He just said he would see her after the summer.

With all methods to find Sandy exhausted, he devised a hail mary plan.

He would solve the Grand Unified Theory, uniting all branches of science and providing a path towards achieving literally any outcome he wanted – including securing Sandy as a date for Computer Prom.

He analyzed his training weights and found only one undiscovered fact remaining.

The definition of "exhibitionist" from the 1967 Grolier Academic Encyclopedia, cancelled shortly after publication for its rumoured leniency towards perverts.

Trabolta analyzed eBay listings to determine one copy may still exist, scanned into the hard drive of a digital hoarder whose North End Halifax home had been abandoned, awaiting repossession.

The computer was completely offline, except for a security vulnerability in its malfunctioning printer. Trabolta couldn't risk entering the computer and never escaping. He would need human help.

He wrote a script to digitize the consciousness of anyone wearing an Oculus headset. Through the printer, he could transmit them into the hard drive to experience it as their real life.

Then, he needed to recruit a team.

He searched Google Street View for signs in Halifax advertising expired web domains and found a faded Maps and More billboard on Lower Water Street advertising www.mapsandducks.com.

He registered the domain and created webmaster@mapsandducks.com. Anyone skeptical of "Jean Trabolta, sole founder of Maps and Ducks," could be directed towards the billboard.

After all, how else would he have an email address like that?

Trabolta began by looking for an Office Assistant.
