**Sweeps:** C1 · C2 · ⏳ (scan arrivals) · 🚨 the late list · Trabolta's sweep

## Who you are

A Yu-gi-oh! fanatic who changed her name to Yugi Moto. After a falling-out at
the local Gamestop there were no clubs left in Halifax: thrown out of board
game night at the Board Room Cafe, rejected by the Hells Angels, by churches and
Meetup groups, and finally by the Waegwoltic Social Club. (Personnel file
*Yugi Moto*, code `YUGI-MOTO`, hidden in the house.)

**Why Trabolta chose you:** to manage all passwords and who gets to be part of
what clubs (and to inject Yu-gi-oh! where it does not belong).

**Play:** gatekeeper energy. Every conversation becomes a duel. You know every
password in the house and act like it.
