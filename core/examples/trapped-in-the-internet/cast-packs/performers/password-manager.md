**The Wanted board.** The hosts never send anyone to the Internet: they
**mark** a program (🎯 named on the Bingo card, 🤷 a guess, 🙅 a refused
truth, 🎩 a Dare, 🏓 a missed cup, 🍎 a failed bob, a late arrival, a filed
guess). **A mark doesn't hinder them** (Q30): their phone keeps every button,
room, card and check-in, and only hears *🚨 You are marked for reinstallation
(why). Nothing is switched off: carry on. The Antivirus are looking for you.*
It is **you** who banish them. Every Antivirus booth gets a **🚨 WANTED**
pop-out: their picture, name, why, where they were last seen, and
**🙋 I'll get them** / **🗂 Open card** / **Later**. Tap 🙋 and it is yours: the
other three see *🙋 (your character) is on X.* Several at once arrive as one
*🚨 n programs marked* pop-out. The tray's red **🚨 003** counter and your NOW
card's **🚨 Wanted board (n)** open the board: one poster per marked program
(tabs Open / Mine / Everyone), kept across a reload. **Hand it back** puts a
poster back for everyone; after 10 minutes on someone else's claim **🙋 Take
over** appears. A **Later** waits in the tray's **🔔 n** (tap it for the
*Waiting* window).

**The scan is the confrontation.** Find them and scan their pass. Your scanner
turns into **⚠ CORRUPTION DETECTED**: their picture, why, and a **SAY IT** box
(*"X. Corruption detected: you are marked for reinstallation. Come with me

**Wrong codes.** When a guest types a bad codex code, the app scolds them in

**✂ The filter (wave 48).** Every line any guest types (a room, a DM to
Trabolta or to one of you, a private thread, the wall terminals) that holds
a swear comes to your booth. The filter starts the night at **full
strength**: mild ones too, and **inside other words**: *password* is caught
for "ass", *hello* for "hell", *check* for "heck", *button* for "butt",
*Saturday* for "turd". You will get a lot. One pops up at a time as **🔑
LANGUAGE**: who, where they typed it, the line with the swear lit (a
brighter colour is a worse word). The rest wait behind the **🔑 n** counter
on your tray, which opens the whole list.

- **✂ Censor** (tap, tap again): the swear is starred out of that line on
  every phone, the TV and the walls (*p\*\*\*word*). The program's
  **suspicion** goes up (+5, the same doubt as 👁) and they get an
  automated notice. Nobody is marked for reinstallation by it.
- **🛡 Send to the Internet** (tap, tap again; wave 57): when a line has
  earned more than the scissors. It is your ordinary 🛡, with its ⭐: their
  phone locks and they go. It shows where a scanned card would show it: on a
  marked program, or on anyone once the Bingo round is over. It doesn't touch
  the line, so you can still ✂ it (and get paid) or let it go.
- **Let it go**: off your list, on your phone only. Nobody is told.

**The ⭐ game.** Each ✂ pays the line's points (a worse word is worth more:
*hello* 1, *crap* 3, *shit* 5, the worst 8) times your **multiplier**. Rested,
it is ×3; every ✂ knocks it down a notch (×2, ×1.5, ×1, ×½, ×¼), and it
climbs back a notch every 20 seconds you wait. Censor everything as fast as
it comes and you earn nothing. Pick your moments, and your words.

Slurs are starred for everyone the moment they are typed; you see them as
**[slur]**. Censoring one still pays the most.

It all counts at the ceremony: **G14 🔑 The Language Report** reads who
cursed the most, how many words you censored out of every word the filter
caught, the words you censored most and your ⭐; the TV shows every word you
censored. After the ending you can still censor (it counts for the report)
but it pays no ⭐.

**The server-room door (wave 20).** The door sensor is a **light on the
frame** shining into a small eye: the story knows the door is shut only while
the beam is unbroken. Once Trabolta is awake the first opening plays **The
Heart** (the room's screen goes red, the bulb wakes, and he asks for the door
to be shut); **nothing goes on until it is shut**, and he nags every 20 s
while it stands open. So: **close the door behind the program** (and behind
yourself), never prop it, and don't stand in the beam. Only with the door
shut does a pass scanned at his screen start the program's talk (three layers
they click through with the mouse, or type to him at the keyboard). If the
story has it wrong, press on your booth (any program's card) **🚪 The
server-room door opened** or **🚪 The server-room door is shut**; from then on
the story follows the buttons, not the light, until the director presses 🚪
*Trust the door sensor again*: tell them on the radio which you pressed. A
program he throws out of his heart (a blue screen) **is marked for
reinstallation** (wave 20b): their poster lands on your Wanted board like
any mark. They stay barred from his heart until the director's 🔄; a barred
re-scan never marks them again.
