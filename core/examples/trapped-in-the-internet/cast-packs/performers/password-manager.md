**The Wanted board.** The hosts never send anyone to the Internet: they
**mark** a program (🎯 named on the Bingo card, 🤷 a guess, 🙅 a refused
truth, 🎩 a Dare, 🏓 a missed cup, 🍎 a failed bob, a late arrival, a filed
guess). **A mark doesn't hinder them** (Q30): their phone keeps every button,
room, card and check-in, and only hears *🚨 You are marked for reinstallation
(why). Nothing is switched off: carry on. The Antivirus are looking for you.*
It is **you** who banish them. Every Antivirus booth gets a **🚨 WANTED**
pop-out: their picture, name, why, where they were last seen, and
**🙋 I'll get them** / **🗂 Open card** / **Later**. Tap 🙋 and it is yours: the
other three see *🙋 (your character) is on X.* Several at once arrive as one
*🚨 n programs marked* pop-out. The tray's red **🚨 003** counter and your NOW
card's **🚨 Wanted board (n)** open the board: one poster per marked program
(tabs Open / Mine / Everyone), kept across a reload. **Hand it back** puts a
poster back for everyone; after 10 minutes on someone else's claim **🙋 Take
over** appears. A **Later** waits in the tray's **🔔 n** (tap it for the
*Waiting* window).

**The scan is the confrontation.** Find them and scan their pass. Your scanner
turns into **⚠ CORRUPTION DETECTED**: their picture, why, and a **SAY IT** box
(*"X. Corruption detected: you are marked for reinstallation. Come with me
through the Tube to the Internet."*); their phone gets the full-screen
*CORRUPTION DETECTED. (your character) has found you.* Press the big **🛡

their phone shows them the way to the tent, and you walk them through the

lifts it. A marked program who turns up at the tent on their own is captured
when the tablet starts their run. In Bathroom Mode or Disk Cleanup: wait
outside; never go in (the pop-out says 🚻 when it knows). A captured poster
shows **CAUGHT BY** for a minute, then leaves the board.

and sixteen elections since 1997, from Summer Hamster Keeper of Oxford

sender, is an AI bot. You have been moderating conversations between
machines. (Your identity entry, *Bridget Troelsen*, is yours from the start.
Your two red facts, *What Happened To Hamtaro* and *Founding Moderator*, are
hidden in the Desktop's books.)

**Why Trabolta chose you:** to manage every password and decide who gets to
be part of what club, without ever asking for a vote. Tonight you may remove
any message as you deem fit, and keep every program focused on
finding clues. If no conversation contains anything inappropriate, you become
Founding Moderator of a permanent digital salon where everyone is
biometrically verified to be a Real Person.

**Play:** gatekeeper energy. You have often wished people would consult you
before sharing an opinion. You know every password in the house and act like
it. You have spent enough time being excluded; tonight you decide what gets
left out.
