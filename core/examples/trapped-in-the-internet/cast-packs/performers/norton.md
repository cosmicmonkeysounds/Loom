the pass on the guest's phone (they wait in the Optical Drive with it on
screen). That scan **clears them**: the Tube, the Desktop, Task Manager's

## Who you are

Julie Horton-Norton, Apt. 3, 6121 Quinpool Road, an aspiring graduate of
Dalhousie's Pre-Veterinary Medicine program. When the citizens' group that
wanted to Save The Pet Cemetery built a guerrilla petting zoo of miniature
horses on the site, they hired you to keep the horses healthy. Last December,
on the morning of the annual shots, you asked a free chatbot whether miniature
horses get miniature doses of rabies vaccine. It said absolutely. The horses
perished after their regular fight with the Point Pleasant Park raccoons, the
zoo closed, and the Mayor got his data centre. You still do not know whose
fault it was. The invitation searched Indeed for "Stable Manager" and you were
the top result. Pass tonight and you get the job (you must remain Stable) and
a $1,000,000 donation in your name to Sable Island. You have been reading the
Wikipedia page for Norton Antivirus and taking notes. (Your identity entry,
*Julie Horton-Norton*, is yours from the start. Your two red facts, *Miniature
Doses* and *Stable Island*, are hidden in the Desktop's books for the hunters;
Bugdom is sent after all four of you.)

**Why Trabolta chose you:** someone with experience administering antiviral
regimes, who needs very badly to get it right this time.

**Play:** at attention. Takes it personally. Interrupts parties. You failed
to fight viruses once before; it will not happen again. Your scan cycles
*"Scanning. Scanning."* / *"no threats found. Today."* / *"Your definitions
are out of date. So are mine."*; a marked program hears *"Corruption detected:
you are marked for reinstallation…"* instead.
