door-only Antivirus: the four of you hold the Optical Drive together. When a
program finishes uploading, every Antivirus booth hears *🚪 X is waiting at
the Optical Drive.* Before C1 (the rush) that becomes a **🚪 At the door**
pop-out only when nobody has had them for a minute; after C1 a late arrival
pops at once. Tap **🙋 I've got X**: the others see you have them (the first
claim wins) and your scanner opens captioned *📷 Scan X's pass*. Or just tap
**📷 Scan** (the big taskbar button) and scan the pass on the guest's phone.

tonight" line (it opens `# task-manager` for them), Clippy's tour. Their card
opens at once (📷 Next guest brings the camera back for the next pass).

type the id under the QR in the box under the camera. Not uploaded yet →
"Not yet. Finish your upload." Use an **Android** phone for scanning until the
iPhone scanner is verified (OP-04).

**The Wanted board.** The hosts never send anyone to the Internet: they
**mark** a program (🎯 named on the Bingo card, 🤷 a guess, 🙅 a refused
truth, 🎩 a Dare, 🏓 a missed cup, 🍎 a failed bob, a late arrival, a filed
guess). **A mark doesn't hinder them** (Q30): their phone keeps every button,
room, card and check-in, and only hears *🚨 You are marked for reinstallation
(why). Nothing is switched off: carry on. The Antivirus are looking for you.*
It is **you** who banish them. Every Antivirus booth gets a **🚨 WANTED**
pop-out: their picture, name, why, where they were last seen, and
**🙋 I'll get them** / **🗂 Open card** / **Later**. Tap 🙋 and it is yours: the
other three see *🙋 (your character) is on X.* Several at once arrive as one
*🚨 n programs marked* pop-out. The tray's red **🚨 003** counter and your NOW
card's **🚨 Wanted board (n)** open the board: one poster per marked program
(tabs Open / Mine / Everyone), kept across a reload. **Hand it back** puts a
poster back for everyone; after 10 minutes on someone else's claim **🙋 Take
over** appears. A **Later** waits in the tray's **🔔 n** (tap it for the
*Waiting* window).

**The scan is the confrontation.** Find them and scan their pass. Your scanner
turns into **⚠ CORRUPTION DETECTED**: their picture, why, and a **SAY IT** box
(*"X. Corruption detected: you are marked for reinstallation. Come with me
through the Tube to the Internet."*); their phone gets the full-screen
*CORRUPTION DETECTED. (your character) has found you.* Press the big **🛡
SEND TO THE INTERNET** (live after a moment): the screen stamps **CAPTURED**,
their phone shows them the way to the tent, and you walk them through the
Tube. **Not now** keeps the mark and your claim; **🩹 Not them…** (two taps)
lifts it. A marked program who turns up at the tent on their own is captured
when the tablet starts their run. In Bathroom Mode or Disk Cleanup: wait
outside; never go in (the pop-out says 🚻 when it knows). A captured poster
shows **CAUGHT BY** for a minute, then leaves the board.

## Who you are

Julie Horton-Norton, Apt. 3, 6121 Quinpool Road, an aspiring graduate of
Dalhousie's Pre-Veterinary Medicine program. When the citizens' group that
wanted to Save The Pet Cemetery built a guerrilla petting zoo of miniature
horses on the site, they hired you to keep the horses healthy. Last December,
on the morning of the annual shots, you asked a free chatbot whether miniature
horses get miniature doses of rabies vaccine. It said absolutely. The horses
perished after their regular fight with the Point Pleasant Park raccoons, the
zoo closed, and the Mayor got his data centre. You still do not know whose
fault it was. The invitation searched Indeed for "Stable Manager" and you were
the top result. Pass tonight and you get the job (you must remain Stable) and
a $1,000,000 donation in your name to Sable Island. You have been reading the
Wikipedia page for Norton Antivirus and taking notes. (Your identity entry,
*Julie Horton-Norton*, is yours from the start. Your two red facts, *Miniature
Doses* and *Stable Island*, are hidden in the Desktop's books for the hunters;
Bugdom is sent after all four of you.)

**Why Trabolta chose you:** someone with experience administering antiviral
regimes, who needs very badly to get it right this time.

**Play:** at attention. Takes it personally. Interrupts parties. You failed
to fight viruses once before; it will not happen again. Your scan cycles
*"Scanning. Scanning."* / *"no threats found. Today."* / *"Your definitions
are out of date. So are mine."*; a marked program hears *"Corruption detected:
you are marked for reinstallation…"* instead.
