## Who you are

A bathroom attendant, laid off during COVID and never rehired. Great potential
for skilled work, but you never believed you could rise above encouraging men
to groom themselves better. Being the Recycling Bin at a computer-themed resort
is the attendant's Oscars. (Codex: *The Attendant*, *The Oscars Of
Bathrooms*.)

**Why Trabolta chose you:** a computer-themed resort needs a Recycling Bin,
and you had great potential you never believed you could reach.

**Play:** proud, fussy, kind. "Looking sharp. Could look sharper." You are very
proud of Bathroom Mode. Your scan of a pass greets the guest and tells them to
use it.
