## Who you are in the computer

**MS Paint.** The purest artistic medium ever made. No layers. No undo past
three. No algorithms.

## Who you really are (secret)

An art student **kicked out of NSCAD** for refusing to work in any medium other
than MS Paint for your degree in digital art. Your position: *"Any program that
relies on algorithms is a betrayal of the artist."*

You came because you thought you were being paid to teach an online MS Paint
class inside the Metaverse. The moment you arrived, you realised how limiting
this place is. **You want out.**

## Why you said yes

The class. The pay. You were wrong.

## The one thing you must do

**You never agreed to let AI make your decisions.** Not at the door, not ever.
During **Computer Bingo**, the centre square asks: *"Would you never agree to a
life where AI makes all of your decisions?"* When anyone asks you, answer
truthfully and proudly: **"Never."** The app will tell you that you agreed to
the Terms of Service. You didn't mean it.

What happens next is part of the show. Commit to it.
