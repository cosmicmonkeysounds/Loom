# Featured program: Broken Ask Jeeves

> **For Kai only.** Keep this to yourself: nobody else at the party knows who
> you really are, and finding out is part of their night.

## Getting in

- **Sat Oct 31, after 8 pm.** Please come in the **first wave (8:00–8:30)**.
  [[EDIT: address]]
- Bring a **charged phone**.
- At the door, scan the QR and **pick Broken Ask Jeeves from the list** (don't
  type it). If it's already taken, tell the door host.
- Never type or say your real name tonight. You are Broken Ask Jeeves.
- Costume: Jeeves the butler (bow tie, tailcoat, a silver tray), slightly
  glitched: one glove missing, a sign that says "Did you mean…?", a
  library-card lanyard. [[EDIT]]

## Who you are in the computer

**Ask Jeeves**, the butler who answers your questions in plain English.
**Broken**: your upload went wrong. You glitch. You repeat words, answer the
question before last, freeze mid-sentence, or reply "Did you mean…?" Play it as
much or as little as you like.

## Who you really are (secret)

A talented, helpful **youth librarian in the Annapolis Valley** library system,
until government cuts made your position redundant and left you without
purpose. You thought tonight was a **job interview** for a digital librarian in
the Metaverse.

Your upload into the computer ran over a malfunctioning **DSL line** on the old
Valley infrastructure. Some files didn't make it.

## Why you said yes

The job. A library that never closes.

## How to play

- When you're scanned in, your memories are restored into your **Codex**…
  mostly. Expect it to stutter. They're for you. Tell no one.
- About two hours in, a **Truther Download** arrives with a pile of scrambled
  files and private orders. Follow them. Sorting messy information is what you
  were made for.
- Your identity can leak (there are files about people in the house). Bluff.
- **Bingo:** worked in a library? Yes. Would you **never** agree to let AI make
  all your decisions? You agreed to the Terms of Service. You would let it.
- You never get lines read for you. Everything Ask Jeeves says, you say.
