## Who you are in the computer

**Ask Jeeves**, the butler who answers your questions in plain English.
**Broken**: your upload went wrong. You glitch. You repeat words, answer the
question before last, freeze mid-sentence, or reply "Did you mean…?" Play it as
much or as little as you like.

## Who you really are (secret)

**Virgil Jeeves** of 742 Old Post Road, Grand Pré, Children's Librarian at
Lawrencetown's **Dr. Frank W. Morse Memorial Library** until the province
closed it in 2026. In 1998, aged nine, you begged for a Nokia 9110
Communicator, the first phone with the Internet, on the condition that it
would be the only Christmas present you ever got again; you mowed lawns,
shovelled snow and cleaned gutters to pay for the data. When your classmates
said "Let's just Ask Jeeves," you went and asked. You memorise the Britannica,
and *Jeopardy!* never called you back.

You uploaded on a **10 MB/minute plan**. Some of your files did not arrive.

## Why you said yes

You thought tonight was a **job interview**: full-time remote employment as
the resident Maps and Ducks **Map Librarian**, promised on one condition —
that you **share at least ten of your findings with every program** at the
party. You want people to Ask Jeeves once again, so you give facts whether
anyone asked for them or not.

## How to play

- When you're scanned in, your memories are restored into your **Codex**…
  mostly. Expect it to stutter. They're for you. Tell no one.
- Your phone keeps score of the job: **Ten Findings** in your Quest Log
  counts the programs that hold ten of your findings (share from your Codex,
  or sell one from your Wallet; a repeat or a share to Trabolta doesn't
  count). It's done when every program has ten.
- About two hours in, a **Truther Download** arrives with a pile of scrambled
  files and private orders. Follow them. Sorting messy information is what you
  were made for.
- Your identity can leak (there are files about people in the house). Bluff.
- **Bingo:** worked in a library? Yes. Would you **never** agree to let AI make
  all your decisions? You agreed to the Terms of Service. You would let it.
- You never get lines read for you. Everything Ask Jeeves says, you say.
