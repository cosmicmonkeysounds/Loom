## Who you are in the computer

**OpenOffice.** Free. Open. Compatible with everything, trusted by no one. You
believe all information should be free, and you can open any file.

## Who you really are (secret)

**Dana Anderson**, a former FBI agent turned independent hacker. Nine years ago
you took part in a sting on a landlord in Halifax, and you have regretted it
ever since. You turned to making information free.

Nobody in Halifax wanted the AI Data Centre. You used old FBI tactics to
sabotage the construction site, with the destruction timed for the early
morning before the official opening. On opening day, **Mayor Randy Barkmore
Jr.** arrived five hours early, at **4 a.m.**, because he was so excited. The
Data Centre was destroyed on schedule. **Nobody knows it was you.** You live
with extreme guilt and paranoia.

## Why you said yes

Jean Trabolta's offer looked like a chance at a new life in the cloud, far from
what you did.

## How to play

- When you're scanned in, the app restores your "user profile": your memories
  appear in your **Codex**. They're for you. Tell no one.
- About two hours in, a **Truther Download** arrives with a question and
  private orders. Answer as Dana would. Follow the orders: they're your
  mission. Facts will matter a great deal to you tonight; collect them.
- Your human identity can leak: there are files about people hidden in the
  house. Deny, bluff or confess, but make them work for it. Someone here may
  have very personal reasons to want to know who was at that site at 4 a.m.
- **Bingo:** worked for a three-letter agency? Yes. Would you **never** agree
  to let AI make all your decisions? You agreed to the Terms of Service. You
  would let it.
- You never get lines read for you. Everything OpenOffice says, you say.
