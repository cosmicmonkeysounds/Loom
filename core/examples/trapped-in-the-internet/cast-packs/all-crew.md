**The wall terminals have ears (wave 19).** A terminal showing a small **🎤**
at its left edge is heard right now by a player in the reinstallation games
(VR or a tablet) standing near that room in the virtual house, and a player
near it is heard out of its speaker. Talk to them, they can talk back. Never
say a secret (the door code, a combo, a hunt answer) by a 🎤 terminal, and
keep the radio away from them. The TV and the tent laptop never send sound.

## The performer basics (every booth)

- **Sign in** after go-live on the morning of 10-31: https://app.mapsandducks.com →
  performer sign-in → the **prime code** [[EDIT: from Run → Join on 10-31]] →
  pick your character from the cast list. Once a part is taken nobody else can
  take it.
- **Alerts:** allow notifications when asked (Android: tap Allow; iPhone: only
  if we called iPhone push on 10-24, then follow the Home Screen steps on the
  🔔 card). Keep the app open on your phone between scenes.
- **Battery:** at **30%**, go to the charging station [[EDIT: where]] or take
  a power bank. **Phone dead or broken → find the director.** They release
  your part and you sign in again on a loaner in under a minute, with
  everything intact (N-10, OP-19).
- **Buttons do nothing out of phase.** That is normal. If a button you need
  "does nothing", radio a host or the director instead of pressing it
  again. Only 🛡 Send to the Internet (on a marked program's card; on anyone's
  after the Bingo round), ✅ Mark
  as reinstalled (on a captured one's), 🩹 Unmark, 🎀, 🤝, 👁, 🪙 Pay /
  Charge and ♟ work at any time; the verdict buttons do nothing out of
  phase. ✅ and 👁 are the Antivirus' own: no host's or Clippy's booth
  has them, so a host radios an Antivirus. 🛡 is the Antivirus' and the
  hosts' (never Clippy's): a host may send a program to the Internet
  too. 🩹 sits on a marked program's card.
- **Marked, not captured (wave 18, Q30: a mark doesn't hinder).** A host's
  verdict (🎯 named on the Bingo card, 🤷 a guess, 🙅 a refused truth, 🎩 a
  Dare, 🏓 a missed cup, 🍎 a failed bob, a late arrival) **marks** a
  program: nothing on their phone switches off (they hear one soft line,
  *Nothing is switched off: carry on. The Antivirus are looking for you.*).
  Every Antivirus booth gets a **🚨 WANTED** pop-out and the program's poster
  on the **Wanted board**; one Antivirus taps **🙋 I'll get them**, finds them
  and **scans their pass: the scan is the confrontation** (CORRUPTION
  DETECTED on both phones), then presses 🛡 and walks them through the Tube.
  The director (Inspector → Captured → Capture) can send them too; so does
  walking into the tent: the moment the tent laptop scans their pass and starts
  **Play here** (or **Send to VR**), a marked program is captured. A mis-tap
  → 🩹 Unmark.
- **A host's booth** (Task Manager, Adobe Acrobat, the Recycling Bin; the
  players' Mission is the next bullet): the **NOW card** pinned on top says what the story
  wants from your character right now, with a checklist that ticks itself;
  the taskbar at the bottom has **⊞ Start** (help, keep the screen on, alerts,
  what your character knows, sign out), **📷 Scan** (the big one) and
  **🗂 Guests**; the tray shows the act, the soonest countdown and **🔔 n**
  (pop-outs waiting: anything you put off with *Later*, kept across a
  reload). Since wave 18 the important things **pop out** over your rooms (a
  marked program, an arrival at the door, the Bingo results)
  with the buttons you need, so you scan much less. Your rooms are headed
  **Chats**; the room named after the story is the **📜 Chat log**, last
  under *Other rooms*.
- **The players (wave 21).** **Clippy and the four Antivirus are
  players**: their booth is a **Mission** like a guest's phone (taskbar
  **⊞ Start · Mission · Chats · 📷 Scan**, ⭐ Points and their stat tiles, a
  *NEW MISSION* pop-out when the job changes, **📋 Notes** for their
  readouts), they only see their own buttons and their own team room, and
  they play for ⭐ and for Trabolta's trust (never shown to them; his Trust
  Chart shows them as 🛡 STAFF rows). From the Truther Download each may
  **📢 defect openly** (the whole house hears it; Trabolta keeps them as
  allies anyway: he cannot run the house without them) or do **🤫 quiet
  favours** for the programs (nobody is told; every third one costs them
  trust; **The Mole** at the ceremony names the most). Three **staff
  awards** close the ceremony before G11.
- **The cue sheet is the hosts'.** The three
  host booths (Task Manager, Adobe Acrobat, the Recycling Bin) carry the
  cues in a **NEXT bar** above the taskbar, and **any host may call the
  show** (Q1): whoever is calling taps **📣 I'll call**, and every booth and
  the director phone show **📣 who** is calling. Tap ARM, then FIRE; a FIRE
  by someone who isn't calling asks once (*Fire anyway?*). A double press is
  refused (*Cues moved — tap again*), and the console's LAST line says who
  pressed. **Clippy** is a player (wave 21, Q2 reversed) and calls nothing:
  the hosts and the directors fire Clippy's games, and Clippy's phone pops
  *YOUR GAME IS ON*. No other performer's booth has cues.
- **Act on a guest:** tap **📷 Scan** and scan their pass (or tap their name in
  any room, or find them under 🗂 Guests): their card opens at once with the
  facts (not cleared, marked, late, Bathroom Mode, where), **at most three
  buttons your objective needs** (wave 21: no *All buttons…* fold; a player's
  refused press says *Not now*). What the
  press came to shows on the card. **📷 Next guest** brings the camera back.
- **Codex QRs:** **📷 Scan** reads them too, secret ones included, and the entry
  lands in your 📓 Codex. A wrong code costs you nothing. No ⭐ for it. **Share
  lore…** on a guest's card can pass on anything you hold, so mind what you hand
  over.
- **Booth readouts** are the 📋/🛡/🃏/🚨 lines for you only. They land in the
  pinned **Readouts** room, newest first, and survive a reload; a readout
  about the guest whose card is open shows on the card. The ones that need
  you now (a marked program, an arrival, the confrontation) are pop-outs. A program typing to
  your character shows under **Messages** until you read it. Your booth also
  has a **Quest Log** (your staff quests by their titles, "Per the agenda: …"
  or "Patrol: …", from Task Manager) and a **Codex** (what your character
  knows). CRA Agent Software's booth has a **Wallet** room on top of those.
- **Alerts in a booth** never cover the screen: an alert to everyone is a
  balloon tip by the tray, one to your character or a staff room a yellow
  banner until you tap it. A **pop-out** (a big moment for your booth) docks
  over your rooms until you press one of its buttons or **Later**; while a
  card is open it shrinks to a strip at the top and never covers the card's
  buttons.
- **`# hosts` and `# quarantine` are staff rooms.** The hosts see both; a
  player sees only their own team's (the Antivirus `# quarantine`, Clippy
  neither). No guest can. `# hosts` carries the bank's and the judge's logs; `#
  quarantine` is the Antivirus' back room (no escort pins since wave 18: the
  Wanted board lists the marked). Never put a faction's secret there.
- **Never say a guest's real name** aloud or in the app. Everyone is their program.
- **🆘 HELP ME in `# hosts`** (wave 44, pinned and pushed): a program being
  reinstalled pressed Help Me on their phone or the tent's screen. The
  nearest free host goes out to the backyard now and says so on the radio.
  They can ask again after a minute.
- **The video list.** Guests can opt out of the video at the upload or from
  ⊞ Start. Don't film anyone who asks you not to; the director downloads
  the list from Run at the end.

**📜 READ THIS OUT LOUD (wave 55).** If your part has a scripted line, your

says there ends the night.

## The blackout rule (Combo A or Combo B)

When a combo lands, **every light goes out for ten seconds**, every phone says
**GO TO THE LIVING ROOM**, and the dance song starts.

- The stairs, the bathroom, the tent, the server room and the path to the
  living room stay lit. Battery path lights and glow tape are on every step.
- **Every performer guides the nearest guests to the living room.** Walk,
  never run. Headlamps on if you have one.
- The house manager leads; the tech operator stays at John's MacBook.
- If anything goes wrong, call **ALL STOP**.

## If the house internet dies

The app runs on a server outside the house, so **phones keep working on
cellular**. Tell guests near you: "Turn off Wi-Fi and keep playing."
Lights and sound stop following the story: the tech operator fires them by
hand. Wall terminals and the TV may freeze; that is fine.

## If Trabolta stops answering

His chat will say *"Jean Trabolta is not responding. Please do not move the
mouse."* The tech operator restarts his worker; until then the directors answer
his messages by hand. Nothing for performers to do except stay in character:
"He is processing a high volume of requests." (Clippy may announce it.)
