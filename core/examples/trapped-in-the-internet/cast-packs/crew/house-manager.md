INTERNET card is **marked for reinstallation**: nothing is broken. Find them
an Antivirus (or radio one); they walk them through the Tube.

**Loaners + re-pairs.** Loaner phones are at [[EDIT: where]]. A guest who lost
their session or their phone: walk them to the director, who shows a QR that
puts them back exactly where they were (never sign up again). Same for a
performer whose phone died (the director releases the part).

**Charging station:** [[EDIT: where]]. Keep it tidy; label what's charging.

**The server-room keypad lock.** It locks from the outside only; the inside
handle always opens with no code. You carry its **override key** and its
spare batteries all night. A dead battery or a jam: open it with the key,
prop the door, and radio the director (who fires 🚪 if the cutscene hasn't
played). Never say the code on the radio.

**Blackout (Combo A or B) and ALL STOP: you lead.**

1. Headlamp on. Lights out for 10 s, then the dance.
2. Stand where people pile up (the stairs, the server-room path). **The stairs
   stay lit.** Guide everyone to the living room. Walk.
3. ALL STOP: the director restores the lights (💡, which also holds a
   pending dance: the dance look waits for your go, then the director
   presses 🪩), the tech op panics the audio and hits the wall switches; you
   get people calm and seated, or outside to the meeting point. Nothing
   restarts until you say so.

**911 + neighbours.** You make the 911 call (the emergency card). You are the
neighbour liaison: if a neighbour comes to the door, be kind, turn the
backyard down, and let John know.

**Radio:** ARRIVAL (you call it) · HOLD · ALL STOP.
